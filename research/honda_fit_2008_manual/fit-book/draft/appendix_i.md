# Appendix I: Recall and TSB list

This appendix is the complete, standalone reference for every NHTSA safety recall and Technical Service Bulletin (TSB) confirmed for the 2007-2008 Honda Fit. A **recall** is a free, manufacturer-run repair program ordered after a safety-related defect is found in a group of vehicles. A **TSB** is a repair instruction a manufacturer sends to its own dealers about a known problem; it is not a safety recall and is not always a free repair. Always check your own VIN at a recall lookup tool (see Appendix H), since coverage can depend on where a car was sold or registered, and on whether a part was already replaced under an earlier campaign.

## Recall details

### 07V549000 — Frontal airbag ODS/OPDS wire harness corrosion

<!-- F-WP02-027 -->
- **Recall began:** December 12, 2007
- **Applies to:** Certain 2007-2008 Fit vehicles originally sold, or currently registered, in 21 listed salt-belt states plus Washington, DC: Connecticut, Delaware, Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kentucky, Maine, Maryland, Massachusetts, Michigan, Minnesota, Missouri, New Hampshire, New Jersey, New York, Ohio, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island, Vermont, Virginia, West Virginia, and Wisconsin.
- **Component:** Frontal airbags — Occupant Detection System (ODS) / Occupant Position Detection System (OPDS) wire harness.
- **Defect:** The wire harness for the front passenger ODS/OPDS is routed underneath the carpet on the driver's side floorboard. In salt-belt areas, road salt tracked in on a driver's shoes can melt and seep through the carpet, reaching the harness. If the harness corrodes and a wire breaks, the SRS indicator light comes on, and the ODS/OPDS may fail to detect a child seat or an out-of-position passenger, so the passenger's frontal and side airbags will not be suppressed when they should be.
- **Consequence:** In a crash, a deploying front passenger airbag or side airbag increases the risk of injury to small or out-of-position occupants.
- **Remedy:** Dealers inspect and, if necessary, replace the wire harness, free of charge. Contact Honda at 1-800-999-1009.

### 10V033000 — Power window master switch water intrusion (1st campaign)

<!-- F-WP02-001 -->
- **Recall began:** March 19, 2010 (Honda recall no. R28)
- **Applies to:** Certain model year 2007-2008 Fit, approximately 141,140 units sold in the US. This is an all-trims recall, not base-only.
- **Component:** Power window master switch, driver's door.
- **Defect:** Under severe conditions, rain water or spilled liquid entering through an open driver's window can reach the power window master switch and damage its printed circuit board. A damaged circuit board can short and overheat the switch connectors, which can melt and, in rare cases, produce smoke or fire.
- **Remedy:** Dealer inspects the switch and installs a cover around the switch housing to prevent water entry; if the switch is already damaged, it is replaced and a cover installed — free of charge. Contact Honda at 1-800-999-1009.

### 10V624000 — Headlight low-beam wiring harness / combination switch

<!-- F-WP02-003 -->
- **Recall began:** January 24, 2011
- **Applies to:** 143,083 model year 2007-2008 Fit vehicles in the US. All-trims recall.
- **Component:** Exterior lighting — headlights, high/low beam dimmer (combination) switch and its wiring harness.
- **Defect:** An installation error in the wiring harness for the combination switch caused the low-beam circuit wires to be pulled tighter than intended. Repeated use of the headlight switch can wear the electrical connector terminal for the low-beam circuit, lowering conductivity and letting copper oxide build up at the contacts.
- **Consequence:** Low-beam headlights can become inoperative, reducing the driver's visibility and the vehicle's visibility to other traffic, increasing crash risk.
- **Remedy:** Dealer inspects the low-beam terminal and performs the necessary repair, free of charge. Contact Honda at 1-800-999-1009.

### 13V260000 — Power window master switch water intrusion (re-recall)

<!-- F-WP02-002 -->
- **Recall began:** Approximately June 24, 2013; Honda's own recall notice states that by October 18, 2013, replacement parts were in stock in sufficient quantity to complete repairs on all affected units.
- **Applies to:** Certain model year 2007-2008 Fit. All-trims recall.
- **Component:** Power window master switch, driver's door — the same switch as 10V033000.
- **Defect:** Honda determined the 2010 remedy was not sufficient to prevent water intrusion into the switch. Under severe conditions, water can still reach the switch and impair its function, and a damaged switch can overheat and cause smoke, melting, or fire.
- **Remedy:** Dealer replaces the power window master switch, free of charge, using an updated part.

### 16V344000 — Passenger frontal airbag inflator, Takata, original-equipment and replacement, Zone A/B/C

<!-- F-WP02-028 -->
- **Recall began:** June 27, 2016 (Honda numbers KA0, KA1, KA2, KA3)
- **Applies to:** Certain 2007-2008 Fit vehicles, among many other Honda/Acura models, in a geographic Zone A/B/C recall — the 2007-2008 Fit is named in every zone, though exact notification timing is zone-dependent. This campaign covers **both** original-equipment-installed and replacement passenger frontal airbag inflators.
- **Component:** Airbags, frontal, passenger side, inflator module.
- **Defect:** Passenger frontal airbag inflators, whether original equipment or replacement, may rupture due to propellant degradation after long-term exposure to absolute humidity and temperature cycling.
- **Consequence:** An inflator rupture may send metal fragments toward vehicle occupants, causing serious injury or death.
- **Remedy:** Dealers replace the passenger frontal airbag inflator, free of charge. Contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138.

### 17V029000 — Passenger frontal airbag inflator, Takata, later zone expansion

<!-- F-WP02-029 -->
- **Recall began:** March 2017 (Honda numbers KD9, KE0)
- **Applies to:** Certain 2007-2008 Fit vehicles, among other Honda/Acura models, in a later geographic Zone A/B/C expansion of the same defect family as 16V344000.
- **Component:** Airbags, frontal, passenger side, inflator module.
- **Defect:** Same as 16V344000 — passenger frontal airbag inflators, original equipment or replacement, may rupture due to propellant degradation from long-term exposure to absolute humidity and temperature cycling.
- **Consequence:** An inflator rupture may send metal fragments toward vehicle occupants, causing serious injury or death.
- **Remedy:** Dealers replace the front passenger airbag inflator, free of charge. Contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138.

### 19V501000 — Passenger frontal airbag inflator, Takata, remedy-part-only

<!-- F-WP02-004 -->
- **Recall began:** On or about August 12, 2019 (Honda internal numbers E5D and X5C)
- **Applies to:** Certain model year 2007-2008 Fit vehicles specifically equipped with a passenger frontal airbag inflator that was itself installed as a recall remedy or replacement service part — in other words, a Fit that already had its original passenger airbag inflator swapped under an earlier Takata campaign.
- **Component:** Passenger frontal airbag inflator.
- **Defect:** These replacement/remedy inflators may explode due to propellant (phase-stabilized ammonium nitrate) degradation from long-term exposure to high absolute humidity, high temperature, and temperature cycling — the same underlying defect mechanism as the original mass recall, but affecting the second-round replacement parts.
- **Consequence:** An inflator explosion can send sharp metal fragments toward the driver or other occupants, risking serious injury or death.
- **Remedy:** Dealer replaces the passenger frontal airbag inflator again, free of charge. Contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138.

### 20V770000 — Drive shaft corrosion, manual transmission

<!-- F-WP02-005 -->
- **Recall began:** American Honda notified NHTSA on or about December 10, 2020.
- **Applies to:** 2007-2008 Fit vehicles with a **manual transmission** (nationwide), plus 2009-2013 Fit vehicles with a manual transmission in specific salt-belt states or with a salt-belt registration history. Automatic-transmission 2008 Fits are not affected by this recall.
- **Component:** Powertrain — driveline — drive shaft.
- **Defect:** The protective coating on certain drive shafts may not have been applied properly during manufacturing, making the shaft more susceptible to corrosion from road salt or other contaminants; a sufficiently corroded shaft can break.
- **Consequence:** A broken drive shaft can cause a sudden loss of drive power.
- **Remedy:** Dealer inspects the drive shaft(s) and replaces them if necessary, free of charge.

## Technical Service Bulletins (TSBs)

<!-- F-WP02-008, F-WP02-009, F-WP02-010 -->
Two TSBs are confirmed applicable to the 2007-2008 Fit:

| Bulletin number | Date | Title | Component |
|---|---|---|---|
| 14-058 | Approx. September 23, 2014 | Warranty Extension: Electric Power Steering With DTC 32-09 Or 61-04 | Electric power steering (EPS) system |
| 13-021 | Version 3, September 16, 2015 | Rocker arm oil pressure switch causing Malfunction Indicator Lamp with DTC P2646/P2647/P2651/P2652 | Rocker arm oil pressure switch |

**14-058 — Electric power steering warranty extension.** Explicitly names the 2006-2009 Honda Civic and 2007-2008 Fit. American Honda extended the EPS warranty to 10 years from the original purchase date or 150,000 miles, whichever comes first, for vehicles with a stored diagnostic trouble code 32-09 (EPS current sensor) and/or 61-04 (open or short in the EPS motor harness). The bulletin describes the symptom as steering that feels heavier than normal or is hard to turn, particularly when the vehicle is not moving.

**13-021 — Rocker arm oil pressure switch.** The bulletin's "AFFECTED VEHICLES" table lists "2007-11 Fit — ALL — ALL" as one of several rows in a multi-model bulletin. It addresses an intermittently failing rocker arm oil pressure switch that causes the Malfunction Indicator Lamp to illuminate with DTC P2646/P2651 (circuit low voltage) or P2647/P2652 (circuit high voltage); the bulletin notes the symptom may be intermittent and may not be reproducible after clearing the code. The corrective action is to replace the rocker arm oil pressure switch (Honda part number 37250-PNE-G01) and its O-ring (part number 91319-PAA-A01).

**Total TSB count.** We could not confirm this for the 2008 Fit. Check your owner's manual, or ask a Honda dealer, for a full list of every bulletin issued for your specific car. Different aggregator sites reported conflicting totals (19 versus approximately 42), likely because they count bulletins differently, and neither count could be independently confirmed.

## How to check for new recalls and TSBs yourself

Use the NHTSA vehicle lookup tool listed in Appendix H to search by your VIN or by year/make/model. This is the fastest way to see whether a new recall or TSB has been issued since this book was written, and it also shows owner-submitted complaints and formal government investigations, which can reveal problems before they turn into an official recall.
