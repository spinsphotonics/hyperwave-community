# Chapter 6: Starting and stopping the car

## What you will learn

- The four positions of the ignition switch, the slot where you turn the key to start the car
- How to start a manual transmission car, step by step
- How to start an automatic transmission car, step by step
- What the immobilizer light means and what to do if it blinks
- How to release the shift lock on an automatic if the shifter will not move out of Park

## The short version

Before you turn the key, you set the parking brake and check that the shifter is in the right spot. <!-- F-WP04-001 --> On a manual, you press the clutch pedal all the way down first. <!-- F-WP04-002 --> On an automatic, you press the brake pedal and confirm the shift lever is in Park. <!-- F-WP04-003 --> Once the engine starts, ease off the accelerator, the gas pedal, so the engine does not race. <!-- F-WP04-004 -->

## The full explanation

### The ignition switch

Your Fit's ignition switch has four positions. <!-- F-WP04-006 --> LOCK is where you insert or remove the key; on an automatic, the shift lever must also be in Park to turn the key to LOCK. <!-- F-WP04-006 --> ACCESSORY runs the audio system and the accessory power socket. <!-- F-WP04-006 --> ON is the normal driving position, and switching from ACCESSORY to ON lights up several dashboard indicators briefly as a test. <!-- F-WP04-006 --> START is spring-loaded and used only to start the engine; it springs back to ON when you let go. <!-- F-WP04-006 -->

A reminder beeper sounds if you open the driver's door while the key is left in LOCK or ACCESSORY, and removing the key stops it. <!-- F-WP04-007 --> On an automatic, the shift lever must be in Park before you can remove the key. <!-- F-WP04-007 --> Never remove the key while driving. Doing so locks the steering wheel and can cause you to lose control of the car. <!-- F-WP04-007 -->

Before you start driving, the owner's manual's own pre-drive checklist tells you to check the gauges and indicators on the instrument panel, the panel of dials and lights in front of you, along with your mirrors, seat position, seat belts, and closed doors. <!-- F-WP04-008 -->

### Starting a manual transmission Fit

1. Apply the parking brake. <!-- F-WP04-001 -->
2. In cold weather, turn off electrical accessories to reduce battery drain. <!-- F-WP04-001 -->
3. Press the clutch pedal all the way to the floor. The START position will not work unless the clutch pedal is fully pressed. <!-- F-WP04-002 -->
4. Without touching the accelerator, turn the key to START. <!-- F-WP04-001 -->
5. Do not hold START for more than 15 seconds at a time. If the engine does not start, wait at least 10 seconds before you try again. <!-- F-WP04-001 -->
6. Once the engine starts, ease off the accelerator so it does not race. <!-- F-WP04-004 -->

If the engine still does not start after 15 seconds, or it starts and stalls right away, repeat the START step with the accelerator pressed about halfway down. <!-- F-WP04-004 --> If it still will not start, the engine may be flooded, meaning too much fuel has reached it. Press the accelerator all the way down and hold it there while starting; if that does not work, go back to the halfway-down step. <!-- F-WP04-005 --> Cold weather and altitudes above 8,000 feet (2,400 m) make starting harder. <!-- F-WP04-005 -->

### Starting an automatic transmission Fit

1. Apply the parking brake. <!-- F-WP04-001 -->
2. In cold weather, turn off electrical accessories to reduce battery drain. <!-- F-WP04-001 -->
3. Make sure the shift lever is in Park, and press the brake pedal. <!-- F-WP04-003 -->
4. Without touching the accelerator, turn the key to START. <!-- F-WP04-001 -->
5. Do not hold START for more than 15 seconds at a time. If the engine does not start, wait at least 10 seconds before you try again. <!-- F-WP04-001 -->
6. Once the engine starts, ease off the accelerator so it does not race. <!-- F-WP04-004 -->

The flooding and hard-starting notes above apply the same way on an automatic. <!-- F-WP04-004, F-WP04-005 -->

### The automatic transmission's shift positions

Your base automatic Fit has these shift lever positions: P (Park), R (Reverse), N (Neutral), D (Drive, which uses gears one through five on its own), D3 (like D, but it locks out 4th and 5th gear, useful for engine braking downhill or in stop-and-go traffic), 2 (locks 2nd gear, for climbing power, engine braking, or slippery starts), and 1 (locks 1st gear). <!-- F-WP04-039 --> An "S" position and paddle shifters exist only on the Sport trim, not on your base car. <!-- F-WP04-039 -->

### Releasing the shift lock

Normally, you move the automatic shift lever out of Park by pressing the brake pedal and pressing the lever's release button. <!-- F-WP04-038 --> If that does not work, your Fit has a shift lock release slot, a hidden covered slot in the shift lever console, next to the lever's release button. <!-- F-WP04-038 --> To use it: set the parking brake, remove the key from the ignition, protect the notch of the slot's cover with a cloth, and carefully pry the cover off with a small flat-tip screwdriver or a metal fingernail file. <!-- F-WP04-038 --> Insert the key into the slot, push down on the key while you press the shift lever's release button, and move the lever out of Park to Neutral. <!-- F-WP04-038 --> Remove the key from the slot, reinstall the cover with its notch on the left side, press the brake pedal, and restart the engine. <!-- F-WP04-038 --> Needing this procedure means your car is developing a problem, so have it checked by a dealer. <!-- F-WP04-038 -->

### The manual transmission's shift pattern

Your 5-speed manual is synchronized in all forward gears, meaning the gears mesh smoothly without extra effort from you. <!-- F-WP04-040 --> It has a mechanical lockout that prevents shifting directly from 5th gear into reverse. <!-- F-WP04-040 --> Always press the clutch pedal all the way down when you shift, and do not rest your foot on the clutch pedal when you are not shifting, since that wears the clutch faster. <!-- F-WP04-040 --> Come to a complete stop before you shift into reverse; shifting into reverse while moving can damage the transmission. <!-- F-WP04-040 --> Pause briefly with the clutch down, or pass through a forward gear for a moment, before you engage reverse, to stop the gears from grinding. <!-- F-WP04-040 -->

### The immobilizer light

The key symbol on your dashboard is the immobilizer system indicator. <!-- F-WP04-029 --> At key-ON, it should light briefly, then go off, confirming the car recognized your key's chip. <!-- F-WP04-029 --> If it blinks instead, the system did not recognize your key. Turn the key to LOCK, remove it, reinsert it, and turn to ON again. <!-- F-WP04-029 --> Another key or metal object near the ignition switch can also cause this, so keep spare keys and fobs away from the switch while starting. <!-- F-WP04-029 --> If it keeps blinking, call the dealer. <!-- F-WP04-029 --> The light also blinks a few times when you turn the key from ON down to ACCESSORY or LOCK; that is normal, not a fault. <!-- F-WP04-029 --> Never alter the immobilizer system or add devices to it, and always take your key with you when you leave the car. <!-- F-WP04-029 -->
