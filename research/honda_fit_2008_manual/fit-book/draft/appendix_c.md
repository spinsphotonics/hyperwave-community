# Appendix C: Torque specifications

**Torque** is a measure of twisting force — how hard you turn a bolt or nut with a wrench. A **torque wrench** is a tool that stops you (or clicks, or shows a reading) once you have reached a set amount of twisting force, so you do not over-tighten or under-tighten a fastener. Over-tightening can strip threads or crack parts; under-tightening can let a part loosen and fail while you drive. This appendix gives every torque number this project's research turned up for the 2008 Fit base trim, and it says so plainly whenever a number could not be confirmed. The service manual that mechanics normally use for this exact number (the Honda factory service manual) was not available as a free source during this research, so most values below come from the owner's manual (which prints a few) or from cross-checked online forum and repair-site sources (which print the rest, with lower confidence). The **confidence tier** column tells you how much to trust each number: Tier 1 means it came from Honda's own document; Tier 3 means a published repair reference or parts site; Tier 4 means an online forum or Q&A post. A "community-reported" value has not been checked against an official document — treat it as a helpful starting point, not a guarantee, and check it against your own shop's torque chart or the fastener's own markings before you rely on it.
