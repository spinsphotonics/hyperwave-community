# Chapter 7: The dashboard explained

## What you will learn

- What every warning light on your base 2008 Fit's dashboard means
- What to do for each light, and whether it is safe to keep driving
- Which lights described in the owner's manual you will never see on your base car
- What each gauge and readout on your instrument panel, the panel of dials and lights in front of you, shows you

## The short version

Your instrument panel has 20 warning lights and messages that apply to your base Fit, and this chapter lists every one. <!-- F-WP04-009 --> Some lights only give you information, some tell you to schedule a repair soon, and a few tell you to pull over right away. <!-- F-WP04-010, F-WP04-011 --> Learn this table before your first drive, because it is the page you will come back to most.

## The full explanation

### How many lights does your Fit have

The owner's manual describes 26 named indicators across US and Canadian Fit models. <!-- F-WP04-009 --> Six of those do not apply to a US base Fit, leaving 20 that you may actually see. <!-- F-WP04-009 --> The immobilizer system indicator, the key-shaped light that confirms your key's anti-theft chip was recognized, is covered in Chapter 6, since it comes up during starting.

### The lights you will never see

Your base Fit does not have these six indicators, because they belong to other trims or other countries: the fog light indicator (Sport only), the security system indicator (Sport only, since base has no remote entry system), the cruise main and cruise control indicators (Sport and Canadian LX only, since base has no cruise control), the daytime running lights indicator (Canadian models only), and the washer level indicator (Canadian models only). <!-- F-WP04-032 --> If your base car ever shows one of these six, something aftermarket, meaning added after the factory build, is probably involved. <!-- F-WP04-032 -->

### The gauges

Your instrument panel shows a tachometer, a gauge that measures how fast your engine is spinning; a speedometer; a fuel gauge; an odometer, which shows total distance driven; a trip meter with A and B settings you can reset separately; a select/reset knob; and an information display for text messages like the Maintenance Minder and "CHECK FUEL CAP." <!-- F-WP04-033 --> Your Fit has no separate needle gauge for coolant temperature. Instead, the High and Low Temperature indicator lights tell you about coolant temperature. <!-- F-WP04-033 -->

Press the select/reset knob to switch between Trip A and Trip B, and press and hold it until the display reads "0.0" to reset one. <!-- F-WP04-034 --> The odometer shows total miles driven; tampering with it to change the reading is illegal under US federal law. <!-- F-WP04-034 -->

The fuel gauge shows approximately how much fuel is in the tank, and it may read slightly more or less than the actual amount. <!-- F-WP04-035 --> The needle drops to empty after you turn off the ignition and comes back up once the engine runs again. <!-- F-WP04-035 --> Do not drive with an extremely low fuel level. Running out of fuel can make the engine misfire and can damage the catalytic converter, a part of the exhaust system that reduces pollution. <!-- F-WP04-035 -->

The tachometer has a red zone, the danger zone the engine speed limiter protects against on upshifts and the reference point for safe engine braking on downshifts. <!-- F-WP04-036 --> The 2008 Fit's factory redline, the highest safe engine speed, is 6,500 rpm. <!-- F-WP04-036 --> We could not confirm the normal idle RPM for the 2008 Fit. Check your owner's manual. <!-- F-WP04-036 -->

Your speedometer is a standard analog needle gauge, and US models read in mph. <!-- F-WP04-037 -->
