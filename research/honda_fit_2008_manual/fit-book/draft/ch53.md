# Chapter 53: Engine Management

## What you will learn
- What an ECU is, and what "tuning" or "reflashing" it actually means.
- The single most surprising fact in this whole book about aftermarket support: which Fit generation Hondata actually supports.
- What Hondata sells for your exact car, and what it costs.
- What does NOT exist for your car, so you do not waste money chasing it.
- Why a header dyno number "for a Honda Fit" you find online might not even be for your car.

## The short version
A company called Hondata sells a full engine-tuning system built specifically for your 2007-2008 Fit, right now, today <!-- F-WP17-013 -->. Hondata has never sold anything like it for the newer 2009-2014 Fit, which is a different car with a different engine <!-- F-WP17-017 -->. If you assumed the newer Fit has better tuning support than yours, that assumption is backwards <!-- F-WP17-022 -->.

## The full explanation

### Words you need first
An ECU, short for Engine Control Unit, is the small computer that runs your engine <!-- background -->. It decides things like fuel amount and spark timing <!-- background -->. "Tuning" an ECU means changing those settings, usually to make more power or run differently <!-- background -->. A "reflash" rewrites the software inside the factory ECU itself, through the car's OBD-II port <!-- background -->. OBD-II is the standard data port under your dashboard, used on all cars built after 1996 <!-- background -->. A reflash tool does not need to physically cut any wires <!-- background -->. A "piggyback" is a separate small box that sits between the ECU and its sensors <!-- background -->. It changes some signals without touching the factory ECU's own software <!-- background -->. A "standalone" system fully replaces the factory ECU with a new one <!-- background -->. CARB is the California Air Resources Board, the state agency that sets emissions rules <!-- background -->. A CARB EO, or Executive Order, is a specific approval number from that agency <!-- background -->. It lets an aftermarket part be sold as legal for street use in every state, not only outside California <!-- background -->.

### The headline finding
Here is the single most important finding in this chapter, so read it twice <!-- F-WP17-013 -->. Hondata's FlashPro system is a real, full-reflash tuning product <!-- F-WP17-013 -->. Hondata's own product page lists it for "Vehicle: Fit, Year: 2007-2008, Market: US/Canada" <!-- F-WP17-013 -->. That is your exact car <!-- F-WP17-013 -->. This is not a guess or an inference <!-- F-WP17-013 -->. It is a direct statement from the manufacturer's own current catalog <!-- F-WP17-013 -->.

Now for the surprising part <!-- F-WP17-017 -->. A direct check of Hondata's own product catalog, filtered specifically to the Fit, shows only two products <!-- F-WP17-017 -->. Both of them are for the 2007-2008 car, your car <!-- F-WP17-017 -->. Zero products exist in that same catalog for the 2009-2014 Fit <!-- F-WP17-017 -->. Hondata's own customer forum has a thread from a 2009-2014 Fit owner asking "Still nothing for the L15A7?" <!-- F-WP17-017 -->. The L15A7 is the engine in the 2009-2014 Fit <!-- F-WP17-017 -->. A reader might expect the newer car to have more aftermarket support, since it is newer <!-- F-WP17-022 -->. For Hondata specifically, the exact opposite is true <!-- F-WP17-022 -->. Never assume the newer Fit has better tuning support without checking for yourself <!-- F-WP17-022 -->.

### What Hondata sells for your car
Hondata sells two different FlashPro versions for the 2007-2008 Fit <!-- F-WP17-013 -->. The full version, product code FP-FIT-US, costs $795 <!-- F-WP17-013 -->. It unlocks complete custom tuning and full datalogging <!-- F-WP17-013 -->. Datalogging means the system records engine data over time, so a tuner can study it later <!-- F-WP17-013 -->. It comes with 20 hours of on-board datalogging memory <!-- F-WP17-013 -->. The second version, product code FP-FIT-US-CARB, costs $595 <!-- F-WP17-014 -->. It offers datalogging plus a limited menu of preset tuning options, not fully open custom tuning <!-- F-WP17-014 -->. Hondata states this CARB version carries CARB EO number D-742-1, which keeps it legal for street use in every state, including California <!-- F-WP17-014 -->.

Here is the real tradeoff <!-- F-WP17-014 -->. The $595 CARB version keeps you street-legal everywhere, but limits you to Hondata's own preset calibrations <!-- F-WP17-014 -->. The $795 full version unlocks real custom tuning instead <!-- F-WP17-014 -->. That kind of tuning is what you need to get the most out of a header or other bolt-ons <!-- F-WP17-014 -->. Hondata does not represent the full version as CARB-legal for street use, though <!-- F-WP17-014 -->. Choose based on whether legality or full tuning matters more to you <!-- F-WP17-014 -->.

Both versions connect through your car's OBD-II port <!-- F-WP17-013 -->. Neither one requires cutting into your factory ECU <!-- F-WP17-013 -->. Hondata's own specification page lists the exact factory ECU part numbers this system supports <!-- F-WP17-015 -->. That range is RME-A11 through RME-A89 for US cars, and RME-C11 through RME-C89 for Canadian cars <!-- F-WP17-015 -->. An independent retailer's own product page reproduces that same exact part-number range, word for word <!-- F-WP17-015 -->. That second, separate confirmation makes this an unusually solid fact <!-- F-WP17-015 -->.

### What does NOT exist for your car
Hondata also makes older products called K-Pro and s300 <!-- F-WP17-018 -->. Neither one applies to any Honda Fit, in either generation <!-- F-WP17-018 -->. K-Pro is built only for Honda's K-series engines, used in cars like the Civic Si and Acura RSX <!-- F-WP17-018 -->. Your Fit does not have a K-series engine <!-- F-WP17-018 -->. The s300 line is built for much older, 1992-1999 OBD1-era Hondas <!-- F-WP17-018 -->. Your Fit is a newer OBD2 car <!-- F-WP17-019 -->. Very old-school Honda ECU-chipping tools, such as Uberdata, Neptune, or Crome, are also built only for those same 1992-1995 OBD1 Hondas <!-- F-WP17-019 -->. None of them can work on your car's ECU <!-- F-WP17-019 -->.

A plug-and-play standalone system, like an AEM Infinity harness, is a different kind of product than FlashPro <!-- F-WP17-021 -->. AEM sells this kind of harness for some older Honda engines <!-- F-WP17-021 -->. No dedicated, named harness for the Fit or the L15A1 was found in this research <!-- F-WP17-021 -->. That does not prove one does not exist somewhere <!-- F-WP17-021 -->. It only means none was found <!-- F-WP17-021 -->. This is recorded as unverified, not as a confirmed "no" <!-- F-WP17-021 -->.

### A real piggyback option, with real limits
At least one owner has used a piggyback controller, an Apexi VAFC, on a GD3 Fit <!-- F-WP17-020 -->. A piggyback, remember, sits between the ECU and its sensors rather than replacing the ECU <!-- F-WP17-020 -->. Owners report that the VAFC could adjust the air-fuel mixture <!-- F-WP17-020 -->. Owners also report that trying to use it to affect VTEC engagement triggered the check engine light <!-- F-WP17-020 -->. VTEC is Honda's variable valve-timing system <!-- F-WP17-020 -->. This is a single community report, so treat the specific limitation as reported, not fully confirmed <!-- F-WP17-020 -->.

### A trap this research caught
While researching this chapter, a claim about a "+9 hp" header surfaced online <!-- F-WP17-016 -->. Tracing it back showed the header itself was built for the "2009-2012 Honda Fit," the newer car <!-- F-WP17-016 -->. The forum discussion about it also sat in a section for that newer car, not yours <!-- F-WP17-016 -->. This is exactly the kind of mix-up this book is built to catch <!-- F-WP17-016 -->. A number you find online for "a Honda Fit" might actually be for the newer, different car, not the one you own <!-- F-WP17-016 -->. Always check the model year before trusting a number <!-- F-WP17-016 -->.

### Putting it together
Real, current, full-reflash ECU tuning exists for your exact car, sold today by a mainstream company, for $595 or $795 <!-- F-WP17-022 -->. This is not a niche or discontinued method <!-- F-WP17-022 -->. Hondata sells the same kind of product family for many other current Honda and Acura models <!-- F-WP17-022 -->. The newer 2009-2014 Fit, by contrast, has no equivalent Hondata product at all <!-- F-WP17-022 -->. Do not assume the newer car has better tuning support only because it is newer <!-- F-WP17-022 -->. For this brand and this exact question, your 2007-2008 car is the one Hondata actually built for <!-- F-WP17-022 -->.
