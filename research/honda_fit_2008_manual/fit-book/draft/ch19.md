# Chapter 19: Documents to Keep in the Glovebox and at Home

## What you will learn
- Which three or four papers belong in your car's glovebox.
- Which papers belong at home instead, and why.
- Why the title should never travel in the car.
- How keeping good records now helps you later, including when you sell the car.

## The short version
Keep your registration, your insurance card, and your inspection sticker in the glovebox, because you may need to show them at any time. Keep your title, your bill of sale, and your maintenance records at home instead, in a safe place. Never keep the title in the car, because a stolen title makes a stolen car much easier to resell.

## The full explanation

### What belongs in the glovebox
Keep your current **registration** in the glovebox <!-- F-WP08-005 -->. Registration is the state's record that your car is legally allowed on the road <!-- F-WP08-005 -->. Keep your proof of insurance card there too <!-- F-WP08-005 -->. If your state issues a safety or emissions inspection sticker or certificate, keep the current one in the car as well <!-- F-WP08-005 -->. Some owners also keep a copy of the Buyers Guide sticker from when they bought the car, described in Chapter 17 <!-- F-WP08-005 -->. A card listing an emergency contact, plus any medical conditions or allergies, is also worth keeping in the glovebox <!-- F-WP08-005 -->. Do **not** keep the title in the glovebox <!-- F-WP08-005 -->. A stolen title makes a stolen car much easier for a thief to resell <!-- F-WP08-005 -->.

### What belongs at home
Keep your **title** at home, in a safe place <!-- F-WP08-006 -->. The title is the single most important paper for this car, and it is not something you need on a daily drive <!-- F-WP08-006 -->. Keep your original bill of sale and purchase paperwork with it <!-- F-WP08-006 -->. Keep your Buyers Guide sticker copy from the sale too <!-- F-WP08-006 -->. Keep the owner's manual at home, or write down where the digital copy is saved <!-- F-WP08-006 -->. Keep your maintenance and repair receipts together <!-- F-WP08-006 -->. They matter for warranty claims, and they help prove the car's service history if you ever sell it <!-- F-WP08-006 -->. Keep any recall notice letters you receive from Honda or NHTSA, covered in Chapter 18 <!-- F-WP08-006 -->. Keep your auto insurance policy documents at home too, separate from the insurance card in the car <!-- F-WP08-006 -->.

### Why this matters later
Good records at home make it much easier to answer "what wears by then" questions if you ever sell the car <!-- F-WP08-006 -->. Chapter 20 covers this in detail for the 2008 Fit specifically <!-- F-WP08-006 -->.
