# Appendix B: Fluid capacities and types

This appendix lists every fluid in your 2008 Fit: the type or spec to buy, and how much your car holds. A few terms come up more than once. **Viscosity** describes how thick or thin a fluid flows, written as a code like 5W-20. **MTF** stands for manual transmission fluid, the fluid inside a manual gearbox. **ATF** stands for automatic transmission fluid, the fluid inside an automatic transmission. **DOT** stands for Department of Transportation. A DOT number on brake fluid is a US safety-grade rating, and a higher number is not automatically "better" for your car. Using the wrong one can cause damage. Numbers marked M/T are for cars with a manual transmission; numbers marked A/T are for cars with an automatic.

## Engine coolant

Coolant, also called antifreeze, is the liquid that flows through your engine and radiator to keep the engine from overheating.

| What | Value | Fact ID |
|---|---|---|
| Type | Honda Long-Life Antifreeze/Coolant Type 2 only, pre-mixed 50% antifreeze and 50% water. Never add straight antifreeze or plain water. If Honda coolant is not available, a major-brand non-silicate coolant rated for aluminum engines may be used temporarily, then the system should be flushed and refilled with Honda coolant as soon as possible. | F-WP09-020 |
| Capacity after a coolant change, manual transmission | 3.8 L (1.00 US gal) | F-WP09-021 |
| Capacity after a coolant change, automatic transmission | 3.7 L (0.98 US gal) | F-WP09-022 |
| Capacity total, after engine overhaul, manual transmission | 5.4 L (1.43 US gal) | F-WP09-023 |
| Capacity total, after engine overhaul, automatic transmission | 5.3 L (1.40 US gal) | F-WP09-024 |
| Reserve tank capacity | 0.4 L (0.11 US gal) | F-WP09-025 |

## Manual transmission fluid (MTF)

| What | Value | Fact ID |
|---|---|---|
| Type | Honda Manual Transmission Fluid (MTF) preferred. If unavailable, an SAE 10W-30 or 10W-40 motor oil with the API Certification Seal ("For Gasoline Engines") may be used as a temporary replacement, but motor oil lacks the proper additives, and continued use can cause stiffer shifting. | F-WP09-026 |
| Capacity, fluid change | 1.6 US qt (1.5 L) | F-WP09-027 |
| Capacity, total (including the differential, after overhaul) | 1.7 US qt (1.6 L) | F-WP09-028 |

The differential is the set of gears inside the transmission housing that lets your two front wheels spin at different speeds when you turn. It shares the same fluid as the manual transmission on your Fit.

## Automatic transmission fluid (ATF)

| What | Value | Fact ID |
|---|---|---|
| Type | Honda Genuine ATF-Z1 only. Do not mix it with other transmission fluids. Using a different fluid can cause the transmission to wear out faster, and damage from using the wrong fluid is not covered by Honda's new-vehicle warranty. Note for today's buyer: Honda ATF-Z1 has since been discontinued industry-wide and replaced by Honda ATF DW-1 for service fills on many older Hondas; ask your parts store for the current equivalent. | F-WP09-029 |
| Capacity, fluid change | 2.5 US qt (2.4 L) | F-WP09-030 |
| Capacity, total (after overhaul) | 6.3 US qt (6.0 L) | F-WP09-031 |

## Brake fluid and clutch fluid

| What | Value | Fact ID |
|---|---|---|
| Type | Honda Heavy Duty Brake Fluid DOT 3 preferred, or a DOT 3 or DOT 4 brake fluid from a sealed container as a temporary replacement. Brake fluid marked DOT 5 is not compatible with your Fit's braking system and can cause extensive damage. | F-WP09-032 |
| Clutch fluid (manual transmission only) | Manual-transmission Fits use a hydraulic clutch that shares the same fluid specification as the brake system above. Fluid level should sit between the MIN and MAX marks on the clutch fluid reservoir, which has a gray cap; a low level can point to a leak in the clutch hydraulic system. | F-WP09-033 |
| Capacity | This work package's sources do not give a brake or clutch fluid capacity number for the 2008 Fit. | — |

## Power steering fluid

| What | Value | Fact ID |
|---|---|---|
| Type / capacity | Not applicable. Your 2008 Fit uses Electric Power-Assisted Rack-and-Pinion Steering (EPS) on every trim. EPS uses an electric motor to help you turn the wheel, not a hydraulic pump and fluid reservoir, so there is no power steering fluid to check or change. | F-WP09-034 |

## Windshield washer fluid

| What | Value | Fact ID |
|---|---|---|
| Type | Any commercially available windshield washer fluid. Do not use engine antifreeze or a vinegar-and-water mix in the washer reservoir. | F-WP09-035 |
| Capacity | We could not confirm this for the 2008 Fit. Check your owner's manual. | F-WP09-035 |
