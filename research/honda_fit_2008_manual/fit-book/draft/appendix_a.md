# Appendix A: Full specifications

This appendix lists every specification found for your 2008 Fit base trim: engine, transmission, dimensions, weight, cargo, fuel economy, crash ratings, and price. Most numbers below come straight from Honda's own 2008 press materials. Where a source disagreed with another, or where no reliable number could be found at all, that is written out honestly instead of leaving the row blank. A few terms are defined the first time they appear. **Redline** is the highest engine speed (in RPM, or revolutions per minute) the engine is designed to reach before the driver should shift or ease off. **Compression ratio** compares the volume inside a cylinder when the piston is at the bottom of its stroke to the volume when it is at the top; a higher ratio generally squeezes the air/fuel mixture more tightly before it burns. **Curb weight** is how much the car weighs by itself, with a full tank of fuel and all standard equipment, but no passengers or cargo. **Wheelbase** is the distance between the centers of the front and rear wheels. **MSRP** stands for manufacturer's suggested retail price — the sticker price Honda recommended before tax, license, registration, and destination charge.

| Item | Value | Fact ID |
|---|---|---|
