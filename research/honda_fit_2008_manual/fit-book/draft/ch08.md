# Chapter 8: Controls You Will Use Every Day

## What you will learn
- How to work the lights, turn signals, hazards, and horn
- How to run the wipers, washers, and both defrosters
- How the heating and air conditioning (HVAC, or heating, ventilation, and air conditioning) controls work
- How the power windows, door locks, and child locks work
- How the radio works, and why the base Fit has no aux jack (a port for plugging in a phone or music player)

## The short version
Most controls sit on the two steering-column levers or on the dashboard near the gauges. The left lever runs your lights and turn signals. The right lever runs your wipers and washers. The radio plays AM, FM, and CDs, but the US base Fit has no aux jack, so you cannot plug in a phone or MP3 player directly <!-- F-WP05-024 -->.

## The full explanation

### Horn
Press the center pad of the steering wheel to sound the horn <!-- F-WP05-001 -->.

### Headlights
The light switch sits on the left steering-column lever, the same lever you use for turn signals <!-- F-WP05-002 -->. Turn it one position on to light the parking lights, taillights, instrument panel, side markers, and rear license plate <!-- F-WP05-002 -->. Turn it to the next position to turn on the headlights <!-- F-WP05-002 -->. A "lights on" indicator on the instrument panel reminds you the lights are on. If you leave the lights on and turn the ignition to ACCESSORY or LOCK, that indicator stays lit, and a chime sounds when you open the driver's door <!-- F-WP05-002 -->.

### High beams
Push the left lever forward to turn on the high beams; a high-beam indicator lights up <!-- F-WP05-003 -->. Pull the lever back to return to low beams <!-- F-WP05-003 -->. To flash your high beams without leaving them on, pull the lever back lightly and let go; the high beams stay on only while you hold the lever <!-- F-WP05-003 -->.

### Turn signals
The same left lever signals your turns. Push it down to signal a left turn and up to signal a right turn <!-- F-WP05-004 -->. The lever returns to center on its own once the turn finishes, or when you release it <!-- F-WP05-004 -->. For a lane change, push the lever lightly and hold it; it will not latch, so it stops signaling when you let go <!-- F-WP05-004 -->.

### Hazard warning lights
A dedicated hazard button flashes all four turn signals and both dashboard turn-signal indicators at once <!-- F-WP05-005 -->. It sits near the instrument panel, grouped with the brightness knob and the rear defogger button <!-- F-WP05-005 -->.

### Windshield wipers and washer
The right steering-column lever runs your wipers <!-- F-WP05-006 -->. It has six positions. MIST runs the wipers at high speed only while you hold the lever up. OFF stops them. INT (intermittent) sweeps the wipers every few seconds. LO runs them at a steady low speed. HI runs them at a steady high speed. Pulling the lever toward you sprays washer fluid; the wipers run at low speed and make one more sweep after you let go <!-- F-WP05-006 -->. Two-speed, intermittent front wipers come standard on the base Fit, not only on the Sport trim <!-- F-WP05-007 -->.

### Rear wiper and washer
A separate rotary switch runs the rear wiper and washer <!-- F-WP05-008 -->. OFF parks the wiper. Turning the switch clockwise turns the rear wiper on. Holding it past ON also sprays the rear washer while the wiper runs. Turning the switch counterclockwise sprays the rear washer alone, without running the wiper <!-- F-WP05-008 -->. Both washers share one fluid tank; there is no separate rear tank <!-- F-WP05-008 -->. The rear wiper and washer come standard on base trim <!-- F-WP05-009 -->.

### Defrosting the front windshield
Turn the fan on; high speed clears the glass fastest. Slide the fresh air/recirculation lever to fresh air mode <!-- F-WP05-010 -->. Turn the mode control dial to the windshield-defroster setting <!-- F-WP05-010 -->. This turns on the air conditioner automatically, since dry air clears the glass faster; you cannot switch the A/C off in this mode <!-- F-WP05-010 -->. Set the temperature so the air feels warm. Closing the round dashboard corner vents sends more air to the defroster vents <!-- F-WP05-010 -->. Once the glass clears, switch back to fresh air mode so the windows do not fog again <!-- F-WP05-010 -->.

### Defrosting the rear window
Press the rear window defogger button on the dash to turn its heating grid on and off; the button has its own indicator light <!-- F-WP05-011 -->. This button includes a built-in shutoff timer, so it turns itself off after a set time rather than running forever <!-- F-WP05-012 -->. Never scrape the inside of the rear glass up and down; always wipe it side to side <!-- F-WP05-011 -->. The defogger's heating wires run along the inside of the glass, and scraping can damage them <!-- F-WP05-011 -->.

### Heating and air conditioning (HVAC)
Four main controls sit across the dash, left to right <!-- F-WP05-013 -->. The fan control dial raises or lowers airflow. The temperature control dial makes the air warmer as you turn it clockwise. The fresh air/recirculation lever picks where the air comes from. The mode control dial picks which vents the air flows from: dashboard vents, floor vents, a mix of the two, the windshield defroster, or a floor-and-defroster mix <!-- F-WP05-013 -->. A small amount of air always flows from the dashboard corner vents, no matter which mode you pick <!-- F-WP05-013 -->. A separate A/C button turns the air conditioning compressor on and off, with its own indicator light <!-- F-WP05-013 -->. Air conditioning comes standard on every 2008 US Fit, including base trim <!-- F-WP05-014 -->.

The fresh air/recirculation lever controls where intake air comes from <!-- F-WP05-015 -->. Slide it to fresh air, and outside air enters through intakes at the base of the windshield; keep that area clear of leaves and debris <!-- F-WP05-015 -->. Slide it to recirculation, and the system reuses air already inside the cabin <!-- F-WP05-015 -->. Leave the system in fresh air mode most of the time, since recirculation can fog your windows, especially with the A/C off <!-- F-WP05-015 -->. Switch to recirculation only for short stretches through dust or smoke, then switch back <!-- F-WP05-015 -->.

### Power windows
The ignition must be in the ON position for any window to move <!-- F-WP05-016 -->. Push a window switch down and hold it to lower that window; release it to stop partway <!-- F-WP05-016 -->. Pull back and hold to raise a window <!-- F-WP05-016 -->. The driver's window has an AUTO feature: push its switch firmly down past the first light push, then let go, and the window lowers all the way by itself <!-- F-WP05-016 -->. Pulling back briefly while it lowers stops it partway <!-- F-WP05-016 -->. A MAIN switch on the driver's door disables the passenger window switches when pushed in; the driver's own window keeps working <!-- F-WP05-016 -->. Push the MAIN switch again to turn the passenger switches back on <!-- F-WP05-016 -->. Keep the MAIN switch off, disabling the passenger windows, whenever children ride in the car, so they cannot operate a window and hurt themselves <!-- F-WP05-016 -->. Always check that everyone is clear before closing any window; a closing power window can seriously injure a hand or fingers <!-- F-WP05-016 -->. Power windows, including auto-down on the driver's side, come standard on base trim <!-- F-WP05-017 -->.

### Door locks
Each door has a lock tab on top; push it in to lock and pull it out to unlock <!-- F-WP05-018 -->. You can also lock or unlock the front doors with the ignition key <!-- F-WP05-018 -->. On the driver's door, pushing the top of the master power-lock switch locks all doors and the tailgate together; pushing the bottom unlocks them all <!-- F-WP05-018 -->. Each passenger door's own tab locks or unlocks only that door <!-- F-WP05-018 -->. Turning the key once in the driver's door from outside unlocks only that door; turning it again unlocks the rest <!-- F-WP05-018 -->. To lock a passenger door from outside, push in its tab and close the door <!-- F-WP05-018 -->. To lock the driver's door from outside, take the key out, push in the tab or press the top of the master switch, then close the door <!-- F-WP05-018 -->. A lockout-prevention feature exists too. With the driver's door open and the key still in the ignition, the master switch will not work, and pushing in the driver's tab does not lock the car <!-- F-WP05-018 -->. This design stops you from locking your only key inside by accident <!-- F-WP05-018 -->.

### Child safety locks
Each rear door has a childproof lock lever near its edge <!-- F-WP05-019 -->. Set it to LOCK, and that rear door cannot open from the inside handle at all, no matter how the regular lock tab is set <!-- F-WP05-019 -->. It opens only from outside <!-- F-WP05-019 -->. To release it, pull the lock tab up and open the door from outside <!-- F-WP05-019 -->.

### Dome light
The ceiling light has a three-position switch: ON, Door Activated, and OFF <!-- F-WP05-020 -->. In Door Activated mode, the light turns on when you open any door, or unlock the doors with the key <!-- F-WP05-020 -->. It fades out over about 30 seconds after every door closes tightly <!-- F-WP05-020 -->. It also turns on when you remove the key from the ignition, and fades the same way if you do not then open a door <!-- F-WP05-020 -->. If a door stays open with no key in the ignition, the light shuts off completely after 3 minutes to protect the battery <!-- F-WP05-020 -->.

### 12-volt power outlet
The accessory power socket sits in the dash's center pocket, directly above the aux jack pocket; pull up its rubber cover to use it <!-- F-WP05-021 -->. It powers 12-volt DC (direct current, the steady, one-direction electricity a car battery supplies) accessories rated 120 watts or less, about 10 amps; it will not power a heating element like an old cigarette lighter <!-- F-WP05-021 -->. The ignition must be in ACCESSORY or ON for it to have power; it goes dead in LOCK <!-- F-WP05-021 -->. This outlet comes standard on base trim <!-- F-WP05-022 -->.

### Radio and audio
The base Fit comes with a 160-watt AM/FM/CD system and 4 speakers <!-- F-WP05-023 -->. With the ignition in ACCESSORY or ON, turn the system on with the PWR/VOL knob or the AM/FM button, and adjust volume on the same knob <!-- F-WP05-023 -->. Change bands with the AM/FM button <!-- F-WP05-023 -->. Five ways exist to find a station. TUNE steps through frequencies one at a time, and SEEK searches automatically for the next strong signal <!-- F-WP05-023 -->. SCAN samples every strong station for about 10 seconds each <!-- F-WP05-023 -->. Six preset buttons store one AM station and up to two FM stations each, for 6 AM and 12 FM presets total <!-- F-WP05-023 -->. AUTO SELECT finds and stores strong local stations, which helps on a road trip far from home <!-- F-WP05-023 -->. The system also plays CDs and raises volume on its own as road noise increases with speed <!-- F-WP05-023 -->.

**Base trim has no aux jack.** This is an important, real limit of the car. An aux jack lets you plug a phone or music player into the stereo with a cable. Two official Honda sources confirm the aux jack ships only on the US Fit Sport and on Canadian models, never on the US base Fit <!-- F-WP05-024 -->. Compared to the Sport's 200-watt, 6-speaker system, the base system also lacks MP3/WMA playback and a 5-mode equalizer (a tool for adjusting different sound frequencies) <!-- F-WP05-025 -->. Speed-sensitive volume, however, works on both trims <!-- F-WP05-025 -->. The stereo gives you no direct way to plug a phone in. If you want to play music from a phone in a base Fit, you will need a separate solution, such as an FM transmitter.

To set the clock on base trim's radio, press and hold the CLOCK button until you hear a beep; the displayed time starts blinking <!-- F-WP05-026 -->. Press the H button repeatedly to set the hour, then the M button repeatedly to set the minutes <!-- F-WP05-026 -->. Press CLOCK again to lock in the time <!-- F-WP05-026 -->. To round to the nearest hour instead, press CLOCK until it beeps, then press the R button <!-- F-WP05-026 -->.

### Steering wheel
The steering wheel tilts up and down; this feature comes standard on base trim <!-- F-WP05-027 -->. Push the lever under the steering column all the way down to unlock the wheel <!-- F-WP05-027 -->. Move the wheel up or down while keeping the gauges visible, then push the lever back up to lock it in place <!-- F-WP05-027 -->. The wheel does not telescope, meaning it does not slide closer to or farther from you <!-- F-WP05-027 -->.

## Step by step: Defrost a foggy windshield
1. Turn the fan on; pick high speed to clear the glass faster <!-- F-WP05-010 -->.
2. Slide the fresh air/recirculation lever to fresh air mode <!-- F-WP05-010 -->.
3. Turn the mode control dial to the windshield-defroster setting <!-- F-WP05-010 -->.
4. Set the temperature dial so the air feels warm.
5. Close the round dashboard corner vents to send more air to the defroster vents <!-- F-WP05-010 -->.
6. Once the windshield clears, switch back to fresh air mode <!-- F-WP05-010 -->.

## What can go wrong
- Closing a power window on someone's fingers can cause serious injury; always check that hands and fingers are clear first <!-- F-WP05-016 -->.
- Leaving the passenger window switches active with a child in the car lets that child operate a window and get hurt; use the MAIN switch to disable them <!-- F-WP05-016 -->.
- Scraping the inside of the rear window up and down can damage the defogger's heating wires; always wipe side to side <!-- F-WP05-011 -->.
- Expecting an aux jack on a base-trim stereo will leave you stuck; the US base Fit does not have one <!-- F-WP05-024 -->.
- Leaving a door open with the key in the ignition can drain your battery through the dome light and chimes. The light itself shuts off after 3 minutes to help protect the battery <!-- F-WP05-020 -->.
