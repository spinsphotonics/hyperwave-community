# Chapter 1: What This Car Is

## What you will learn

- Why Honda built the Fit, and what kind of car it is.
- Why the year and the generation of your Fit matter.
- What a chassis code is, and which one your car has.
- Where your 2008 Fit was built, and which trims Honda sold in the US that year.

## The short version

Your car is a 2008 Honda Fit, base trim. Honda built it at its plant in Suzuka, Japan. <!-- F-WP01-035 --> In the US, Honda sold only two versions of the 2008 Fit: the base Fit and the Fit Sport. <!-- F-WP01-026 --> Your car belongs to the first generation of the Fit, a car with the internal code GD3. It is not the same car as the Fit that came after it.

## The full explanation

### A small car built to hold a lot

The Honda Fit is a small hatchback, a car with a rear door that swings up instead of a trunk lid. Honda built the Fit around a 1.5-liter engine, a size measurement that tells you how much air and fuel the engine's cylinders can hold. <!-- F-WP01-002 --> Later chapters cover the engine, the size of the car, and how far a tank of gas takes you. For now, the key point is this: the Fit was built to be small on the outside but roomy on the inside. It was also built to use less fuel than a bigger car.

Honda's engine for this car carries the code L15A1. <!-- F-WP01-001 --> An engine code is a short label a manufacturer uses to identify one specific engine design, separate from any other engine that company builds. You will see this code on parts boxes and repair information, so it is worth remembering.

### Why the generation of your Fit matters

A chassis code is a short label Honda uses inside the company to identify one specific version of a car's structure and parts. Your 2008 Fit carries the chassis code GD3. <!-- F-WP01-031 --> Every part built for this car, from body panels to engine mounts, was designed around that GD3 structure.

The safety-testing group IIHS states that the Fit "was introduced in the 2007 model year" in the US. <!-- F-WP01-037 --> That means the GD3 Fit was sold new in the US only for the 2007 and 2008 model years. A model year is the year Honda assigns to a specific version of a car. It does not always match the calendar year the car was actually built or sold.

Honda later replaced this car with a new design. That newer Fit used a different chassis code and different parts throughout, even though it kept the same name. This book covers only the first-generation, 2008, GD3 Fit. If your car is a Fit built for model year 2009 or later, the two cars share a name, but they do not share a design. Do not use this book's specific numbers, part information, or procedures for that later car. Whenever this book gives you a number, a part, or a step, it is speaking about the 2008 GD3 Fit only.

### Base trim and Fit Sport

Honda's 2008 US lineup for the Fit had exactly two trims, a trim being one specific equipment level of a car that a manufacturer sells under one name. Those two trims were the base Fit and the Fit Sport. <!-- F-WP01-026 --> This book is written for the base Fit. Chapter 2 walks through exactly what the base trim has, and what it does not have compared with the Fit Sport.

### Where your car was built

Honda built the 2008 Fit at its plant in Suzuka, in Japan's Mie Prefecture. <!-- F-WP01-035 --> Every 2008 US-market Fit, base and Sport alike, came from that same plant.
