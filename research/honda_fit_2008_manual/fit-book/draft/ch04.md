# Chapter 4: Keys, locks, and getting in

## What you will learn

- How the key and the anti-theft chip inside it work
- Why your Fit does not have a remote fob, and how to lock and unlock it instead
- How to use the child safety lock on the back doors
- How to find the fuel door and the tailgate (the rear hatch door) release
- How to open the hood safely, step by step

## The short version

Your base 2008 Fit uses a plain metal key, not a fob, and that key has a hidden chip that talks to the car's anti-theft system. <!-- F-WP03-001 --> You lock and unlock the car with the key, a small tab on each door, or a power switch on the driver's door. <!-- F-WP03-003 --> The hood, fuel door, and tailgate (the rear hatch, since the Fit is a hatchback and has no separate trunk) each open a different way. <!-- F-WP03-007 --> This chapter walks you through all of them.

## The full explanation

### The key and the immobilizer

Your car came with two metal keys and a small tag with a key number on it, which you use to order a new key later. <!-- F-WP03-001 --> Each key has a chip built into it. This chip talks to your car's immobilizer, a system that stops the engine from starting unless it senses the right chip. <!-- F-WP03-001 --> The key has no battery, but a damaged chip still stops the car from starting. <!-- F-WP03-001 -->

Some Fit models come with a remote transmitter, a small fob you press to lock or unlock the car from a distance. Your base Fit does not have one. <!-- F-WP03-002 --> That feature is built into the Sport trim only. On your car, you lock and unlock every door using the key or the switches inside the car. <!-- F-WP03-002 -->

### Locking and unlocking the doors

Every door has a small tab on top. Push the tab in to lock that door, and pull it out to unlock it. <!-- F-WP03-003 --> Your Fit also has power door locks, which use an electric switch instead of your hand at each door. On the driver's door, a master switch locks every door and the tailgate at once when you press the top of it, and unlocks all of them when you press the bottom. <!-- F-WP03-003 --> Turning the key in the driver's door lock also works: the first turn locks or unlocks only the driver's door, and the second turn locks or unlocks the rest of the car. <!-- F-WP03-003 --> One safety note: the master switch will not work while the driver's door is open and the key is in the ignition, the slot where you turn the key to start the engine. <!-- F-WP03-003 -->

### The child safety lock

Each rear door has a small lock lever near its rear edge. <!-- F-WP03-004 --> Set that lever to LOCK, and the rear door cannot open from inside, no matter how the regular door lock tab is set. <!-- F-WP03-004 --> The door still opens normally from outside using the outer handle. <!-- F-WP03-004 --> Use this lock any time a child rides in the back seat.

### The fuel door

Your base Fit's fuel door has no lever or cable inside the car. <!-- F-WP03-006 --> To open it, walk around to the side of the car and pull on the notched edge of the fuel door itself. <!-- F-WP03-006 --> The fuel cap, the small cap that seals the gas tank opening, stays attached to the car by a short tether, so you cannot drop it or lose it while refueling. <!-- F-WP03-006 -->

### The tailgate

The Fit is a hatchback, so its rear cargo door is called the tailgate, not a trunk. <!-- F-WP03-007 --> It opens only from the outside: pull its handle, then lift it up. <!-- F-WP03-007 --> It has no separate button or lever inside the car. Instead, it locks and unlocks together with the driver's door whenever you use the key, the door lock tab, or the master power-lock switch. <!-- F-WP03-007 --> On your base trim, you can also lock or unlock the tailgate directly with the key. <!-- F-WP03-007 -->

## Step by step: Open the hood

**Safety warnings**

- Always park on level, flat ground and set the parking brake before you open the hood. <!-- P-WP03-1 -->
- Always let the engine cool before you open the hood if you recently finished driving. <!-- P-WP03-1 -->
- Do not open the hood if you see steam or spray coming from underneath it. Wait until it stops. <!-- F-WP03-005 -->
- Always check that the support rod, the metal rod that holds the hood open, is properly seated in its hole before you let go of the hood. <!-- P-WP03-1 -->

**Steps**

1. Park on level ground, turn off the engine, and set the parking brake.
2. Reach under the lower-left corner of the dashboard, near your left knee, and pull the hood release handle. <!-- F-WP03-005 -->
3. The hood will pop up slightly but stay held by a second, hidden latch.
4. Walk to the front of the car and slide your fingers under the hood's front edge, near the center.
5. Feel for the second latch handle and push it to release the hood. <!-- F-WP03-005 -->
6. Lift the hood the rest of the way open.
7. Pull the metal support rod free from its clip under the hood.
8. Fit the tip of the support rod into the matching hole on the underside of the hood. <!-- F-WP03-005 -->
9. Let go of the hood slowly to make sure the rod is holding its weight.

**To close the hood**

10. Lift the hood slightly to free the support rod from its hole, then clip the rod back in place.
11. Lower the hood to about one foot above the front fenders, the body panels over the front wheels, then let it drop the rest of the way. <!-- F-WP03-005 -->
12. Push down lightly on the hood to confirm it caught and is fully latched.

**How you know it worked:** The hood stays open on its own, held only by the rod, with no need for you to hold it up. When closed, it sits flush and does not move when you push down on it.

## What can go wrong

- You pull only the interior release and never find the second hood latch, so the hood will not lift.
- You let go of an open hood before the support rod is properly seated in its hole.
- You slam the hood shut from too low, instead of letting it drop the last foot on its own.
