# Chapter 60: Track-day prep checklist

## What you will learn

- What to do the night before a track day
- What to check and bring the morning of the event
- What to check between track sessions
- Why finishing car prep early matters more than doing it at the last minute

## The short version

Finish your car prep several days before the event, not the night before, and pack your gear bag completely so the morning is simple. <!-- F-WP20-008 --> On the morning of, check your cold tire pressure and dress for the day's required clothing. <!-- F-WP20-009 --> Between sessions, recheck tire pressure and wheel torque, and take time to eat, drink, and rest. <!-- F-WP20-010 -->

## The full explanation

### The night before

Finish any car repairs or upgrades several days ahead of the event, not the night before. <!-- F-WP20-008 --> A last-minute repair that goes wrong, like a broken bolt or a surprise problem, is one of the most common ways a driver arrives at the track exhausted and unprepared. <!-- F-WP20-008 --> The night before, pack your car and gear bag completely, so the morning of the event stays simple. <!-- F-WP20-008 --> Pack your helmet and gloves, your completed or shop-signed tech inspection form, a pen, and a tire pressure gauge. <!-- F-WP20-008 --> Also pack a small tool bag with a jack, a wheel-lock key if your car uses one, and zip ties. <!-- F-WP20-008 --> Also pack a filled gas container if the track does not sell fuel, and a cooler with water or a sports drink and food. <!-- F-WP20-008 --> Bring cash for tow-truck tips or tolls, and a toll transponder if you will drive toll roads to get there. <!-- F-WP20-008 --> Many event organizers hold check-in the evening before the event. <!-- F-WP20-008 --> Attending it avoids a morning-of line for waivers, the tech form, car numbers, and your event wristband or tech sticker. <!-- F-WP20-008 -->

### The morning of

Check your tire pressures while the tires are still cold, right at the start of the day. <!-- F-WP20-009 --> Tire pressure rises once the tires warm up on track. <!-- F-WP20-009 --> Note your cold baseline before you leave, so you can adjust it correctly later. <!-- F-WP20-009 --> Confirm the car has no new fluid leaks and no dashboard warning lights before you leave home. <!-- F-WP20-009 --> Dress for the event's required clothing rather than showing up in shorts and sandals. <!-- F-WP20-009 --> Many organizations, including NASA, require long sleeves and pants, and closed-toe shoes. <!-- F-WP20-009 --> Bring sunscreen, comfortable weather-appropriate clothing, snacks, and plenty of water. <!-- F-WP20-009 --> A good rule of thumb is roughly one quart of fluid for every 50 pounds of body weight over a full track day. <!-- F-WP20-009 --> Heat and nervous energy both increase how much fluid you lose. <!-- F-WP20-009 --> Arrive with enough time before the mandatory drivers' meeting to unload, get through registration and tech, and not feel rushed. <!-- F-WP20-009 -->

### Between sessions

After each session, recheck tire pressure, and tire temperature too if you have a temperature gun on hand. <!-- F-WP20-010 --> Track surface temperature and how hard you drive both change your tire pressure through the day. <!-- F-WP20-010 --> Recheck your wheel and lug torque periodically, since track driving can work fasteners loose faster than everyday street driving. <!-- F-WP20-010 --> Top off engine oil if your car is a known oil-consumer, or at least check the level, using oil and a funnel packed for exactly this purpose. <!-- F-WP20-010 --> Use the break between sessions to rehydrate, eat something, and rest in the shade, rather than only working on the car. <!-- F-WP20-010 --> Staying hydrated and fed directly affects how well, and how safely, you drive later in the day. <!-- F-WP20-010 --> Many organized programs hold an instructor debrief or driver meeting between sessions. <!-- F-WP20-010 --> Taking a few notes on what changed between sessions is a widely repeated habit. <!-- F-WP20-010 --> A braking point, an odd noise, or a new tire pressure are all worth writing down, for tracking progress and problems across the day. <!-- F-WP20-010 -->
