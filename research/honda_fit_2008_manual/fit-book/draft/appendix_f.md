# Appendix F: Part numbers for common maintenance items

This appendix lists the Honda genuine part numbers, sizes, and specifications for the maintenance items you are most likely to buy for your 2008 Fit base trim: filters, spark plugs, the battery, wiper blades, and light bulbs. A **part number** is the unique code a manufacturer or parts store uses to identify one exact part, so you can order the correct one instead of guessing from a picture. A **trade number** is a standardized code (like "7443" or "T10") that many bulb makers use for the same size and shape of light bulb, so the same trade number generally works across different car brands. Where this project's research could not find a real part number, that is written out honestly below rather than left blank or guessed — do not buy a part based on a made-up number; ask a Honda parts counter or dealer, or bring your old part in for a match.

## Battery

| Item | Value | Notes | Fact ID |
|---|---|---|---|
| Battery group size | 151R | This is the original-equipment size for the 2008 Fit. Group 51R is a commonly reported upgrade with more cold-cranking amps, but it is a noticeably tighter physical fit, and some owners report needing to modify the battery tray to use it. This number was not independently confirmed by a Honda document during this research pass, so treat it as medium confidence rather than certain. | F-WP09-046 |
