# Chapter 5: Sitting right

## What you will learn

- How to slide and recline the driver's seat correctly
- How to tilt the steering wheel and adjust the head restraints (headrests)
- How your power mirrors work, and why you set them before you drive
- How to fasten your seat belt so it protects you correctly
- The full setup routine to run every time you get in, before you start driving

## The short version

Before you drive, you slide your seat, tilt the steering wheel, set your head restraints, aim your mirrors, and fasten your seat belt correctly. <!-- F-WP03-008 --> Your base Fit has power mirrors and a tilt-only steering wheel, so you use switches and a lever instead of pushing on things by hand. <!-- F-WP03-010 --> Doing all of this before you move keeps you safe, because these adjustments protect you in a crash and help you see the road. <!-- P-WP03-2 -->

## The full explanation

### The seat

The driver's seat slides forward and backward using a lever under the front edge of the seat cushion. <!-- F-WP03-008 --> Lift the lever, slide the seat to where you want it, then let go and rock the seat to confirm it caught. <!-- F-WP03-008 --> A second lever on the side of the seat-back, the part of the seat behind your back, tilts that backrest angle. <!-- F-WP03-008 --> Pull the lever out, lean the seat-back to the angle you want, and release the lever to lock it. <!-- F-WP03-008 --> The rear outer seat-backs recline too, using a release lever on the outside edge of each one. <!-- F-WP03-008 --> The owner's manual does not give an exact travel distance or a number of notches for either seat adjustment. <!-- F-WP03-008 --> Reclining your seat-back too far is dangerous, because a reclined seat-back stops your seat belt from holding you properly in a crash.

### The steering wheel

Your base Fit's steering wheel tilts up and down, but it does not telescope, meaning it does not slide toward or away from you. <!-- F-WP03-009 --> To adjust it, push the lever under the steering column, the part of the steering assembly under the wheel, all the way down to unlock it. <!-- F-WP03-009 --> Move the wheel to an angle where you can still see all the gauges, the dials in front of you, then push the lever back up to lock it. <!-- F-WP03-009 -->

### The head restraints

Every seating position has a head restraint, the padded part above the seat-back that supports your head. <!-- F-WP03-011 --> The two front head restraints slide up and down. <!-- F-WP03-011 --> Pull straight up to raise one, or press its release button sideways while you push it down to lower it, and always use both hands and never do this while driving. <!-- F-WP03-011 --> Line up the middle of the back of your head with the center of the restraint. <!-- F-WP03-011 --> The rear outer head restraints do not adjust for height; they only lift out for cleaning after you press a release button. <!-- F-WP03-011 -->

### The mirrors

Your base Fit has power mirrors on the outside, not manual ones you push by hand. <!-- F-WP03-010 --> With the ignition on, use a selector switch to pick the driver's or the passenger's mirror, then use a second switch to tilt that mirror up, down, left, or right. <!-- F-WP03-010 --> Return the selector switch to its center, off position when you finish, so the setting locks in place. <!-- F-WP03-010 --> The inside rear-view mirror also has a day and night flip tab that cuts glare from headlights behind you at night. <!-- F-WP03-010 -->

### The seat belt

To buckle up, push the metal latch plate into the buckle and tug on it to confirm it caught. <!-- F-WP03-012 --> Press the red button on the buckle when you want to release it. <!-- F-WP03-012 --> For a correct fit, pull the lap portion down low across your hip bones first, then pull the shoulder portion up to remove slack, so it crosses the middle of your chest and shoulder, not your neck or arm. <!-- F-WP03-012 --> If the belt still crosses your neck or arm, change the shoulder anchor height instead. <!-- F-WP03-012 --> Each front shoulder belt anchor, the fixed point where the belt attaches near your shoulder, slides up and down a track with four height positions. <!-- F-WP03-013 --> Squeeze the two small buttons on the anchor to unlock it and slide it. <!-- F-WP03-013 --> This height adjuster exists only on the front seats, not the rear ones. <!-- F-WP03-013 -->

## Step by step: Adjust your seat and mirrors before driving

**Safety warnings**

- Always make every seat, head restraint, mirror, and steering wheel adjustment before you start driving, never while the vehicle is moving. <!-- F-WP03-008, F-WP03-009, F-WP03-010, F-WP03-011 -->
- Always sit as far back from the steering wheel as you safely can while still reaching the controls. Sitting too close raises your injury risk if the front airbag inflates. <!-- P-WP03-2 -->
- Do not recline the seat-back too far. A reclined seat-back stops the seat belt from holding you properly in a crash. <!-- P-WP03-2 -->
- Always confirm the seat and seat-back are locked in position by rocking them before you drive. <!-- F-WP03-008 -->

**Steps**

1. Sit in the seat and pull up the lever under the front edge of the seat cushion. <!-- F-WP03-008 -->
2. Slide the seat to a position where you can fully press the pedals, then release the lever.
3. Rock the seat forward and back to make sure it is locked in place.
4. Pull the lever on the side of the seat-back and lean to a comfortable, upright angle.
5. Release the lever to lock the seat-back, then rock it to confirm it caught. <!-- F-WP03-008 -->
6. Pull straight up on each front head restraint, or press its side button and push down, until it is level with the middle of your head. <!-- F-WP03-011 -->
7. Push the lever under the steering column down to unlock the wheel. <!-- F-WP03-009 -->
8. Move the wheel up or down until you can see all the gauges clearly, then push the lever back up to lock it.
9. Try moving the wheel to confirm it is locked in place.
10. Flip the tab under the inside mirror to day mode, then tilt it for a clear view out the back window.
11. Turn the ignition to the ON position and move the mirror selector switch to the driver's side. <!-- F-WP03-010 -->
12. Press the edges of the mirror adjustment switch until that mirror shows a clear view, then repeat for the passenger's side mirror.
13. Return the mirror selector switch to its center, off position so the setting is not changed by accident.
14. Fasten your seat belt, then pull the lap portion low and snug and the shoulder portion across your chest. <!-- F-WP03-012 -->
15. If the belt crosses your neck or arm, squeeze the two buttons on the shoulder anchor and slide it to a better height. <!-- F-WP03-013 -->

**How you know it worked:** You can fully press the pedals and see every gauge, both outside mirrors show a clear view without you leaning, and the shoulder belt rests across the middle of your chest, not your neck.

## What can go wrong

- You adjust the mirrors, seat, or steering wheel after you already started driving.
- You leave the seat too close to the steering wheel.
- You forget to lock the seat-back angle lever.
- You leave the mirror selector switch turned on instead of centering it, which can let the setting drift.
