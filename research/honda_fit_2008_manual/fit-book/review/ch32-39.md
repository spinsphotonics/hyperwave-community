## R2 — Safety

**Chapter 32 (fuse/bulb/wiper replacement procedures):** All four "Step by
step" procedures (headlight bulb, tail/brake bulbs, wiper blades, fuse) carry
a "Safety warnings, before you..." block immediately before their numbered
steps. PASS, no changes needed.

**Chapter 33 (wash/wax/interior clean procedure):** Safety warnings block
(antenna removal, no bleach/solvents on belts, air-dry fully, no harsh
cleaners on wheels) sits before the numbered steps. PASS, no changes needed.

**Chapters 34, 37, 38, 39 (non-procedural):** No content instructs a reader to
bypass a safety system, drive with a known brake or steering problem, or
otherwise act recklessly. Flashing-check-engine-light guidance ("pull over
and stop driving"), brake-fluid-leak guidance ("stop driving and have it
inspected right away"), and burning-smell guidance ("pull over, shut off the
engine") are all correctly framed as stop-driving warnings, consistent with
D9. PASS, no changes needed.

**Chapter 35 (check engine light):** Correctly separates "steady light, keep
driving carefully" from "flashing light, stop now" and never tells the
reader to ignore or clear a code as a substitute for a repair. PASS.

**Chapter 36 (won't-start flowchart):** One issue found and fixed. Step 9
(jump-starting) originally gave the connection order first and only *then*
told the reader to follow Chapter 16's safety warnings — warning after the
action, which conflicts with D9 ("the book says so before the steps, not
after"). Fixed by reordering so the safety-warning sentence comes first,
before the connection-order steps.

## R3 — Facts and legal

**Fact ID coverage:** Every number, code, and cost in ch32-39 is followed by
an HTML Fact ID comment (`<!-- F-WP##-### -->` or `<!-- P-WP##-# -->`), or in
ch32's bulb table, carries its Fact ID in an explicit table column. Numbers

of these are factual claims requiring a citation.

**Chapter 35's OBD-II code table — spot-checked against facts/WP-12.md:**
All 20 codes were compared line-by-line against their Fact ID cards
(F-WP12-002 through F-WP12-021). Meanings, "first thing to check" guidance,
and the Source-column tags (TSB / 2+ / 1 only) all match. Specifically
confirmed:
- **P2649 is NOT linked to TSB 13-021.** ch35.md explicitly states: "this
  code is not covered by the same Honda bulletin as P2646/P2647, unlike an
  earlier version of this book's notes claimed" — matching facts/WP-12.md's
  own VERIFIER CORRECTION note on F-WP12-012, which struck a prior false
  claim that TSB 13-021 covered P2646/P2647/P2651/P2652 *and* implied it also
  covered P2649. The TSB in fact names only the oil pressure *switch* codes
  (P2646/P2647/P2651/P2652), not the oil control *solenoid* code (P2649).
  ch37.md's cross-reference to "P2646, P2647, and P2649" together is a
  chapter pointer only and makes no TSB claim. No drift found — this is
  correctly and consistently NOT linked anywhere in ch32-39.
- P2646/P2647 correctly ARE shown as TSB-backed ("TSB" tag), consistent with
  facts/WP-12.md.
- P0451 and P0113 correctly carry the "1 only" single-source flag, matching
  WP-12.md's own single-source caveat for those two codes.
