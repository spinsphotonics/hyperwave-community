## R2 — Safety (warnings before step 1; jack stands if lifted; engine off/cool for fluid work; eye protection for brake/battery work)

Chapters 1, 2, 3, and 7 contain no procedures ("Step by step" sections) —
N/A, as expected (they are identification/spec/dashboard-reference
chapters).

**ch04 — "Open the hood":** Safety warnings (level ground + parking brake;
let the engine cool; do not open if venting steam; confirm the support rod
is seated) all appear in a dedicated block before Step 1, matching
`procedures/WP-03-1.md` verbatim. No car-lifting, no fluid handling, no
brake/battery work — jack-stand and eye-protection checks are N/A. PASS.

**ch05 — "Adjust your seat and mirrors before driving":** Warnings (never
adjust while moving; sit back from the wheel; don't over-recline; confirm
locks by rocking) appear before Step 1, matching `procedures/WP-03-2.md`
verbatim. No lifting/fluid/brake/battery content. PASS.

**ch06 — Starting a manual/automatic transmission Fit:** This chapter has
no heading literally titled "Step by step" — the numbered starting steps
sit inside "The full explanation" and are drawn directly from fact cards
(F-WP04-001 through -005), not from a dedicated procedure card (WP-04
produced no procedure cards per its research brief). Scored as N/A against
the strict "Step by step" rubric. Flagging rather than fixing, per the
"don't invent a warning the source doesn't have" rule: neither the fact
cards nor the chapter mentions the standard "never run the engine in an
enclosed space/garage" carbon-monoxide warning that a real owner's manual
or mechanic would typically include near engine-starting instructions. The
underlying WP-04 fact cards (checked directly) do not contain this warning
anywhere, so it was not invented here — recommend a future research pass
add a fact card for it (likely sourced from the owner's manual's general
"Safety Message" front matter) so a later editing pass can add the warning
with a citation. No lifting, fluid, or brake/battery work otherwise.

**ch07:** No procedures (a reference table only). N/A.

**ch08 — "Defrost a foggy windshield":** This is a low-risk knob/lever
adjustment (fan, vent mode, temperature dial) with no lifting, no fluid
handling, and no brake/battery work, so the jack-stand / engine-off-and-cool
/ eye-protection checks are N/A. It has no dedicated "Safety warnings" block,
but none of the source facts (F-WP05-010) describe a hazard for this task,
and inventing one would violate the "don't invent" rule — scored PASS with
this note rather than flagged, since no real warning is being omitted.

**ch09 — Utility/Tall/Long/Refresh Mode (4 procedures):** All four have a
"Safety warnings, before you start:" block placed before "Steps:", matching
`procedures/WP-05-1.md` through `WP-05-4.md` verbatim, including the
airbag-clearance, tie-down, and "park + engine off before reclining" (Refresh
Mode) warnings. No lifting or brake/battery work. PASS for all four.

**ch10 — "Refuel the car":** Safety warnings (gasoline is flammable;
engine off before fueling; no heat/sparks/flame; outdoors only; wipe spills;
never top off after auto-click-off) appear before Step 1, matching
`procedures/WP-05-5.md` verbatim. "Engine off" is explicitly stated as
required for this under-hood-adjacent fluid task. No lifting or brake/
battery work (eye protection N/A). PASS.
