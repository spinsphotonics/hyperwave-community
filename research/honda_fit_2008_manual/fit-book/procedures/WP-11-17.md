Procedure ID: P-WP11-17
Chapter: 30 (Coolant, transmission fluid, brake fluid, power steering, washer fluid)
Title: Check and change the automatic transmission fluid
Skill level: 3 (needs jack stands and care for the drain step; the level check alone is skill level 1)
Time: 15 minutes for a level check; 40 minutes for a full drain and refill

Tools:
- Jack and 2 jack stands, or ramps (see P-WP11-22), for the drain step only
- Wrench or socket to fit the drain plug (size UNVERIFIED — check yours, see Appendix C)
- Drain pan
- Fluid pump or long-neck funnel, to refill through the dipstick tube

Parts and supplies:
- Honda ATF-Z1 (Automatic Transmission Fluid) only — the manual is explicit that mixing in another brand's ATF can cause deterioration and damage not covered by the new-vehicle warranty [Source: S-001/S-023, Tier 1]
- Approximate capacity is on the order of 2.5 US qt (2.4 L) at a drain-and-refill, versus about 6.3 US qt (6.0 L) total in the whole system — this larger "total" figure is only reached by a full teardown, not a simple drain, so a normal service only replaces roughly the smaller amount; check Appendix B for the fully verified split [Source: S-001/S-023, S-024, medium confidence]
- New washer for the drain bolt

Safety warnings:
- Do not work under a car held up only by a jack. Use jack stands or ramps.
- Always check and fill the level with the engine at normal operating temperature and the car on level ground, per the manual.
- Do not fill above the dipstick's upper mark; overfilling can damage the transmission.
- Always clean up any spilled fluid immediately.

Steps:

Level check only:
1. Park on level ground and start the engine, letting it run until the radiator fan comes on, then shut it off.
2. Wait about 60 seconds, but no longer than 90 seconds, before checking.
3. Remove the dipstick (yellow loop) and wipe it clean with a rag.
4. Insert the dipstick fully back into the transmission.
5. Remove it again and read the fluid level against the upper and lower marks.
6. If the level is below the lower mark, add Honda ATF-Z1 through the dipstick tube until it is between the marks.
7. Reinsert the dipstick fully and securely.

Full drain and refill, continuing after the level check:
8. Raise the car and support it on jack stands.
9. Place the drain pan under the transmission's drain plug.
10. Remove the drain plug and let the old fluid fully drain.
11. Reinstall the drain plug with a new washer, tightened securely.
12. Lower the car back to level ground.
13. Add fresh Honda ATF-Z1 through the dipstick tube using a pump or long funnel.
14. Recheck the level using steps 1 through 5 above, topping off as needed.
15. Check for leaks at the drain plug after a short drive.

How you know it worked: The dipstick reads between the upper and lower marks after the correct warm-up-and-wait routine, there are no leaks at the drain plug, and the car shifts smoothly with no delay or harsh engagement.

Common mistakes:
- Checking the level cold, or without waiting the 60-90 seconds after shutdown, which gives an inaccurate reading.
- Using any ATF other than Honda ATF-Z1.
- Overfilling past the upper mark.
- Forgetting a new drain-plug washer, causing a slow leak.
