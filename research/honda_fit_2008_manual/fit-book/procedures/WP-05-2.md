Procedure ID: P-WP05-2
Chapter: 9
Title: Set up Tall Mode (flip the rear seat cushions up for tall items)
Skill level: 1 = anyone
Time: 3 minutes for a first-timer
Tools:
- None required
Parts and supplies:
- None required
Safety warnings:
- Always remove any items from the rear seat before you fold the cushion up.
- Always make sure the cushion is locked firmly against the seat-back before loading anything against it.
- Do not stack items higher than the top of the seat-backs — they can block your rear view and be thrown around the vehicle in a crash.
- Do not put cargo where it blocks the driver's view through the rear window.
Steps:
1. Remove everything from the rear seat cushion you are flipping up.
2. Pull up on the rear seat cushion.
3. Fold the seat leg down as the cushion comes up.
4. Push the seat cushion firmly against the seat-back until it locks in place.
5. Repeat for the other side if you want both cushions up.
6. Load a tall item into the open floor space now created in front of the seat-back.
7. To return to normal seating afterward, make sure the floor under the seat is empty first.
8. Pull the seat leg up fully.
9. Slowly push the seat cushion back down while holding it with both hands.
10. Set the seat leg into its floor guide. A latch appears when it is seated correctly.
How you know it worked: The seat cushion stays locked upright against the seat-back on its own — it does not fall back down when you let go.
Common mistakes:
- Not pushing the cushion firmly enough against the seat-back, so it isn't actually locked and falls during driving.
- Forgetting to fold the seat leg down, which can leave the mechanism loose.
- Loading an item so tall it blocks the rear window or interferes with the seat-backs when you later want to return the seat to normal.
Source IDs: S-001 (Honda Owner's Manual) (F-WP05-031, F-WP05-028)
