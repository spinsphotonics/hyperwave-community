Procedure ID: P-WP07-2
Chapter: 16
Title: Jump start the car
Skill level: 2 (needs basic tools — jumper cables and a booster battery or vehicle)
Time: 15 minutes for a first-timer
Tools:
Jumper cables
A booster battery or another vehicle with a good battery
Parts and supplies:
None
Safety warnings:
Always wear eye protection while working near a battery. Batteries can vent hydrogen gas and contain acid, and a spark near the terminals can cause a small explosion.
Always keep sparks, open flames, and smoking materials away from the battery. It can explode.
Do not jump start a battery with slushy or frozen electrolyte. Let it thaw first.
Do not connect the second jumper cable to the dead battery's negative terminal or to any other engine part. Use the grounding point ("stay") shown in your manual instead.
Always turn off all electrical accessories on both vehicles before connecting cables.
Do not try to push- or pull-start a car with an automatic transmission. It will not work.
Always keep loose jumper cable ends away from each other and from metal until fully disconnected.
Steps:
1. Open the hood and check the physical condition of your battery before doing anything else.
2. In cold weather, check that the battery's electrolyte is not slushy or frozen.
3. Turn off the heater, A/C, audio system, lights, and every other electrical accessory.
4. Put the transmission in neutral (manual) or Park (automatic), and set the parking brake.
5. If using another vehicle, have someone start it and run it at a fast idle.
6. Connect one jumper cable to the positive (+) terminal on your dead battery.
7. Connect the other end of that cable to the positive (+) terminal on the booster battery.
8. Connect the second jumper cable to the negative (-) terminal on the booster battery.
9. Connect the other end of the second cable to the grounding stay on your car, not the battery.
10. Start your engine. If cranking is slow, recheck that every cable clamp has a solid metal contact.
11. Once your car is running, disconnect the negative cable from your car, then from the booster battery.
12. Disconnect the positive cable from your car, then from the booster battery.
13. Keep the loose cable ends apart and away from any metal until both cables are fully removed.
How you know it worked: The engine starts and keeps running on its own once both cables are disconnected, and the charging system indicator is off.
Common mistakes: Connecting the last cable to the dead battery's negative terminal instead of a ground point. Leaving accessories on while connecting cables. Trying to push-start an automatic. Letting stray cable ends touch metal while still connected on one end.
