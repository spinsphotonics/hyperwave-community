Procedure ID: P-WP03-2
Chapter: 5
Title: Adjust your seat and mirrors before driving
Skill level: 1 = anyone
Time: 5 minutes for a first-timer
Tools:
None required
Parts and supplies:
None
Safety warnings:
Always make every seat, head restraint, mirror, and steering wheel adjustment before you start driving, never while the vehicle is moving. (F-WP03-008, F-WP03-009, F-WP03-010, F-WP03-011)
Always sit as far back from the steering wheel as you safely can while still reaching the controls; sitting too close raises your injury risk if the front airbag inflates.
Do not recline the seat-back too far. A reclined seat-back stops the seat belt from holding you properly in a crash.
Always confirm the seat and seat-back are locked in position by rocking them before you drive. (F-WP03-008)
Steps:
1. Sit in the seat and pull up the lever under the front edge of the seat cushion. (F-WP03-008)
2. Slide the seat to a position where you can fully press the pedals, then release the lever.
3. Rock the seat forward and back to make sure it is locked in place.
4. Pull the lever on the side of the seat-back and lean to a comfortable, upright angle.
5. Release the lever to lock the seat-back, then rock it to confirm it caught. (F-WP03-008)
6. Pull straight up on each front head restraint, or press its side button and push down, until level with the middle of your head. (F-WP03-011)
7. Push the lever under the steering column down to unlock the wheel. (F-WP03-009)
8. Move the wheel up or down until you can see all the gauges clearly, then push the lever back up to lock it.
9. Try moving the wheel to confirm it is locked in place.
10. Flip the tab under the inside mirror to day mode, then tilt it for a clear view out the back window.
11. Turn the ignition to the ON position and move the mirror selector switch to the driver's side. (F-WP03-010)
12. Press the edges of the mirror adjustment switch until that mirror shows a clear view, then repeat for the passenger's side mirror.
13. Return the mirror selector switch to its center, off position so the setting is not changed by accident.
14. Fasten your seat belt, then pull the lap portion low and snug and the shoulder portion across your chest. (F-WP03-012)
15. If the belt crosses your neck or arm, squeeze the two buttons on the shoulder anchor and slide it to a better height. (F-WP03-013)
How you know it worked: You can fully press the pedals and see every gauge, both outside mirrors show a clear view without you leaning, and the shoulder belt rests across the middle of your chest, not your neck.
Common mistakes: Adjusting mirrors, seat, or the steering wheel after you start driving. Leaving the seat too close to the steering wheel. Forgetting to lock the seat-back angle lever. Leaving the mirror selector switch on instead of centering it, which can let the setting drift.
