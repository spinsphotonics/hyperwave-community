Procedure ID: P-WP05-4
Chapter: 9
Title: Set up Refresh Mode (recline the front seats flat to rest)
Skill level: 1 = anyone
Time: 5 minutes for a first-timer
Tools:
- None required
Parts and supplies:
- None required
Safety warnings:
- Always park the vehicle, set the parking brake, and turn off the engine before reclining the seats this far — this is not a driving position.
- Always reinstall both front head restraints and lock both seats securely upright again before anyone drives or rides in the car.
- Do not drive the vehicle, or let anyone ride in a moving vehicle, with the front seats in this reclined position — seat belts are not designed to protect a reclined occupant.
- Do not let a child use this mode unsupervised; there is a fold-down mechanism and lever that could pinch fingers.
Steps:
1. Park the car, set the parking brake, and turn off the engine.
2. Recline the rear seat-backs as far back as they will go.
3. Remove both front head restraints and store them somewhere secure so they do not roll around.
4. Using the seat slide lever, move both front seat cushions as far forward as they will go.
5. Pull up the seat-back angle adjustment lever on each front seat.
6. Pivot each front seat-back backward until it is level with the rear seat cushions, creating one large cushioned area.
How you know it worked: The front seat-backs lie flat and level with the rear seat cushions, forming one continuous cushioned surface, and each seat-back stays down on its own without you holding it.
Common mistakes:
- Forgetting to remove the head restraints first, which stops the seat-back from lying fully flat.
- Letting go of the seat-back too quickly when returning it upright — it can spring up faster than expected and should be controlled by hand.
- Forgetting to relock the seats and reinstall the head restraints before driving again.
To reverse (return to normal driving position): Hold the seat-back and pivot it back upright (control the motion by hand so it doesn't come up too fast), reinstall both head restraints, and lock both seats securely before driving.
