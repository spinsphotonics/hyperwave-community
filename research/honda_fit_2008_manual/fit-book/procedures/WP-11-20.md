Chapter: 32 (Lights and bulbs, wipers, fuses)
Title: Replace the wiper blades
Skill level: 1 (anyone)
Time: 15 minutes for a first-timer (both front blades and the rear blade)

Tools:
- None required in most cases

Parts and supplies:
- Front wiper blades: driver's side 24 inches, passenger's side 16 inches [Source: S-026, Tier 3, medium-high confidence, corroborated by a second independent aggregator]
- Rear wiper blade (models with a rear wiper): 14 inches [Source: S-026, Tier 3, medium-high confidence]
- Check blade condition at least every 6 months and replace at the first sign of cracking, hardening, or streaking, per the manual [Source: S-001/S-023]

Safety warnings:
- Do not open the hood while the wiper arms are raised off the glass; the hood can hit and damage the arms.
- Always handle a raised wiper arm carefully; do not let it snap down onto bare glass without a blade attached.

Steps:

Front wiper blades:
1. Raise the wiper arms off the windshield, lifting the driver's side first, then the passenger's side.
2. Press and hold the lock tab on the blade assembly.
3. Slide the blade assembly toward the lock tab until it releases from the wiper arm.
4. Grasp the tabbed end of the old blade and pull firmly until it comes out of its holder.
5. If the new blade has no plastic or metal reinforcement strip, move the strip from the old blade into the new one's edge slots.
6. Make sure the new blade's rubber tabs fit into each notch of the reinforcement strip.
7. Slide the new blade into the wiper arm until it is engaged along its full length.
8. Lower the wiper arm back down onto the windshield.
9. Repeat steps 2 through 8 for the other front wiper arm.

Rear wiper blade (if equipped):
10. Raise the rear wiper arm off the rear glass.
11. Slide the old blade out of the wiper arm.
12. Move the reinforcement strip to the new blade if it does not already have one.
13. Slide the new blade assembly onto the wiper arm until it locks in place.
14. Lower the rear wiper arm back down against the glass.

How you know it worked: All wiper arms sit flat against the glass with the new blades locked in place, and a test run of the wipers wipes cleanly with no streaks, chatter, or missed spots.

Common mistakes:
- Forgetting to transfer the metal or plastic reinforcement strip into a new blade that ships without one.
- Letting a raised, unblocked wiper arm snap down onto bare glass.
- Opening the hood with the wiper arms still raised.
