Procedure ID: P-WP03-1
Chapter: 4
Title: Open the hood
Skill level: 1 = anyone
Time: 3 minutes for a first-timer
Tools:
None required
Parts and supplies:
None
Safety warnings:
Always park on level, flat ground and set the parking brake before opening the hood.
Always let the engine cool before opening the hood if you just finished driving.
Do not open the hood if you see steam or spray coming from underneath it. Wait until it stops. (F-WP03-005)
Always make sure the support rod is properly seated in its hole before letting go of the hood.
Steps:
1. Park on level ground, turn off the engine, and set the parking brake.
2. Reach under the lower-left corner of the dashboard and pull the hood release handle. (F-WP03-005)
3. The hood will pop up slightly but stay held by a second, hidden latch.
4. Walk to the front of the car and slide your fingers under the hood's front edge, near the center.
5. Feel for the second latch handle and push it to release the hood. (F-WP03-005)
6. Lift the hood the rest of the way open.
7. Pull the metal support rod free from its clip under the hood.
8. Fit the tip of the support rod into the matching hole on the underside of the hood. (F-WP03-005)
9. Let go of the hood slowly to make sure the rod is holding its weight.
To close the hood:
10. Lift the hood slightly to free the support rod from its hole, then clip the rod back in place.
11. Lower the hood to about one foot above the front fenders, then let it drop the rest of the way. (F-WP03-005)
12. Push down lightly on the hood to confirm it caught and is fully latched.
How you know it worked: The hood stays open on its own, supported only by the rod, with no need to hold it up; when closed, it sits flush and does not move when you push down on it.
Common mistakes: Only pulling the interior release and not finding the second hood latch, so the hood will not lift. Letting go of an open hood before the support rod is seated. Slamming the hood shut from a low height instead of letting it drop the last foot on its own.
