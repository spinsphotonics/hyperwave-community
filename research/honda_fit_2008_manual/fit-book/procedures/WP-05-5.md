Procedure ID: P-WP05-5
Chapter: 10
Title: Refuel the car
Skill level: 1 = anyone
Time: 5 minutes for a first-timer
Tools:
- None required
Parts and supplies:
- Regular unleaded gasoline, 87 AKI (pump octane) or higher (Fact ID F-WP05-035)
Safety warnings:
- Gasoline is highly flammable and explosive. You can be burned or seriously injured while handling it.
- Always turn off the engine before you start fueling.
- Always keep heat, sparks, and open flame away from the fuel fill area.
- Always handle fuel outdoors only, never in an enclosed space.
- Always wipe up any spilled fuel immediately.
- Do not try to "top off" the tank once the pump nozzle clicks off on its own — leave room for the fuel to expand as its temperature changes.
Steps:
1. Park with the driver's side of the car closest to the fuel pump.
2. Turn off the engine.
3. From outside the car, open the fuel fill door by pulling its notched edge.
4. Remove the fuel fill cap slowly — a hissing sound as tank pressure escapes is normal.
5. Fit the cap's tether attachment into the slit on the fuel fill door so the cap hangs there while you fuel.
6. Insert the pump nozzle and select 87 AKI regular unleaded (or higher) fuel.
7. Fill until the nozzle clicks off automatically, then stop — do not try to add more.
8. If the nozzle clicks off repeatedly before the tank feels full, try a different pump; this can mean a problem with the fuel vapor recovery system, and a dealer visit may be needed if it keeps happening.
9. Screw the fuel fill cap back on until it clicks at least once.
10. Close the fuel fill door.
How you know it worked: The pump handles cleanly, the cap clicks at least once when tightened, and the fuel gauge needle moves up. No "CHECK FUEL CAP" message or malfunction indicator lamp appears on your next drive.
Common mistakes:
- Not tightening the fuel cap until it clicks, which can trigger a "CHECK FUEL CAP" message and the malfunction indicator lamp (Fact ID F-WP05-039) — this can take several days of driving to clear even after you fix the cap.
- Trying to "top off" the tank past the automatic click-off, risking a spill and leaving no room for fuel expansion.
- Forgetting to close the fuel fill door before driving away.
