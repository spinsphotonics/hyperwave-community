Procedure ID: P-WP11-2
Chapter: 25 (Air filter and cabin filter)
Title: Replace the engine air filter
Skill level: 1 (anyone — no jacking, no fluids, a few clips)
Time: 15 minutes for a first-timer

Tools:
- Flat-tip (flathead) screwdriver, to help pop the housing clips if they are stiff
- Phillips screwdriver (some housings use a screw instead of a clip)

Parts and supplies:
- Engine air filter element, Honda genuine part 17220-PWA-505 (2007-2008 Fit fitment confirmed on the parts page) — this number replaced an older part, 17220-PWA-J10 [Source: S-027]
- Replace every 15,000 miles (24,000 km) if you drive mostly in dusty conditions, per the maintenance schedule; otherwise follow the Maintenance Minder message [Source: S-001/S-023]

Safety warnings:
- Always let the engine cool before opening the hood if it has just been running — parts near the filter box can be hot.
- Do not start the engine with the air filter housing open or the element removed.

Steps:
1. Open the hood and find the air filter housing, a black plastic box on the driver's side of the engine bay.
2. Release the clips holding the two halves of the housing together (there are usually four).
3. Lift the top half of the housing up and out of the way.
4. Note which way the old filter element sits before you remove it.
5. Lift out the old, dirty filter element.
6. Wipe out any dust or leaves from inside the housing with a dry rag.
7. Place the new filter element in the same orientation as the old one.
8. Close the top half of the housing and press down until every clip snaps shut.
9. Check that all clips are fully closed before starting the engine.

How you know it worked: The housing halves sit flush together, every clip is snapped closed, and the engine starts and idles normally.

Common mistakes:
- Forcing a clip and cracking the plastic housing.
- Installing the new filter upside down or backward.
- Leaving one clip unlatched, which lets unfiltered air leak past the filter.
