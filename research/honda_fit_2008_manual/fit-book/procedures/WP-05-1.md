Procedure ID: P-WP05-1
Chapter: 9
Title: Set up Utility Mode (fold the rear Magic Seat down flat for cargo)
Skill level: 1 = anyone
Time: 5 minutes for a first-timer
Tools:
- None required
Parts and supplies:
- None required
Safety warnings:
- Always remove any items from the rear seat before you fold it.
- Always make sure there is nothing on the floor before you fold the seat-back down.
- Always keep cargo below the bottom of the windows so it does not block the side curtain airbags.
- Always tie down or secure loose cargo so it cannot fly forward during hard braking.
- Do not put heavy items directly on a folded seat-back.
- Do not carry a passenger on a rear seat you have folded down; each side must be returned fully upright, locked, and belted before anyone sits there.
Steps:
1. Decide which side(s) of the 60/40 rear seat you are folding — driver's side, passenger's side, or both.
2. Remove everything from that rear seat.
3. Move the matching front seat as far forward as it will go.
4. Lock the rear outer head restraint on that side in its lowest position.
5. If folding the driver's side, also set the rear-center head restraint to its lowest position.
6. If folding the driver's side, pull the latch plate to release the center seat belt from its detachable anchor and let the belt retract into the ceiling holder; store the buckle there too.
7. Pull up the release lever on the outside of that seat-back.
8. Fold the seat-back forward until it lies flat.
9. Repeat steps 2-8 for the other side if you need the full-width flat floor.
10. Load cargo, keeping it below window height and tied down.
How you know it worked: The seat-back lies flat and level, creating a flat load floor from the tailgate forward; it does not spring back up when you let go.
Common mistakes:
- Forgetting to move the front seat forward first, so the folded seat-back does not lie flat.
- Leaving the head restraint up, which blocks the seat-back from folding flush.
- Skipping the detachable-anchor release on the driver's side, which can strain or damage the center seat belt.
- Not checking the passenger airbag-off indicator after returning the seat to the upright position (a folded-then-restored seat can confuse the front passenger's weight sensor).
