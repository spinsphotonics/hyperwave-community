Chapter: 32 (Lights and bulbs, wipers, fuses)
Title: Replace tail light and brake light bulbs
Skill level: 1 (anyone)
Time: 15 minutes for a first-timer (rear light assembly); 10 minutes for the high-mount brake light

Tools:
- Flat-tip (flathead) screwdriver
- A cloth to protect the paint edge while prying the cover

Parts and supplies:
- Rear light assembly bulbs: the manual lists these as 21 W (stop/taillight combined, dual-filament), 21 W (back-up light), and 21 W (turn signal), by wattage rather than a part number [Source: S-001/S-023, Tier 1]
- High-mount brake light bulb: 5 W per the Specifications table [Source: S-001/S-023, Tier 1]
- Common aftermarket cross-reference numbers reported for this generation's rear lighting: 7443 for the stop/taillight, back-up, and turn signal positions, and T10 for side-marker/license-plate-style small bulbs — confirm against your old bulb's markings before buying, since this cross-reference is Tier 3, not from Honda directly [Source: S-025]

Safety warnings:
- Do not force the trim tool or screwdriver against painted body panels without a cloth underneath; you can scratch the paint.
- Always confirm which of the bulbs in the assembly is actually burned out before you disturb the others.

Steps:

Rear light assembly (tailgate side):
1. Open the tailgate.
2. Place a cloth on the edge of the light assembly cover to protect the paint.
3. Pry the cover off carefully at the notch on its middle edge with the flat-tip screwdriver.
4. Identify which bulb is out: stop/taillight, back-up light, turn signal, or taillight.
5. Turn that bulb's socket one-quarter turn counterclockwise and remove it from the assembly.
6. Pull the burned-out bulb straight out of its socket.
7. Push the new bulb straight into the socket until it bottoms out.
8. Turn on the lights (or press the brake pedal, for a stop/taillight bulb) to confirm it works.
9. Turn the socket clockwise back into the light assembly until it locks.
10. Reinstall the light assembly cover.

High-mount brake light (rear window):
11. Open the tailgate.
12. Remove the light assembly cover by pushing in the tabs on both sides and pulling it off.
13. Turn the socket one-quarter turn counterclockwise and remove it from the assembly.
14. Pull the old bulb straight out of its socket.
15. Push the new bulb straight into the socket until it bottoms out.
16. Put the socket back into the light assembly and turn it clockwise to lock it.
17. Press the brake pedal to confirm the new bulb lights up.
18. Push the cover back onto the light assembly until it locks in place.

How you know it worked: The specific light you replaced (taillight, brake light, turn signal, back-up light, or high-mount brake light) turns on when tested, and both covers are fully seated and locked.

Common mistakes:
- Guessing which bulb is out instead of testing each function (brakes, turn signal, reverse) before opening the cover.
- Not seating the socket's quarter-turn lock fully, letting moisture into the housing.
- Prying the cover without a cloth and scratching the paint around it.
