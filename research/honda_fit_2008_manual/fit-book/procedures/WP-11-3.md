Procedure ID: P-WP11-3
Chapter: 25 (Air filter and cabin filter)
Title: Replace the cabin (dust and pollen) air filter
Skill level: 1 (anyone — no tools required on most cars)
Time: 10 minutes for a first-timer

Tools:
- None required in most cases; a flat-tip screwdriver can help release a stiff glove box stop

Parts and supplies:
- Cabin air filter, Honda genuine part 80291-SAA-J01 (2007-2008 Fit fitment confirmed on the parts page) — later superseded by part 80291-SAA-316 [Source: S-027]
- Replace every 15,000 miles (24,000 km) if you drive mostly in urban, sooty air, or when airflow from the heater/AC feels weaker than usual; otherwise follow the Maintenance Minder message [Source: S-001/S-023]
- Note: this filter is on models with air conditioning per the owner's manual

Safety warnings:
- Do not force the glove box past its stops; you can break the hinge or the stop tabs.

Steps:
1. Open the glove box fully.
2. Squeeze both sides of the glove box inward to release the stop tabs.
3. Let the glove box swing down out of the way.
4. Find the filter tray in the housing behind the glove box opening.
5. Pinch the two clips on the tray's left and right sides and pull the tray out.
6. Slide the old filter out of the tray.
7. Look at the arrow printed on the new filter's frame.
8. Slide the new filter into the tray with the arrow pointing down.
9. Push the tray back into the housing until both clips click.
10. Swing the glove box back up and press it closed.

How you know it worked: The glove box closes and latches normally, and air from the vents feels stronger than before if the old filter was clogged.

Common mistakes:
- Installing the filter with the arrow pointing the wrong way, which can reduce airflow.
- Not seating both tray clips, so the tray rattles or falls loose later.
