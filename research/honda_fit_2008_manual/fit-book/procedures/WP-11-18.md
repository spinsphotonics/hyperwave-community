Procedure ID: P-WP11-18
Chapter: 32 (Lights and bulbs, wipers, fuses)
Title: Replace a headlight bulb
Skill level: 1 (anyone)
Time: 15 minutes for a first-timer

Tools:
- Flat-tip (flathead) screwdriver, to remove the inner fender holding clip

Parts and supplies:
- Halogen headlight bulb, type HB2 per the owner's manual's own Specifications table (this bulb family is commonly cross-sold under the H4/9003 name at parts stores; confirm the cross-reference against your own bulb's markings before buying) [Source: S-001/S-023, Tier 1 for "HB2"; S-025, Tier 3, for the common H4 cross-reference]

Safety warnings:
- Do not touch the glass part of a halogen bulb with bare skin; oil from your fingers can make it overheat and shatter when lit. Handle it by its base only.
- Always clean the glass with denatured alcohol and a clean cloth before installing it, if you accidentally touch it.
- Always let the engine cool if it was just running, since you will reach in near the engine bay.

Steps:
1. To reach the driver's side bulb, start the engine, turn the wheel fully right, then turn the engine off.
2. To reach the passenger's side bulb, instead turn the wheel fully left.
3. Use the flat-tip screwdriver to remove the holding clip from the inner fender.
4. Pull the inner fender cover back to expose the back of the headlight.
5. Remove the electrical connector from the bulb by pulling it straight back.
6. Remove the rubber weather seal by pulling on its tab.
7. Unclip the end of the hold-down wire from its slot.
8. Pivot the hold-down wire out of the way and remove the old bulb.
9. Install the new bulb, holding it by its base, making sure its tabs sit in their slots.
10. Pivot the hold-down wire back into place and clip its end into the slot.
11. Check the wire's installation through the inspection window on the headlight assembly.
12. Install the rubber weather seal back over the back of the headlight assembly.
13. Push the electrical connector onto the new bulb until it is securely connected.
14. Reinstall the inner fender cover and the holding clip, pressing the clip's center to lock it.
15. Turn on the headlights to confirm the new bulb works.

How you know it worked: The headlight comes on and looks similarly bright and evenly aimed as the other side, and the inner fender cover and clip are back in place.

Common mistakes:
- Touching the new bulb's glass with bare fingers.
- Forgetting to check the hold-down wire through the inspection window, leaving the bulb loose.
- Not fully locking the fender clip, so it rattles or falls out later.
- Buying a bulb that does not actually match the HB2 fitment because the cross-reference wasn't checked.
