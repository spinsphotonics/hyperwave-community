Procedure ID: P-WP11-16
Chapter: 30 (Coolant, transmission fluid, brake fluid, power steering, washer fluid)
Title: Check and change the manual transmission fluid
Skill level: 3 (needs jack stands and care for the drain step; the level check alone is skill level 1)
Time: 15 minutes for a level check; 40 minutes for a full drain and refill

Tools:
- Jack and 2 jack stands, or ramps (see P-WP11-22), for the drain step only
- Wrench or socket to fit the filler bolt and the drain bolt (sizes UNVERIFIED — check yours, see Appendix C)
- Drain pan
- Fluid pump or a long-neck funnel, since the fill point is checked and filled from the same bolt hole

Parts and supplies:
- Honda Manual Transmission Fluid (MTF); if unavailable, an SAE 10W-30 or 10W-40 motor oil with the API Certification Seal reading "FOR GASOLINE ENGINES" can be used temporarily, but switch to Honda MTF as soon as convenient — motor oil lacks the right additives and can cause stiffer shifting [Source: S-001/S-023, Tier 1]
- Approximate capacity is on the order of 1.6-1.7 US qt (1.5-1.6 L); the exact change-vs-total split is not fully confirmed this pass — fill to the correct level at the bolt hole rather than by a fixed poured amount, and check Appendix B for the verified number [Source: S-001/S-023, S-024, medium confidence]
- New washer for the filler bolt [Source: S-001/S-023]

Safety warnings:
- Do not work under a car held up only by a jack. Use jack stands or ramps.
- Always check and change the fluid with the transmission at normal operating temperature and the car on level ground, per the manual.
- Do not overfill; fluid should only reach the edge of the bolt hole, not spill out under pressure.
- Always clean up any spilled fluid immediately.

Steps:

Level check only:
1. Drive the car until the transmission reaches normal operating temperature.
2. Park on level ground and shut off the engine.
3. Remove the transmission filler bolt.
4. Carefully feel inside the bolt hole with a clean finger for the fluid level.
5. If fluid does not reach the edge of the hole, add Honda MTF until it just starts to run out.
6. Put a new washer on the filler bolt and reinstall it, tightened securely.

Full drain and refill, continuing from a cool transmission:
7. Raise the car and support it on jack stands.
8. Place the drain pan under the transmission's drain bolt.
9. Remove the drain bolt and let the old fluid fully drain.
10. Reinstall the drain bolt with a new washer, tightened securely.
11. Lower the car back to level ground.
12. Remove the filler bolt and add fresh Honda MTF through a pump or long funnel until it reaches the edge of the hole.
13. Reinstall the filler bolt with a new washer, tightened securely.
14. Check for leaks at both bolts after a short drive.

How you know it worked: Fluid sits right at the edge of the filler bolt hole (neither dripping out under pressure nor clearly low), there are no leaks at either bolt after driving, and shifting feels normal, not stiff or grindy.

Common mistakes:
- Checking the level with the transmission cold, which can give a misleading reading.
- Forgetting a new washer on either bolt, causing a slow leak.
- Using plain motor oil long-term instead of switching back to Honda MTF.
- Overfilling by continuing to pump fluid in after it starts running back out of the hole.
