Procedure ID: P-WP11-11
Chapter: 28 (Battery: testing, cleaning, replacing, jump starting)
Title: Replace the battery
Skill level: 2 (needs basic tools)
Time: 20 minutes for a first-timer

Tools:
- Wrench or socket to fit the battery terminal clamp bolts and the hold-down bracket bolt (size UNVERIFIED — check yours, see Appendix C)
- Wire brush or a terminal-cleaning tool
- Battery carrying strap or gloves rated for lifting

Parts and supplies:
- Replacement 12 V battery, group size 151R (the original-equipment size for the 2008 Fit; group 51R is a commonly reported upgrade with more cold-cranking amps, but it is a noticeably tighter physical fit and some owners report needing to modify the battery tray) [Source: S-026, Tier 3, medium confidence — battery group size was not independently confirmed by a Tier 1/2 source this pass]
- Your 5-digit radio anti-theft code, written down before you start (see your Honda paperwork), since disconnecting the battery can lock the radio [Source: S-001/S-023]

Safety warnings:
- Always wear eye protection and protective clothing, or have a skilled technician do this, per the manual — the battery gives off explosive hydrogen gas.
- Do not smoke or bring sparks or flames near the battery.
- Always disconnect the negative (-) cable first, and reconnect it last, to reduce the risk of a spark shorting the vehicle's electrical system.
- Always wash your hands after handling the battery, posts, or terminals; they contain lead and lead compounds.
- Do not drop the battery; it is heavy and can crack, spilling acid.

Steps:
1. Turn off the engine and every accessory.
2. Write down or confirm you have your radio's 5-digit anti-theft code.
3. Loosen and remove the negative (-) cable clamp first, and set it aside so it cannot touch the terminal.
4. Loosen and remove the positive (+) cable clamp next.
5. Remove the bolt or clamp holding the battery hold-down bracket in place.
6. Lift the old battery straight out, using a strap or careful grip; it is heavy.
7. Set the new battery in the tray in the same orientation as the old one.
8. Reinstall the hold-down bracket and its bolt.
9. Reconnect the positive (+) cable clamp first this time, and tighten it.
10. Reconnect the negative (-) cable clamp last, and tighten it.
11. Start the engine to confirm it runs normally.
12. Turn on the radio and enter your 5-digit code if "ENTER CODE" appears.
13. Reset the clock if it lost its setting.

How you know it worked: The engine starts normally, the radio accepts your code (or never asked for one), and the clock is reset if needed.

Common mistakes:
- Disconnecting the positive cable first, which raises the risk of a short if a tool touches metal.
- Forgetting the radio code before disconnecting the battery, then having to look it up afterward.
- Not securing the hold-down bracket, letting the battery shift while driving.
- Buying a battery that is a tight or loose physical fit for the tray without checking dimensions first.
