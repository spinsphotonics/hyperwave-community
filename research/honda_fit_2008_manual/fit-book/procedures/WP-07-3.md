Procedure ID: P-WP07-3
Chapter: 16
Title: The car is overheating
Skill level: 2 (needs basic tools — gloves or a heavy cloth, and coolant)
Time: 30-60 minutes for a first-timer, including cool-down time
Tools:
Gloves or a large heavy cloth (to handle the hot radiator cap)
Parts and supplies:
Engine coolant (proper mixture) or plain water in an emergency — Fact ID F-WP07-016
Safety warnings:
Do not open the hood if you see steam or spray coming from under it. Wait until it stops.
Steam and spray from an overheated engine can scald you badly. Stay clear until things cool.
Always let the engine and radiator cool down before removing the radiator cap.
Do not remove the radiator cap while the engine is hot. Hot coolant can spray out and scald you.
Do not keep driving with the high temperature indicator on. It can cause serious engine damage.
Steps:
1. Safely pull to the side of the road, away from traffic.
2. Shift to neutral (manual) or Park (automatic), and set the parking brake.
3. Turn off all accessories and turn on the hazard warning lights.
4. If you see steam or spray from under the hood, turn off the engine right away.
5. Wait until no more steam or spray is visible before you open the hood.
6. If there is no steam or spray, you may leave the engine running and watch the indicator.
7. If the indicator goes off within a few minutes, wait for it, then continue driving.
8. If the indicator stays on, turn off the engine and let it sit.
9. Look for an obvious coolant leak, such as a split hose. Everything is still very hot.
10. If you find a leak, do not drive again until it is repaired.
11. If you see no leak, check the coolant level in the radiator reserve tank.
12. Add coolant to the reserve tank if the level is below the MIN mark.
13. If the reserve tank was empty, let the engine and radiator cool fully before going further.
14. Using gloves or a heavy cloth, turn the radiator cap counterclockwise to the first stop only.
15. Let the pressure release fully, then push down on the cap and turn it off.
16. Start the engine and set the temperature dial to maximum heat.
17. Add coolant to the radiator up to the base of the filler neck.
18. If you have no coolant mix on hand, plain water is an acceptable emergency substitute.
19. Put the radiator cap back on tightly, then run the engine and watch the indicator.
20. If the indicator blinks or comes on again, stop driving. The engine needs repair.
21. If the temperature stays normal, top the reserve tank to MAX and close its cap tightly.
22. Have the cooling system drained and refilled with the correct coolant mixture soon.
How you know it worked: The high temperature indicator stays off during normal driving, the reserve tank sits between MIN and MAX, and you see no new coolant leaks.
Common mistakes: Opening the hood while steam is still visible. Removing the radiator cap on a hot engine instead of waiting to cool down. Continuing to drive after the indicator stays on instead of pulling over. Forgetting to have the correct coolant mixture put back in after adding plain water.
Source IDs: S-001 (F-WP07-015 through F-WP07-017)
