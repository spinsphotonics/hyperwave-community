## Top-line findings — the two claims the calling task asked about by name

**1. SCCA harness/roll-bar rules (F-WP19-001, 003, 004, 006): HOLD UP, verified against the
actual rulebook text, essentially verbatim.** Confirmed directly from Section 3.3.1/3.3.2 of
the re-fetched PDF: lap belts are required in all cars; non-factory upper-body restraints
(aftermarket harnesses) may ONLY be used in open cars/open-top-position cars, and only with a
roll structure meeting Appendix C or GCR 9.4; roll bars/cages are "strongly recommended" but not
mandatory in Street/Street Touring/Street Prepared/Street Modified, becoming mandatory only in
AM/BM/CM/FM Modified classes and open cars in Prepared/DM/EM. A harness bar rule was also
confirmed present (2-point C-type / 4-bolt truss restrictions). **These claims are accurate as
written and required no changes.**

**2. NASA roll-bar trigger — open-car vs. closed-car, NOT speed-based (F-WP19-010): HOLDS UP,
confirmed verbatim.** Section 11.4.7 of the re-fetched NASA CCR PDF states exactly: "All open
cars should have a roll bar installed to help protect the occupant(s) from injury during a
roll-over." No lap-time, speed, or run-group qualifier appears anywhere in Section 11 (the
HPDE/Time Trial vehicle-technical-regulations section). This directly confirms the fact card's
claim that a closed-body base 2008 Fit never needs a roll bar for NASA HPDE/Time Trial at any
speed or pace — **correct, and correctly identified in the file as correcting the research
plan's own working assumption.**

**3. One real error found and fixed this pass: F-WP19-005 (Appendix C roll bar height/head
restraint spec) misstates when a head restraint is required.** The card claimed a head
restraint is required "if the bar sits within 6.0 in (152.4 mm) of the helmet" — but the actual
rulebook text ties the head-restraint requirement specifically to "Modified Category Specials
(Section 18.4)" under a *different* 2.0 in exception; the "6.0 in" figure that does appear in
Appendix C instead governs an unrelated two-driver-car allowance. This is a genuine value FAIL
on one sub-claim within an otherwise-accurate fact card (the helmet-height and material/
fabrication requirements in the same card were all independently confirmed correct). **Per the
plan's rule, this has been downgraded to UNVERIFIED/Confidence: medium in `facts/WP-19.md`
directly, with the correct rule explained in its place** — see the diff in that file.

**Pass rate: 26/26 fact cards checked. 1 FAIL (F-WP19-005, partial — fixed by edit), 0
YEAR-FAIL.** 13 Tier 2 rulebook facts are CORROBORATED by direct primary-source re-read; the
Tier 4 weight/community facts (9 cards) remain honestly SINGLE/low-confidence, matching a
confirmed-still-blocked source; the Tier 3 aero facts (3 cards) are reasonably corroborated.
