## Flat-tow and jacking-point deep check (per explicit instruction)

Both were re-derived independently from the freshly re-fetched PDF text, not just re-read
against the researcher's own quotes, and both hold up completely:

- **Flat-tow rule**: the manual genuinely does NOT support towing this car with all four wheels
  on the ground for any distance, for either transmission. It offers exactly three methods:
  flatbed ("best"), wheel-lift with front wheels raised ("acceptable"), and a damage-only
  front-wheels-on-ground contingency capped at 50 miles / 35 mph. The automatic-transmission
  prep sequence is genuinely the trap the original card flagged it as: D for 5 seconds, then N,
  then a 3-minute idle, then engine off — skipping this step is explicitly what the manual's own
  "Improper towing preparation will damage the transmission" warning is about. This matches
  facts/WP-07.md F-WP07-025 through F-WP07-030 exactly, word for word.
- **Jacking points**: the manual identifies the jacking point only by a molded body mark and an
  illustration, with no numeric measurement — exactly as the card states. One OCR/text-extraction
  artifact worth noting for the record: the sentence "It is pointed to by a mark molded into the
  underside of the body" is immediately followed in the extracted text by the stray tag
  "(Sport model)" — consistent with the same label-bleed pattern seen throughout this extraction
  (e.g., unrelated TPMS and cruise-control passages also pick up stray "On Sport model" tags from
  nearby diagram captions). This is very likely a text-extraction artifact from an adjacent
  diagram caption, not a real base-vs-Sport difference in jacking-point *location* — the
  wheel-cover-vs-center-cap difference (F-WP07-010) is the only base-vs-Sport difference the
  manual's own prose actually names in this procedure. The original fact card's own hedge
  ("the manual does not give a different numeric jacking point location") already anticipated
  this ambiguity correctly and needs no change, but the book's fresh jacking-point diagram should
  not be drawn as if the base and Sport jacking points differ, absent a clearer source.
