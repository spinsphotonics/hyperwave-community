# Verification: WP-15 Modding Ground Rules — 2008 Honda Fit (GD3)

Verifier method: independent re-fetch of every primary source via `curl` (real browser
User-Agent), text-extracted for PDFs via `pdftotext -layout` and for HTML via a tag-stripping
Python pass, per `queue/decisions_needed.md` ADAPT-2. No source in this WP required the
`r.jina.ai` fallback — every URL cited resolved directly to real, live content on a plain
curl fetch this pass. The two most safety/legally-load-bearing citations named in the task
(the CARB EO lookup URL and the EPA fact sheet) were both independently re-fetched and their
exact quoted language checked word-for-word against the fact cards.

## Top-line findings

**The CARB EO URL and the EPA fact sheet are both real, live, and accurately quoted.**
`https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2021-11/epafactsheetreaftermarketddsandtampering.pdf`
is a genuine EPA-hosted PDF (2.8MB, "FACT SHEET / Clean Air Act / Vehicle Aftermarket / Defeat
Devices and Tampering / March 2020"); every direct quote WP-15 pulled from it (the CAA
203(a)(3)(B) defeat-device text, the 203(a)(3)(A) companion tampering text, the 3-part
"reasonable basis" test, the "Tampering can void manufacturer warranties and insurance
agreements" line, and the CAA citations in the footnotes) matches the real PDF text
character-for-character (allowing for a few obvious OCR artifacts in the PDF itself, e.g.
"CARS" for "CARB" — not WP-15's error, the source PDF's own OCR layer has this typo).
`https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/aftermarket-parts-lookup` returned HTTP 200 on a direct fetch and its
five listed categories (Performance & Add-on Parts, Catalytic Converters, Motorcycles,
Auxiliary Fuel Tanks, Alternate Fuel Retrofit Systems) match the card exactly. CARB's EO
overview page's "valid for production during the specified model year..." quote (F-WP15-008)
also matches verbatim.

**One minor wording overreach found, not rising to FAIL:** F-WP15-004 states the $48,192
penalty applies "per violating vehicle, engine, or defeat device for a manufacturer or
dealer." The real PDF text is more precise: the $48,192 figure for manufacturers/dealers is
stated as "per violative vehicle or engine" (no "or defeat device" in that clause) — the "or
... defeat device" wording only appears in the PDF's second clause, covering the $4,819
figure for "any person other than a manufacturer or dealer." The dollar amounts themselves
($48,192 / $4,819) and which amount applies to which class of person are both correct; only
the appended object list for the manufacturer clause is slightly over-copied from the second
clause. This does not change any number a reader would rely on, so it is recorded here as a
verifier note rather than downgraded to UNVERIFIED. No edit made to facts/WP-15.md for this.

**Internal cross-references (Section D) all check out.** F-WP15-015/016/017 point to
`procedures/WP-11-1.md`, `WP-11-4.md`, `WP-11-5.md`, `WP-11-6.md` through `WP-11-9.md` — all
six files exist and contain the specific values the cards cite (29 lbf-ft/39 N·m oil drain
torque in WP-11-1; 80 lbf-ft/108 N·m wheel-nut torque in WP-11-5; "drum" brakes confirmed in
WP-11-8). F-WP15-018's gap claim — "no WP-11 procedure card covers alignment" — is also
independently confirmed: `procedures/` contains exactly WP-11-1.md through WP-11-23.md (23
files, verified by directory listing), and none of the 23 mention alignment.

**Pass rate: 18/18 fact cards PASS, 18/18 YEAR-OK, 0 FAIL, 0 YEAR-FAIL.** All Tier 2 facts are
sufficiently sourced on their own per the plan's Tier rule; the one Tier 3 source
(Progressive, Section C) is correctly corroborated by a Tier 2 source (EPA's own
warranty/insurance line) per the plan's "Tier 3 plus one other source of any tier" rule.
F-WP15-018 is correctly self-flagged as a documented gap, not an invented value, and needs no
status change.
