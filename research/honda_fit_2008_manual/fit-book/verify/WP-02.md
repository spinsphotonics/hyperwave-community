# Verification: WP-02 Recalls, TSBs, and Known Defects — 2008 Honda Fit (GD3)

Verifier method: independent re-fetch of every primary source via `curl` (real browser
User-Agent) and, where blocked, the `https://r.jina.ai/<url>` reader proxy (per
`queue/decisions_needed.md` ADAPT-2). For every NHTSA recall, the claim was additionally
checked against NHTSA's own structured JSON API
(`api.nhtsa.gov/recalls/recallsByVehicle?make=honda&model=fit&modelYear=2008` and
`api.nhtsa.gov/recalls/campaignNumber?campaignNumber=...`), which is the authoritative,
machine-readable version of the same government record the plan asks us to check against.
Two TSB PDFs were read directly from `static.nhtsa.gov` (primary Honda documents, not
snippets). Community (Tier 4) threads on `fitfreak.net` are blocked outright at the network
level for this session (Cloudflare ASN block, confirmed both via direct curl and via the
r.jina.ai proxy — this is a genuine dead end, not a fixable config issue), so those 22 URLs
were checked for real existence, correct thread title, and topical/generational content match
via independent WebSearch queries seeded on the literal thread ID and title (not the
researcher's WP-02 phrasing), consistent with the ADAPT-1 fallback tier for confirmed dead-end
domains.

## Top-line findings (read this first)

**1. THE RECALL LIST IS INCOMPLETE — 3 real, currently-active NHTSA recalls for the 2008 Fit
are missing from WP-02.** NHTSA's own API (`recallsByVehicle`) returns **8** distinct recall
campaigns for model year 2008 Honda Fit, not the 5 WP-02 found. All 5 that WP-02 found are
confirmed correct. The 3 missing ones are all airbag-related and two are Takata-inflator
campaigns of real severity:

| Missing campaign | Date | Component | Units (all models) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **07V549000** | recall began Dec 12, 2007 | Frontal airbag — ODS/OPDS wire harness corrosion from road salt, can defeat occupant-detection suppression | 34,300 | Limited to vehicles sold/registered in 21 listed salt-belt states + DC. Explicitly lists MY2008 Fit. |
| **16V344000** | recall began June 27, 2016 | Passenger frontal airbag inflator (Takata) — original/OE-assembled inflators, propellant degradation | 1,790,894 (all Honda/Acura models combined) | Zone A/B geographic recall. Explicitly lists "2007-2008 Fit." This is the "original-equipment Takata" campaign that F-WP02-013 said it could not find. |
| **17V029000** | recall began March 2017 | Passenger frontal airbag inflator (Takata) — same defect family, later expansion zone | 308,884 (all Honda/Acura models combined) | Zone A/B geographic recall. Explicitly lists "2007-2008 Fit." |

This directly resolves the open question in F-WP02-013 ("could not confirm... any original
equipment Takata campaign") — the answer is **yes, two of them exist** (16V344 and 17V029), and
they were missed because WP-02's WebSearch-only pass never surfaced them. Given this book's
explicit safety mandate, the recall/TSB chapter (18) and Appendix I **must** be updated to
include all 8 campaigns before this WP can be called complete, not just verified.

**2. Pass rate:** 26 fact cards checked. 24 PASS / 2 FAIL on content accuracy (F-WP02-011,
partially F-WP02-020's sourcing — see below). 24 YEAR-OK / 1 YEAR-FAIL (F-WP02-020, one of its
two cited sources describes a 2011 GE8 Fit, not a 2007-2008 GD3 Fit). Of the 11 Tier 4
community-problem cards, 7 are cleanly CORROBORATED, 4 are SINGLE (only one of the two cited
sources actually supports the specific claim on inspection).

**3. Two long-standing conflicts in `queue/conflicts.md` are now resolved with a primary-source
read** (C-001, C-003, C-005 — see below); one (C-004, TSB count) remains unresolved because the
only sources that could settle it (obd-codes.com, carcomplaints.com) are Cloudflare-blocked to
this session's network egress even through the reader proxy; C-002 was already a non-issue.
C-006 (complaint counts) is now a *three*-way disagreement, not two — see F-WP02-011 below.
