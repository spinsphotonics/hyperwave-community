# Verification report: procedures/WP-14-1.md through WP-14-18.md (Home repairs)

**Method:** All 18 cards were read in full for template compliance (field presence, field
order, safety-before-steps, step word count, level-4 shop-summary format). Word counts on every
numbered step across all 18 cards were computed programmatically. 8 of the 18 cards' cited
technical claims (torque values, part numbers, specific procedural details attributed to a
Source ID) were independently re-fetched via direct curl against the actual cited source pages
and checked verbatim — more than the "at least 5 of 18" the task asked for.

## Template compliance

### Safety warnings before Steps — 18 / 18 PASS
Every card places "Safety warnings:" before "Steps:" (or before "What the shop will do:" for
the three level-4 cards). No card puts a warning after its steps.

### Level-4 cards use a shop-summary instead of full steps — 3 / 3 PASS
WP-14-3 (front struts), WP-14-10 (alternator), and WP-14-11 (ignition switch) are the file's
only three skill-level-4 cards, and all three correctly replace "Steps:" with "What the shop
will do:" plus a "Why this book does not give full DIY steps here" explanation, per the plan's
own rule ("level 4 items get a 'what the shop will do' summary instead of full steps"). Minor,
non-blocking deviation: all three also omit the template's "Tools:" and "Parts and supplies:"
fields entirely rather than including them — the plan does not explicitly authorize dropping
those two fields for level-4 cards, only the Steps-to-summary swap. This is a reasonable,
defensible choice (there is little point listing tools for a job you are telling the reader not
to DIY), but it is a real deviation from "copy the template exactly" and should be a deliberate,
documented decision rather than an implicit one if the book's actual style guide is meant to be
followed literally.
