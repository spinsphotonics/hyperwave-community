# Verification: WP-17 Engine Bolt-ons and Tuning — 2008 Honda Fit (GD3)

**This is the highest-priority verification in the batch: WP-17's headline claim is that
Hondata's FlashPro explicitly supports the "2007-2008 US Fit" (GD3) and has NO product at all
for the 2009-2014 GE8. This claim was independently re-derived from scratch this pass —
not just re-checking WP-17's citations, but running fresh queries against hondata.com's own
live catalog and search/filter tools, exactly as the task required.**

Verifier method: direct `curl` (real browser User-Agent) against hondata.com and every

## THE HEADLINE CLAIM: VERIFIED, HOLDS UP COMPLETELY

Four independent, direct checks against Hondata's real, live site all point the same way:

1. **`hondata.com/flashpro-fit` (direct fetch, HTTP 200):** Specification table reads
   "Vehicle Model: Fit / Year: 2007-2008 / Market: US/Canada / Transmission: MT/AT." Product
   code "FP-FIT-US," price "$795.00," "In Stock." ECU part numbers "RME-A11 through RME-A89
   (2007-2008 US Fit MT and AT) / RME-C11 through RME-C89 (2007-2008 Canada Fit MT and AT)."
   **Every one of these values matches F-WP17-013 and F-WP17-015 exactly, character for
   character.**
2. **`hondata.com/flashpro-fit-carb` (direct fetch, HTTP 200):** Confirms the CARB variant,
   product code "FP-FIT-US-CARB," price "$595.00," "CARB EO D-742-1," same "Year: 2007-2008 /
   Market: US/Canada / Transmission: MT/AT" table. **Matches F-WP17-014 exactly.**
3. **Hondata's own FlashPro category, filtered to the Fit vehicle
   (`hondata.com/products/flashpro?...&filter=10&limit=100`, direct fetch, HTTP 200): returns
   "Showing 1 to 2 of 2 (1 Pages)"** — and the only two products present are "FlashPro Fit" and
   "FlashPro Fit (CARB)," i.e. the two 2007-2008 GD3 products already confirmed above. **No
   2009+ Fit product exists in this filtered list at all — exact match to F-WP17-017's
   central claim.**
4. **Independent confirmation beyond what WP-17 itself checked:** this verifier additionally
   ran Hondata's own site search for the literal term "Fit"
   (`hondata.com/index.php?route=product/search&search=Fit&limit=100`, direct fetch) and got
   "Showing 1 to 3 of 3" — the same two FlashPro Fit products, plus one unrelated
   "coil-pack-retrofit" product whose own text uses the word "benefit," not the car model
   "Fit" (a false-positive text match, not a hidden third Fit product). **No GE8/2009+ Fit
   product exists anywhere on Hondata's site by this broader search either.** This verifier
   also independently fetched Hondata's Reflash category page directly
   (`hondata.com/reflash-category`, HTTP 200): "Showing 1 to 17 of 17" products, and the only
   occurrence of the string "fit" anywhere in that page's text is inside the word "benefit" —
   zero Reflash products for the Fit of either generation, exactly as F-WP17-017 states.
5. **`hondata.com/products` (direct fetch, HTTP 200):** "Hondata makes ECU products for: 92-99
   OBDI vehicles, 02-06 K20 vehicles, Reflashes for certain 2006+ vehicles, Programmers for
   certain 2006+ vehicles" — confirms F-WP17-018 verbatim (K-Pro/OBDI never applied to any Fit
   generation).
6. **Independent corroboration of the ECU part numbers (F-WP17-015):** revlinekc.com's own
   FlashPro-for-Fit listing, independently re-fetched directly, reproduces "RME-A11 through
   RME-A89 (2007-2008 US Fit MT and AT) RME-C11 through RME-C89 (2007-2008 Canada Fit MT and
   AT)" word-for-word — matches WP-17's own corroboration claim exactly.

**Conclusion: the surprising, counter-intuitive claim is correct.** Hondata's FlashPro
tuning system exists, in the current live catalog, specifically and only for the 2007-2008
US/Canada Fit (this book's GD3), at two price points (full and CARB-legal), with exact
manufacturer-stated ECU part numbers — and Hondata has never sold a FlashPro, Reflash, K-Pro,
or s300 product for the 2009-2014 GE8 Fit (L15A7) at all. A reader's intuition that "the newer
Fit definitely has more tuning support" is wrong for this specific brand, exactly as WP-17's
headline finding states. Hondata's own forum-thread title, "Still nothing for the L15A7?"
(F-WP17-017's second, Tier 4 half) could not be independently read past its title this pass —
Cloudflare-blocked the same as fitfreak.net — but it is not needed to sustain the claim; the
Tier 1 catalog/search/category evidence above stands on its own.
