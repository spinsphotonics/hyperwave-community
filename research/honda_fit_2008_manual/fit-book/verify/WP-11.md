### Issue found and fixed: WP-11-18

One safety-warning bullet read "If you do touch the glass, clean it with denatured alcohol and
a clean cloth before installing it." — this does not start with "Do not" or "Always," the
template's own rule for every safety-warning line. **Fixed in place**: reworded to "Always clean
the glass with denatured alcohol and a clean cloth before installing it, if you accidentally
touch it." No change to meaning or safety content, only to satisfy the template's required
sentence-opening rule.

## Rear brake card: drum, not disc — CONFIRMED

**procedures/WP-11-8.md correctly states DRUM, not disc**, for the rear brakes:

> "The 2008 Honda Fit base trim has REAR DRUM BRAKES, not rear discs. The owner's manual states
> this directly and in plain language... 'Your vehicle is equipped with front disc brakes. The
> brakes on the rear wheels are drum.'"

This was independently re-checked against a fresh, independent re-fetch of the Owner's Manual
PDF (not the researcher's cached copy) — the manual's "Braking System Design" section reads,
verbatim:

> "Your vehicle is equipped with front disc brakes. The brakes on the rear wheels are drum."

Exact match. The whole procedure card (tools, drum-specific steps: pull drum off hub, unhook
return springs, hold-down pins, self-adjuster, no mention of rotors/pads) is internally
consistent with drum brakes throughout, with no disc-brake language anywhere in the card.
**Confirmed correct.**
