# Diagram Brief: Brake Assembly (Front Disc and Rear Drum)

**Caption (for the finished diagram):** Exploded-view diagrams of the 2008 Honda Fit's
front disc brake and rear drum brake assemblies, showing each part in the order it comes
apart and goes back together — for Chapter 27 (Brakes) and Chapter 40 (Brake Job, Full).

**Status: BRIEF ONLY — do not attempt this as an SVG.** This car has **two different
brake designs front and rear** (confirmed directly from the owner's manual: "Your
vehicle is equipped with front disc brakes. The brakes on the rear wheels are drum." —
Fact, `procedures/WP-11-8.md`, Tier 1, high confidence). An exploded mechanical view has
to show real part shapes — a caliper casting, a rotor's hat and friction surface, a
drum's flanged shape, a return spring's actual hook geometry — accurately enough that a
16-year-old could recognize the real part in their hand. None of the source material
available to this task included a dimensioned or photographed reference for those
shapes, so drawing them now would mean inventing geometry, which this project's own
rules forbid. This brief gives an illustrator (or a future pass with real reference
photos or the Honda/Haynes service-manual diagrams, redrawn rather than traced) exactly
what to draw and in what order.

## What is confirmed vs. not, at a glance

**Confirmed (Tier 1, safe to draw exactly as described):**
- Front = disc, rear = drum, no exceptions by trim.
- The order parts come off/go back on for both systems (the numbered steps above).
- Wheel nut torque: 80 lbf·ft (108 N·m).
- Which safety warnings must appear before the steps (eye protection, jack stands,
  no compressed air on brake dust, grease-off-linings).

**Not confirmed — do not invent, flag instead:**
- Exact bolt sizes for the caliper slide pins and mounting bracket (one is UNVERIFIED,
  the other is a single-source community report).
- Rotor and drum dimensions, casting shape, or vent design.
- Exact part numbers for pads, shoes, or hardware kits (all UNVERIFIED this pass — see
  Appendix F once sourced).

## What NOT to do

- Do not draw a generic "stock photo" brake exploded view and relabel it for this car —
  disc/drum details vary enough between vehicles that a wrong shape is misleading, not
  just imprecise.
- Do not trace a diagram from the Honda service manual, a parts catalog, or any other
  copyrighted source (Rule D7). Redraw from verified facts once the gaps above are
  filled.
- Do not guess metric bolt sizes to make the parts list look more complete than it is —
  print "size not confirmed" rather than a specific number that was not actually sourced.
