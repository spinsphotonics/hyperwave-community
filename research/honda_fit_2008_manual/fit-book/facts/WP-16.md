# WP-16 Fact Cards — Wheels, tires, suspension, brakes (Chapters 49-51)

Vehicle: 2008 Honda Fit, US market, base trim, first generation, chassis code GD3 (sold in the
US for MY2007-2008 only). Rule D8 and the WP-16 task brief's own rule are both applied
throughout: **every aftermarket product below was checked for the product listing's OWN text**
naming "GD3", "2007-2008", "07-08", or explicit separate 2007 AND 2008 model-year lines — never
a generic "Honda Fit" fitment claim, and never carried over from a GE8 (2009-2014) listing just
because the brand also makes a Fit part. Products that failed this check are listed in the
"Rejected / flagged products" section at the end of this file, not as numbered facts, per Rule 2
("never guess") and RESEARCH_PLAN.md Section 5's instruction for this WP.

This WP builds on WP-10's fitment facts (`facts/WP-10.md`) rather than re-deriving lug pattern
(4x100), center bore (56.1mm), factory offset (ET45), or factory tire size (P175/65R14 81S) —
see F-WP10-011 through F-WP10-016 for those. New wheel/tire/offset combinations found this pass
are added below as new cards, not duplicates.

**Access-method note (per ADAPT-1/ADAPT-2):** most product-listing text below was read by a
direct `curl` fetch of the real page (upgraded confidence, "directly-verified" per ADAPT-2), a
handful needed the `r.jina.ai` reader-proxy route when curl hit a bot-check or JS-only page, and

## Question 1 — Wheel sizes/offsets the community runs without rubbing

```
Fact ID: F-WP16-001
Chapter: 49
Question: Wheel sizes/offsets the community runs without rubbing (baseline, cross-referenced from WP-10)
Value: WP-10 (F-WP10-016) already established, from two independent Tier 4 sources, that 195/50R15
on a 15x6.5-7 wheel at +40 to +42mm offset is the community's commonly-reported "fits without
rubbing" combination at stock ride height, and that 205/45R16 on a 16x7 wheel is a marginal/
borderline size (works only at +42mm minimum offset, with some rear rubbing reported especially
with rear passengers). This WP does not re-derive that finding; see facts/WP-10.md F-WP10-016 for
the full citation. This card exists only to point readers of WP-16 to that baseline before the
NEW combinations below.

## Question 2 — Popular autocross tire sizes for the GD3 (SCCA Solo classing)

```

Chapter: 49, 58

Value: In the SCCA National Solo Rules Appendix A (2017 edition, the most recent edition this WP
could fetch and read in full — see caution below), "Fit" appears under Honda in H Street class
(HS) with NO model-year restriction listed, meaning the entry covers all Fit model years
including the 2007-2008 GD3. A stock, unmodified 2008 Fit therefore runs in H Street.
Applies to: 2008 GD3 base trim (yes, per the rulebook's unrestricted "Fit" entry)
Source ID: S-WP16-W
