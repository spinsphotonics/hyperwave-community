before continuing (arrange a tow).

Question: Shift interlock (shift lock) release — location and procedure on automatics
Value: Location: a covered slot (the "shift lock release slot") built into the shift
lever console, next to the shift lever's release button, hidden under a notched cover.
Procedure (used only if the normal "press brake pedal + press release button" method
fails to move the lever out of Park): (1) set the parking brake; (2) remove the key from
the ignition; (3) protect the notch of the shift-lock-release slot cover with a cloth,
then carefully pry the cover off using a small flat-tip screwdriver or a metal fingernail
file; (4) insert the key into the shift lock release slot; (5) push down on the key while
pressing the shift lever's release button, and move the lever out of Park to Neutral; (6)
remove the key from the slot and reinstall the cover (notch on the left side); press the
brake pedal and restart the engine. The manual notes that needing this procedure means
