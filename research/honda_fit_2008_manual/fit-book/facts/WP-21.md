Fact ID: F-WP21-006
Chapter: 32 (also Appendix D)
Question: How do you check whether a fuse is blown, step by step?
Value: (1) Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0); make sure the headlights and all other
accessories are off. (2) Remove the cover from the fuse box. (3) For the large fuses in the
under-hood fuse boxes, look through the top of the fuse at the wire inside. (4) For the smaller
fuses in the primary under-hood box and all fuses in the interior box, pull each one out with the
fuse puller. (5) Look for a blown wire inside the fuse — a blown fuse's internal wire is broken
or melted. First check the specific fuse(s) listed for the affected device in the fuse chart or
on the fuse box lid diagram, but check all the fuses before concluding a blown fuse is the cause.
Applies to: 2008 GD3 base trim (yes)

Fact ID: F-WP21-008

Question: What does the owner's manual say about replacing a fuse with one of a higher amperage
rating?
Value: The manual explicitly warns against it: replacing a fuse with one that has a higher
rating greatly increases the chance of damaging the electrical system. If the correct-rated
replacement fuse is not available, the manual says to install one with a LOWER rating instead of
a higher one, and to swap in the correctly-rated fuse as soon as possible afterward (a fuse
replaced with a lower rating may blow again, which the manual says does not indicate a problem
by itself).

Fact ID: F-WP21-009
Chapter: 32 (also Appendix D)
Question: What happens if the radio fuse is removed or the battery is disconnected, and how do
you recover?
Value: The audio system disables itself (a security/anti-theft feature). The next time the radio
is turned on, "ENTER CODE" appears in the frequency display. The owner must enter their audio
system's specific 5-digit code using the radio's preset buttons to reactivate it. The code is on
a radio code card that should have come with the vehicle (Honda recommends storing that card at
home and writing the audio system's serial number in the owner's manual as a backup). There are
10 tries to enter the correct code; after 10 failed attempts, the radio must be left powered on
for 1 hour before trying again. If the code card is lost, the code must be obtained from a
dealer using the audio system's serial number.
Applies to: 2008 GD3 base trim (yes)
Source ID: S-001
Source URL: https://techinfo.honda.com/rjanisis/pubs/om/AAA080/AAA0808OM.pdf

removed, the audio system will disable itself. The next time you turn on the radio you will see
'ENTER CODE' in the frequency display. Use the preset buttons to enter the five-digit code.")

## Gaps / limitations

- The manual's fuse chart itself does not give relay locations or a separate "relay map" — the
  WP-21 title says "Fuse and relay maps," but the owner's manual documents fuses only; no
  standalone relay location diagram or relay amperage/function chart was found in the "Taking
  Care of the Unexpected" chapter, the specifications section, or the index (search terms
  "relay," under "R" in the index, and full-text search of the manual). Two relay-named circuits
  (Blower Relay, under-hood fuse 5; and a relay implied by the fuse-box.info page's HAC/Blower
  labeling) are visible only as fuse-protected circuits, not as a physical relay-box diagram.
  This is left UNVERIFIED as its own item below rather than invented.
- All 33 interior positions and all 16 under-hood positions have a value (either an amperage or
  "Not Used"); there are zero UNVERIFIED cells in either fuse table.
