Vehicle: 2008 Honda Fit, US market, base trim, first generation, chassis code GD3, engine L15A1.
Rule D8 applied throughout: every fact below is drawn from a source that either explicitly names
"2008," "2007-2008," or the first-generation Fit (GD3), or — for aftermarket parts-fitment pages —
gives a distinct 2007-2008 (GD3) row/table separate from the 2009+ (GE8) one, and the GD3 row/table
is what was used.

Primary source for this work package is the actual 2008 Fit Owner's Manual (Honda's own document,
P/N 31SAA610), read directly as a 282-page PDF via the r.jina.ai reader proxy per ADAPT-2 (Source
ID **S-023**). Every capacity value pulled from its Specifications table (which extracts with
jumbled/interleaved table cells) was cross-checked against an independently-labeled, cleanly
formatted second source — mainly **S-024** (workshop-manuals.com's Fit L4-1.5L (2008) factory-style
data pages) and **S-015** (Honda's own press-kit specifications) — before being marked high
confidence. See `sources/S-023.md` and `sources/S-024.md` for the exact page URLs used.

Fact ID: F-WP09-001
Chapter: 21
Question: Does the 2008 Fit use Honda's Maintenance Minder or a fixed mileage schedule?
Value: Honda Maintenance Minder. The 2008 Fit owner's manual has no printed dual "Normal
  Conditions / Severe Conditions" mileage table (the kind older Hondas print). Instead, an
  onboard computer calculates remaining engine oil life as a percentage from driving
  conditions and accumulated engine revolutions, displays it on the instrument panel, and
  shows a maintenance item code (Main Item A or B, plus Sub Item numbers) when service is
  due. Driving severity is factored into the calculation automatically rather than being
  left to the owner to classify.
Applies to: 2008 GD3 base trim (yes)
Source ID: S-023
Source URL: https://techinfo.honda.com/rjanisis/pubs/om/AAA080/AAA0808OM.pdf
Exact location in source: printed page 179, "Maintenance Minder" heading, opening paragraph
  ("Your vehicle displays engine oil life and maintenance service items on the information
  display..."); absence of any "Severe Conditions"/"Normal Conditions" heading was confirmed
  by a full-text search of the extracted manual (zero matches outside unrelated airbag-safety
  text)
Tier: 1

  (non-Fit-specific) dealer explainer that implies a fixed 7,500-10,000/3,000-4,999 mi split

Fact ID: F-WP09-015
Chapter: 21
Question: Severe vs. normal service definitions
Value: The 2008 Fit owner's manual does NOT publish a generic "Severe Conditions" vs.
  "Normal Conditions" definitions list or dual mileage table (unlike some older Honda
  models and unlike some other same-era Honda vehicles' manuals). Driving severity is
  instead folded automatically into the Maintenance Minder's calculated oil-life percentage
  and item-code timing. The manual's only explicit, named "severe condition" triggers are
  item-specific: (1) dusty driving conditions -> replace air cleaner element every 15,000 mi;
  (2) high-soot urban/diesel-traffic areas -> replace dust and pollen filter every 15,000 mi;
  (3) mountainous, low-speed driving (A/T only) -> change transmission fluid at 60,000 mi
  then every 30,000 mi; (4) towing behind a motorhome (A/T only) -> change transmission
