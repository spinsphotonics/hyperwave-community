### F-WP18-013
Chapter: 55
Question: What are the key obstacles reported for a GD3 K-series swap (mounts, wiring, ECU, driveshaft/axle, hood/body clearance)?
Value: From the pbomers.com build page (direct read), organized by system:
- **Mounts:** the K-series "adapter"-style motor mount design uses the factory L15 Fit mount locations, but owners who did not either buy aftermarket stiffer mounts or reinforce ("window weld") the stock ones reported the engine "rocks quite a bit" and rubs inside the already-tight bay. Hasport brand mounts are the one specifically named vendor (see also F-WP18-017 for commercial confirmation).
- **Body/hood clearance:** the front core support has to be cut to fit the larger K24 intake manifold; less cutting is needed with a smaller intake manifold such as the 02-04 RSX Type S unit. The stock front-mounted radiator/condenser and their pusher-style cooling fans also foul the taller intake manifold, so several builders relocate the radiator forward and run slimmer pull-style fans instead.
- **Wiring/ECU:** builders report reaching out to over half a dozen harness companies (including Hasport) and being ignored; the page's own solution was to hand-build the harness using a dashboard/ECU wiring comparison between an 08 Acura TSX and an 08 Fit (both share the same drive-by-wire and multiplex electrical architecture) — an 07-08 TSX ECU (tuned with Hondata or KTuner) is the documented ECU choice, and the vehicle's immobilizer must be disabled or re-synced to the new key/ECU at a Honda dealership. The Fit's own dash connector (C101) has to be re-pinned to match the TSX's dash connector (C201) since take a wrecked-car connector proved hard to source. A second independent 2014 k20a.org build (F-WP18-012) confirms the same 07-08-TSX-ECU-plus-FlashPro method independently.
- **Transmission/driveshaft compatibility:** an 08 Civic Si transmission (which already has an LSD) was used; the page explicitly warns "you can't use any K24 transmissions because the casing is different and the mounts won't work," and that Honda changed from a low-frequency to a high-frequency vehicle-speed sensor at some point, so the donor transmission needs to be new enough (the builder believes 2005-model-year-or-newer) to read correctly on the TSX ECU.
- **Axles:** an OEM axle set from a 2002-2004 Acura RSX base model is used (swapped side-to-side, with the RSX's inner boots swapped onto them) rather than the factory Fit axles.
- **Power steering:** because the Fit uses electric power steering, an idler arm from a Honda Civic Si EP3 is needed in place of the missing hydraulic pump linkage.
- **Exhaust/header:** described as "a tough one" — the builder got a custom-made header from another Fit K-swap builder; a second, commercially available off-the-shelf header option was mentioned as existing but not detailed on the page.
Applies to: 2008 GD3 base trim (yes)
