# WP-17 Fact Cards — Engine: bolt-ons and tuning (Chapters 52-53)

Vehicle: 2008 Honda Fit, US market, base trim, first generation, chassis code GD3, engine
L15A1 (confirmed in facts/WP-01.md, F-WP01-001). This is the highest year-confusion-risk WP in
the project (RESEARCH_PLAN.md Section 5, WP-17 entry): the 2009-2014 Fit (chassis GE8) uses a
different engine (L15A7) and a different ECU, and aftermarket tuning support for the two
generations does NOT overlap the way a reader might assume. Rule D8 was applied to every fact
below: every product/claim states, or was independently checked against, explicit model-year
or chassis-code language, and every claim that turned out to be GE8-only is recorded as
REJECTED/not applicable rather than silently dropped or silently kept.

**Research method for this WP:** hondata.com (the single most important source for Chapter 53)
was directly readable via plain `curl` with a browser User-Agent (HTTP 200, full page content,
not JS-blocked) — this is a Tier 1 manufacturer source read directly, not a search snippet, per
ADAPT-2's confidence rules. Several independent retailer/manufacturer product pages (Injen,
A'PEXi, KamiSpeed, RevlineKC, A&J Racing, Weapon*R) were also directly curl-readable. fitfreak.net
was Cloudflare-blocked to both curl and the r.jina.ai proxy on every attempt this pass — the
same dead end already logged in WP-00/01/02/09/11/12/13's queue/blocked.md entries — so every
fitfreak.net-sourced fact below is WebSearch-snippet/AI-summary-sourced per ADAPT-1, with a real
thread URL attached, and is capped at Tier 4/low-or-medium confidence accordingly.

**HEADLINE FINDING (read this before Chapter 53):** Contrary to the assumption that GE8/L15A7
aftermarket tuning support is much deeper than GD3/L15A1 support, a direct, current, Tier 1
read of Hondata's own product catalog shows the opposite is true for Hondata specifically:
Hondata sells a full ECU-reflash tuning system (FlashPro) explicitly for the "2007-2008 US Fit"
(GD3) — and, per Hondata's own catalog, has never sold FlashPro, K-Pro, s300, or a factory
Reflash for the 2009-2014 Fit (GE8/L15A7) at all. See Section B below and the ledger note for
this WP.

## Section C: Honest summary for the reader (Chapter 53)

Fact ID: F-WP17-022
Chapter: 53
Question: Summary — is meaningful ECU tuning realistically available for this specific car?
Value: YES, honestly — and more directly than a reader familiar with the newer Fit's
reputation might expect. A mainstream, currently-in-stock, full-reflash tuning system
(Hondata FlashPro, $795 full version or $595 CARB-legal version) exists specifically for the
2007-2008 US Fit's exact ECU part numbers (F-WP17-013/014/015). This is not a niche or
discontinued method — it is Hondata's normal current product line, the same company and
product family used across many other current Honda/Acura models. On top of that, at least
one community member has run a piggyback air/fuel controller (Apexi VAFC) on a GD3, with
real, honestly-reported limitations (F-WP17-020). What does NOT exist for this car: a
plug-and-play standalone EMS with a dedicated GD3 harness (F-WP17-021, unverified/not found),
and Hondata's own older K-Pro/OBDI hardware, which was never built for this engine or this
OBD generation regardless of Fit generation (F-WP17-018/019). The genuinely surprising finding
of this WP is the DIRECTION of the year-confusion risk: it is not that GE8 tuning products get
mistakenly assumed to work on the GD3 — Hondata's own catalog shows FlashPro tuning support
runs the other way, existing for the GD3 (2007-2008) and NOT for the GE8 (2009-2014) at all,
per Hondata's own product filter and their own customers' forum question "Still nothing for
the L15A7?" (F-WP17-017). A reader should not assume "the newer Fit definitely has more tuning
support" without checking — for this specific brand/product, it is the 2007-2008 car (this
book's car) that Hondata actually supports.

all of which are GD3-scoped facts already individually confirmed above)
