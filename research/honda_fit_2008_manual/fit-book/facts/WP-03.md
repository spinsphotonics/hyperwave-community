Fact ID: F-WP03-001
Chapter: 4
Question: How the key works
Value: The base (non-Sport) 2008 Fit uses a plain metal mechanical key, not a fob. Two keys come with the vehicle. Each key has a chip inside that talks to the car's immobilizer system, so a key with a damaged chip will not start the engine, even though the key itself has no battery. Owners also get a key-number tag for ordering a replacement key.
Applies to: 2008 GD3 base trim (yes)

Fact ID: F-WP03-003
Chapter: 4
Question: How to lock/unlock all doors
Value: Every door has a small tab on top; pushing it in locks that door, pulling it out unlocks it. The base Fit also has power door locks: pressing the top of the master switch on the driver's door locks every door and the tailgate together, and pressing the bottom of that same switch unlocks all of them. Turning the key in the driver's door lock also locks or unlocks the driver's door alone; turning it a second time unlocks the rest of the doors and the tailgate. The master switch will not work while the driver's door is open with the key in the ignition.
Applies to: 2008 GD3 base trim (yes)

Question: Child safety lock location
Value: Each rear door has its own small lock lever near the door's rear edge. Switching that lever to the LOCK position stops that rear door from being opened from inside, no matter what the regular door lock tab is set to; it can still be opened normally from outside using the outer handle.

Fact ID: F-WP03-005
Chapter: 4
Question: Hood release and prop rod location
Value: The hood release handle sits under the lower-left corner of the dashboard, near the driver's left knee. Pulling it pops the hood up only slightly, since a second, hidden safety catch still holds it. To fully free the hood you then reach under its leading edge, feel for that second catch near the center, and push it to release. A metal support rod is stored clipped under the hood; it swings out and its tip fits into a hole on the hood's underside to hold the hood open.
Applies to: 2008 GD3 base trim (yes)

Question: Fuel door release
Value: The fuel door has no interior release lever or cable on the base Fit. To open it, you go around to the outside of the car and pull on the notched edge of the fuel door itself. The fuel cap stays attached to the filler neck by a small tether so it cannot be dropped or lost while fueling.

Fact ID: F-WP03-007
Chapter: 4
Question: Trunk/hatch (tailgate) release
Value: The Fit is a hatchback, so its rear cargo door is called the tailgate, not a trunk. It opens from the outside only: pull its handle, then lift the tailgate up. It has no separate interior release; instead, it locks and unlocks together with the driver's door whenever you use the key, the driver's door lock tab, or the master door-lock switch. On base (non-Sport) models the tailgate can also be locked or unlocked directly with the key.
Applies to: 2008 GD3 base trim (yes)

Fact ID: F-WP03-008
Chapter: 5
Question: Seat adjustment controls and ranges
Value: The driver's seat slides forward and backward using a lever under the front edge of the seat cushion; lift it, slide the seat, then let go and rock the seat to confirm it caught. A second lever on the side of the seat-back tilts the backrest angle; pull it out, lean the seat-back to the angle you want, and release it to lock. Rear outer seat-backs recline separately using a release lever on the outside edge of each seat-back. The manual does not give a numeric travel distance or a number of notches/positions for either adjustment.
Applies to: 2008 GD3 base trim (yes)

Fact ID: F-WP03-009
Chapter: 5
Question: Steering wheel tilt, and telescoping (verify)
Value: The steering wheel tilts up and down: push the lever under the steering column all the way down to unlock it, move the wheel to the angle you want so you can still see the gauges, then push the lever back up to lock it. The manual's index lists only "Tilt the Steering Wheel" (no telescoping entry anywhere in the 282-page manual or its index), and this adjustment is not marked "if equipped" the way genuinely optional features (cruise control, paddle shifters) are elsewhere in the book. The base Fit has a tilt-only steering column; it does not telescope in or out.
Applies to: 2008 GD3 base trim (yes)

Fact ID: F-WP03-010
Chapter: 5
Question: Mirror adjustment: manual or power on base? (verify)
Value: Power. The base Fit's outside mirrors adjust electrically: with the ignition on, a selector switch picks the driver's or passenger's mirror, then a second switch tilts that mirror up, down, left, or right; returning the selector to its center (off) position locks the setting. The manual scopes power mirrors as standard on "all models except Canadian DX," and Canadian DX is not part of the US base trim this book covers, so the US base Fit gets power mirrors, not manual ones. The inside rear-view mirror also has a day/night glare-reducing flip tab.
Applies to: 2008 GD3 base trim (yes)

Fact ID: F-WP03-011
Chapter: 5
Question: Headrest adjustment
Value: All seating positions have head restraints. The two front head restraints slide up and down for height: pull straight up to raise one, or press its release button sideways while pushing it down to lower it (both hands needed, and never while driving). The manual says to line up the middle of the back of your head with the center of the restraint. The rear outer head restraints do not adjust for height; they only lift out for cleaning by pressing a release button.
Applies to: 2008 GD3 base trim (yes)

Fact ID: F-WP03-012
Chapter: 5
Question: Seat belt adjustment (how to fasten and position it)
Value: To buckle up, push the metal latch plate into the buckle and tug to confirm it caught; press the red button on the buckle to release it. For proper fit, pull the lap portion down low across the hip bones first, then pull the shoulder portion up to remove slack so it crosses the middle of the chest and shoulder, not the neck or arm. If the belt still crosses the neck or arm, the manual says to change the shoulder anchor height instead (see F-WP03-013).
Applies to: 2008 GD3 base trim (yes)
