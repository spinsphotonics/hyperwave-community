Fact ID: F-WP06-002
Chapter: 12
Question: Manual transmission reverse lockout method
Value: The transmission has a built-in lockout that prevents shifting directly from 5th
gear to reverse. The manual does not describe a mechanical ring or collar you lift — it
describes a timing/gear-speed lockout. To shift into reverse without the gears grinding:
come to a full stop, push the clutch pedal all the way down, and either (a) pause a few
seconds before moving the lever into reverse, or (b) shift briefly into a forward gear
first. Either action lets the input shaft stop spinning before reverse engages.
Applies to: 2008 GD3 base trim (yes)

Fact ID: F-WP06-012

Question: Fuel economy tips specific to this car, from the owner's manual
Value: Factors that lower fuel economy (per the manual): aggressive driving (hard
acceleration/braking); excess idling and stop-and-go acceleration/braking; running the
engine cold; driving with a heavy load or the A/C on; under-inflated tires. Tips to
improve it: keep up with maintenance and use the correct oil viscosity; keep tires
properly inflated; avoid carrying excess weight; keep the underside clean of snow/mud
buildup; drive moderately (avoid rapid acceleration, hard cornering, hard braking);
observe the speed limit, since aerodynamic drag rises sharply above 45 mph (75 km/h) and
roof racks/carriers/trailers add drag; and, on the manual transmission, use the highest
gear that still lets the engine run smoothly.

Fact ID: F-WP06-014

Question: Winter tire guidance from the owner's manual
Value: Tires marked "M+S" (mud and snow) or "All Season" on the sidewall are suitable for
most winter driving. For the best traction in snow or ice, the manual recommends
installing dedicated snow tires or tire chains, noting these may be legally required in
some places. If snow tires are mounted, they must be radial tires of the same size and
load range as the originals, and must be mounted on all four wheels (not just the drive
wheels) — dry-road traction may be lower than the original tires. Ask the tire dealer for
a maximum speed recommendation for the snow tires.

Fact ID: F-WP06-015
Chapter: 14
Question: Snow chain permissibility from the owner's manual
Value: Tire chains are permitted, but with restrictions specific to this car's limited
tire clearance. Mount chains only on the front tires — never the rear. Only SAE Class "S"
cable-type traction devices with rubber chain tensioners are allowed; metal link-style
chains are explicitly told not to be used. Install only when required by conditions or
local law, follow the chain maker's instructions, mount them as tight as possible, drive
slowly, and check that they aren't contacting the brake lines or suspension — stop and
check if you hear contact. Remove the chains as soon as the road is clear.
Applies to: 2008 GD3 base trim (yes)
