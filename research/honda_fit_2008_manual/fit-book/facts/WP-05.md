Question: How does the recirculation lever work, and when should you use recirculation mode?
Value: The Fresh Air/Recirculation lever controls where intake air comes from. Fact ID: F-WP05-018
Chapter: 8
Question: Door lock controls — manual tab, key, and power locks
Value: Each door has a lock tab on top; push it in to lock, pull it out to unlock. Front doors can also be locked/unlocked with the ignition key (all US models except Sport use a key cylinder on both front doors; check trim specifics for the exact door). Power door locks: pushing the TOP of the master door-lock switch on the driver's door locks all doors and the tailgate together; pushing the BOTTOM unlocks all of them. Each passenger door's own lock tab locks/unlocks only that door. Turning the key once in the driver's door from outside unlocks only the driver's door; turning it again unlocks the rest. To lock a passenger door from outside, push in its tab and close the door. Lockout prevention: with the driver's door OPEN and the key still in the ignition, the master lock switch is disabled, and if you try to push in the driver's door tab to lock it that way, all door tabs pop back up when the door closes — this is designed to stop you from locking your only key inside.

Value: If the fuel fill cap is loose or missing, a "CHECK FUEL CAP" message appears as scrolling text in the instrument panel's information display after the engine is next started (the words cycle CHECK, then FUEL, then CAP, repeatedly).
