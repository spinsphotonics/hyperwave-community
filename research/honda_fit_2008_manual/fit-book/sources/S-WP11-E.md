Standard resting-voltage test: multimeter set to 20V DC, red probe to positive post, black to negative, engine off, battery rested at least 1 hour. 12.6-12.8 V = fully charged/good; 12.2 V is approx. 50% charged; below 12.4 V may indicate partial discharge; below 12.0 V often indicates a weak/failing battery. Cranking (load) test: voltage should stay above 9.6 V during starter cranking at room temperature; running voltage should rise to roughly 13.7-14.7 V once the engine starts (this last figure is charging-system behavior, not the battery itself, and is included here only as a sanity check, not as a Fit-specific spec). Corroborated across 3+ independent Tier 3 publishers on the core 12.6 V / 9.6 V figures; used at medium-high confidence for the general technique in procedures/WP-11-10.md.
