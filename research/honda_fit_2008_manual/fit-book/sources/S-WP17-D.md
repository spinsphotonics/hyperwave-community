Notes: States, in Hondata's own words: "Hondata makes ECU products for: 92-99 OBDI vehicles,
02-06 K20 vehicles, Reflashes for certain 2006+ vehicles, Programmers for certain 2006+
vehicles." This confirms Hondata's K-Pro and OBDI/s300 product lines are scoped to 1992-1999
OBD1 cars and 2002-2006 K20-engined cars respectively -- neither era nor engine family the
2007-2008 Fit (OBD2, L15A1, not a K-series engine) belongs to -- so those two product lines
cannot apply to any Fit, GD3 or GE8. Only the "Programmers" (=FlashPro) and "Reflashes" lines
cover "certain 2006+ vehicles," and of those, filtering the Reflash category to Vehicle=Fit
(https://hondata.com/products/reflash?route=product/category&language=en-gb&filter=10&limit=100,
directly read, same date) returned zero Reflash products for the Fit -- so FlashPro (S-WP17-A/B)
is the ONLY Hondata product of any kind that exists for the Fit, in either generation.
