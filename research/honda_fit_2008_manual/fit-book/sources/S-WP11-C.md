Cabin filter: located behind the glove box in the blower-motor housing; squeeze/push the sides of the glove box to drop it down, remove the filter tray, pinch two clips to free the filter, arrow on new filter faces down. Engine air filter: housing is on the driver's side of the engine bay; release four clips/tabs securing the two housing halves (a flat-tip screwdriver may help), lift the top half, swap the element, reseat and re-latch. Corroborated by 4 independent listings on the cabin filter steps (medium confidence); engine air filter clip count/procedure corroborated by 2 listings (medium confidence). Consistent with Honda's own owner's manual (S-001/S-023), which names both filters and their maintenance intervals but explicitly defers the hands-on replacement steps to a dealer/service manual.
