Front damper pages confirm a spring compressor and a hex wrench (to hold the shaft) are needed to safely disassemble the strut — used to justify skill level 4 in procedures/WP-14-3.md, together with WP-10's own finding (F-WP10-008) that strut top-nut torque could not be confirmed for this car. Rear damper pages confirm NO spring compressor is needed (the coil spring sits separately in the axle beam, not around the shock) and give a real torque value, 40 lbf-ft (54 N-m), for the bottom damper mounting bolt — used in procedures/WP-14-4.md. Front stabilizer bar page confirms the FULL bar swap requires dropping the front subframe and the steering gearbox, a major job — used to justify treating full-bar replacement as skill level 4/shop-recommended and to distinguish it from the much simpler end-link swap in procedures/WP-14-5.md, which cites this page for that distinction but sources the end-link steps themselves from community writeups (S-WP14-D) since this site has no dedicated end-link-only page. Rear stabilizer bushing page gives a simple, real removal/installation procedure (including Honda silicone spray P/N 08209-0001) — used directly in procedures/WP-14-5.md.
