OE fitment row for base (non-Sport): 175/65R14 81S on 5.5Jx14 ET45, offset range 43-47mm, backspacing 128mm/5.04in, cold pressure 2.2 bar/32 psi — this OE tire size and pressure independently match Honda's own owner's manual (S-023) and press release (S-HONDA-SPECS, see facts/WP-01.md), which corroborates the page's reliability for the values that are NOT independently cross-checked (center bore, offset). A second attempt to corroborate the ET45 offset figure against a dealer parts catalog (honda.oempartsonline.com) and a wheel retailer (autorimshop.com) failed: the dealer site returned a Cloudflare block, and the retailer page contained an internally self-contradictory offset field ("Offset of this rim is 100mm" in body text vs. "Offset: N/A" in its own spec table) that is not usable as a real second source. Offset (ET45) is therefore recorded as single-Tier-3-sourced, medium confidence, per RESEARCH_PLAN.md Section 3. Center bore and bolt pattern are treated as higher confidence because they are corroborated by the general 4x100/56.1mm pattern documented for Honda-brand small cars broadly on the site's own bolt-pattern hub page (https://www.wheel-size.com/pcd/4x100/honda/fit/2007-2014/, also directly read) and are consistent with every other Honda Fit generation's data on the same site (a mechanically fixed dimension, not a Sport/base trim variable).
