States: any non-factory vehicle enhancement is a "modification" requiring custom vehicle insurance; performance modifications (turbochargers, suspension, strut bars) and cosmetic modifications (paint, stereo, lighting) typically are not covered by a standard policy and need an added endorsement ("custom parts and equipment coverage") or a separate custom-car policy; safety/security additions (alarms, cameras, adaptive headlights) can qualify for discounts but sometimes raise rates instead if the tech itself is expensive to repair; failing to notify the insurer before modifying can mean the custom parts are not covered and the owner pays out of pocket for repairs or replacement.
