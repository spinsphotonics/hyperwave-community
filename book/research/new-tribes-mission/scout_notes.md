## Special step

This institution is not one of the four named in Procedure S step 5's special institutions
list. The roster's "Fredonia, Wis. boot camp" location claim was searched specifically; no
digitized source located this session confirms or dates a Fredonia, Wisconsin training site.
The only Wisconsin NTM-family training presence located and dated this session is the
present-day Ethnos360 Bible Institute at Waukesha, Wisconsin, "founded in 1955 as New Tribes
Bible Institute" (per `e360-founding`'s own footer text) — a different Wisconsin town and, on
the evidence fetched this session, a later and differently named facility. This is recorded as
NOT FOUND rather than silently treated as confirming the roster's Fredonia claim; see
`sources.csv` row `ntm-fredonia-wisconsin-boot-camp`.

## Institution-specific findings

Two closely related institutional pages (`ethnosca-heritage` for Canada, `ntmuk-heritage` for
the UK) reproduce the identical Paul Fleming quotations word for word but give different
current-membership figures ("more than 2,000 missionaries" vs. "more than 3,000
missionaries") — recorded in `discrepancies.md` #1 as a currency-of-publication difference,
not a founding-era fact conflict. Wikipedia's independently footnoted casualty record — the
1943 Bolivia deaths, the two 1950 plane crashes (one killing founder Paul Fleming), and the
1953 Rattlesnake Fire — was not found duplicated or contradicted in any other source fetched
this session and is used as the chapter's principal source for the Sending section's cost
figures.
