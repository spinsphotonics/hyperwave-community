# Abridged charter — Bible Institute of Los Angeles (`biola`)

**IMPORTANT — no founding document was located.** The roster's key primary documents for this
institution are the Articles of Incorporation (1908), a first catalog, and *The King's Business*.
None of these was located as digitized full text this session: the Articles of Incorporation are
advertised on the Biola History Wiki (confluence.biola.edu) but that server's TLS certificate has
expired and could not safely be fetched; a 1920 catalog reported "full view" on HathiTrust
returned HTTP 403 on every fetch attempt; and both *The King's Business* and Biola's own Academic
Catalogs archive sit behind a JavaScript-rendered JSTOR community site that this session's
`curl`-based tooling cannot execute. See `discrepancies.md` items 2-4 and `sources.csv` rows
NONE-1 through NONE-4 for the full search record. This is logged as **BLOCKED**, on the same
precedent as this book's moody-bible-institute chapter (also BLOCKED for an unrecovered founding
document): a follow-up Fetcher pass against Biola University's own archives, or a working TLS
connection to confluence.biola.edu, is needed before a true abridged charter can be produced.

**What follows instead** is a **partial charter-in-substance**: the Institute's own printed
self-description from the back matter of a 1916 BIOLA-published pamphlet, quoted verbatim, in
place of the unrecovered 1908 Articles of Incorporation. It is a promotional advertisement, not a
legal charter, but it is the only primary text located this session in which the Los Angeles
Institute states its own purpose, character, and terms of admission in its own printed words.

## Headnote

No founding document survives in this partial abridgement. What follows is copied verbatim, in
its own original order, from the back matter of *Bible Institute of Los Angeles Afloat in Hunan,
China* (Bible Institute of Los Angeles, 1916) — English, as printed, digitized by the Internet
Archive (Columbia University Libraries copy), document_id `biola-hunan-1916`. `[...]` marks the
one cut within the passage (a short "Send us the names..." solicitation line, administrative
detail).

## Text

Bible Institute of Los Angeles (EVANGELICAL AND EVANGELISTIC) Trains Men and Women, without Charge, for all Kinds of Christian Work

Advantages

Located in the center of a great and growing cosmopolitan city.

Efficient organization yielding practical results in every department.

Thoroughly qualified teachers, with years of experience in the teaching and ministry of the Word.

[...]

Dr. R. a. TORREY, Dean

Dr. Williams Evans, Associate Dean
