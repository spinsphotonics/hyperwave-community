## Headnote

*Hausordnung der evangelischen Missions-Anstalt zu Basel* [House Rules of the Evangelical
Mission Institute at Basel], German, multiple editions 1816-1912 (Basel Mission Archive); no
edition located as a full text this session. The two fragments below are quoted, in Linda Maria
Ratschiller Nasim's own English rendering, from her 2023 Open Access chapter "The Religious Space

German archival shelfmarks in its footnotes.
