# Discrepancies — northampton-academy

1. **Attribution of the "method of education" account.** Boyd's 1860 text introduces the long
   description of Doddridge's academic method with "The METHOD OF EDUCATION pursued by Dr.
   Doddridge is very minutely and clearly detailed by the Rev. Job Orton, the substance of whose
   account of the matter will here be given" (text/boyd1860.txt, not separately entered as a
   quote record this session). This is Boyd summarizing Orton, not a verbatim reprint of Orton's
   own 1766 words throughout; only the shorter, clearly quotation-marked or clause-level phrases
   used in this dossier's quote records should be treated as close paraphrase-or-quotation. The
   passage is labeled SECONDARY throughout this dossier for that reason. A follow-up Fetcher pass
   against Orton's original *Memoirs* (1766) would let later work upgrade specific claims to a
   PRIMARY citation of Orton himself.

2. **Jennings's academy vs. Doddridge's own academy.** Much of the material located in this
   session under the search term "academy" (text/boyd1860.txt, e.g. lines ~780-960 in the raw
   OCR) describes the academy of the Rev. John Jennings, where Doddridge himself was a student
   before founding his own school. This dossier draws on that passage only for field F9
   (antecedents), and explicitly flags it there so the Writer does not silently attribute
   Jennings's admission practice (examining candidates "as to their improvement in school
   learning" and moral character) to Doddridge's own academy as if it were Doddridge's own
   stated rule. No primary statement was located this session in which Doddridge himself says he
   adopted Jennings's specific admission practice.
