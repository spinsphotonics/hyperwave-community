## Search results
- Target 1 (*Three Changes*): found in full at Founders Ministries (founders.org), a modern online transcription of the 1856 pamphlet. No standalone Internet Archive scan of the pamphlet itself was located. The address is also reprinted at length, with extensive direct quotation, inside the Broadus *Memoir* (1893), Chapter IX.
- Target 2 (*Abstract of Principles*): found in full, multiple hosts (NOBTS PDF used as the fetched copy; SBTS's own current page, Wikisource, creeds.net, and others corroborate the same 20-article text). No image of the 1858 manuscript/print original itself was located.
- Target 3 (*Fundamental Laws*): NOT FOUND as a separate digitized document this session. The NOBTS page states the professor-signing clause "continues as a part of the 'fundamental laws,'" so a fragment of the Fundamental Laws (the signing requirement) is preserved inside the fetched Abstract of Principles text, but the larger Fundamental Laws document (covering trustees, property, etc.) was not located digitized anywhere searched.
- Target 4 (Broadus, *Memoir*): found on Internet Archive under two different scan identifiers. `memoirofjamespet00broa` yields an OCR text file that is unexpectedly incomplete, breaking off mid-Chapter X (roughly p. 165 of 371). `memoirjamespeti00broagoog` (a Google Books scan) is complete (371 pp.) but has noisier OCR. Both were fetched; citations use whichever copy is cleaner for the page in question (see sources.csv notes).
