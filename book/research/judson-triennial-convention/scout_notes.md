# Scout notes — Adoniram Judson and the Baptist General Convention / "Triennial Convention" (`judson-triennial-convention`)

## Target documents (from roster "Key primary documents" cell)
1. Constitution of the General Missionary Convention (1814)
2. Judson's letters to the Baptists (1812-13)
3. *Baptist Missionary Magazine*

## Searches run and results
- `"General Missionary Convention" Baptist Denomination 1814 constitution text archive.org` (WebSearch): found archive.org identifier `proceedingsofbap00amer`, "Proceedings of the Baptist Convention for Missionary Purposes Held in Philadelphia, in May, 1814." Fetched. This pamphlet, printed by the Convention's own order, opens with the full 14-article Constitution (satisfies Target 1), followed by day-by-day minutes of the founding meeting (18-23 May 1814).
- `Wayland "Memoir of the Life and Labors of the Rev. Adoniram Judson" archive.org` (WebSearch): found archive.org identifier `memoiroflifela01wayl` (vol. 1, 1853). Fetched. Quotes at length, in quotation marks, Judson's own 1812 letter to the Third (Baptist) Church in Plymouth explaining his change of views on baptism, the February 1812 Panoplist account of the Salem ordination, and two 1813 letters from Rev. Daniel Sharp of Boston (to Judson, and to Andrew Fuller in England) describing American Baptists' response to Judson's change of views and the steps toward forming a national Baptist missionary body. Satisfies Target 2, though via the Plymouth-church letter rather than a separately-addressed Baldwin letter (see sources.csv row `judson-baldwin-1812-letter`).
- `Judson letter Baldwin 1812 baptism Calcutta` (WebSearch, as part of the above): did not surface a standalone digitized text of a letter specifically addressed to Thomas Baldwin; the letter Wayland actually reprints at length is addressed to the Third Church in Plymouth. Logged as a targeted miss (Target 2's exact addressee named in the E-ticket, "Baldwin/Bolles," was not independently confirmed as the addressee of the specific reprinted letter -- Baldwin and Bolles are named elsewhere in Wayland as Boston Baptist ministers active in the same 1812-1814 events, see dossier F9/M1).
- `Wayland "Memoir of the Life and Labors of the Rev. Adoniram Judson"` (Target 3, *Baptist Missionary Magazine*): not independently located and fetched as a standalone periodical run this session; Wayland's memoir itself quotes and paraphrases the relevant 1812-1814 periodical record (the Panoplist, not the Massachusetts Baptist Missionary Magazine specifically) -- logged as NOT FETCHED for the Magazine itself.
