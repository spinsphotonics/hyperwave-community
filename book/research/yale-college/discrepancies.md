# Discrepancies — yale-college

1. **Exact day the 1701 Charter was passed.** Dexter's headnote to Item IX states that "all
   the Acts" of the Connecticut General Court's autumn 1701 session "bear that date" (9
   October 1701), but that "the day on which the Charter was passed is not certainly known,
   but was probably the sixteenth" (text/dexter1916.txt, lines 1799-1804; not separately
   entered as a quote record this session, since it is Dexter's own editorial statement, not a
   primary document). The roster's known-facts sheet gives simply "1701 [HIGH]." This chapter
   follows Dexter in dating the Act itself "October 1701" rather than asserting a single day.
   Not a cross-source contradiction — a single source's own uncertainty — but flagged so the
   Writer does not silently pick "16 October" as if it were certain.

2. **The 1745 charter and "Laws of Yale College."** The roster's key-documents cell for
   `yale-college` names "Yale charter 1701; laws of 1745." Only the 1701 Charter was located
   and fetched this session, reprinted in full in `dexter1916`. The volume fetched (Dexter,
   1916) is subtitled "under the Original Charter... 1701-1745" and its latest dated item is
   18 April 1745 (Item CCXVIII), before the final 1745 Act was passed that May; the volume
   therefore records the Trustees reading and referring "A Draught of a new Charter for Yale
   College" (12 Sept. 1744, quoted at yale-college-q014's surrounding context) but not the
   final adopted 1745 charter text itself, and no distinct 1745 "Laws" pamphlet was located.
   See sources.csv rows `yale-laws-1745` and `yale-charter-1745`. Flagged for a follow-up
   Scout/Fetcher pass (a likely second Dexter volume covering 1745 onward, or the Connecticut
   State Library's charter collection, would be the place to look).
