# Discrepancies — student-volunteer-movement

1. **Publication date of Mott's *Evangelization of the World in This Generation*.** The roster's
   known-facts sheet and general usage cite the book as 1900. The digitized copy actually
   fetched this session (document_id `mott1900`, Internet Archive identifier
   `evangelizationof00mottuoft`) carries a catalog record date of 1901, and the title page's
   OCR reads "iqoi" (an OCR misread of "1901," itself possibly a later printing/reissue year
   rather than the first edition). Both years are recorded here; the chapter follows the
   roster's 1900 for the first edition while noting the fetched copy is dated 1901 on its own
   title page. Not resolved; a human with access to a first-edition copy (or WorldCat/OCLC
   record) should confirm which year belongs to the true first printing.

2. **Statistical tables in `cleveland1891` (1891 Report) not usable at the level of individual
   cells.** The 1891 Executive Committee report includes several numbered statistical tables --
   "Distribution of volunteers according to stages of preparation" (twelve categories including
   "Sailed," "Deceased," "Renounced," etc.), "Distribution of volunteers according to age," and
   "Distribution of volunteers who have sailed according to fields" (destinations by continent
   and region). The source PDF's page-image layout put each number in its own table cell; the
   Internet Archive's OCR (djvu text) breaks these tables into a long sequence of isolated
   digits and short fragments on separate lines with no reliable way to recover which number
   belongs to which row and column with confidence in this session. Only the clean, single-line
   total ("Total number of volunteers .... 6,200") could be read with confidence, and even that
   was not used as a formal quotation because the surrounding table cells could not be
   verified; the dossier instead relies on the continuous-prose "Achievements" section (items
   1-3, quote_ids svm-q007/q008/q009), which restates the volunteer count (6,000/6,200) and the
   number sailed (320) in ordinary sentences. This means: (a) no per-region destination
   breakdown (dossier field S2) could be stated with a citation; (b) no casualty/death figure
   (dossier field S5) could be stated with a citation, even though the table appears to include
   a "Deceased" row. A human with access to a cleaner scan or a different OCR engine should
   re-transcribe these specific tables (pp. ~29-31 of the 1891 Report) before any specific
   destination or casualty number is added to this dossier or the chapter.

3. **"Chairman" vs. "Moderator" as John R. Mott's convention title (1891).** The Cleveland 1891
   Report's own list of "Officers of the Convention" gives Mott's title as "Moderator," but the
   text of the opening session records: "The Convention was called to order by Mr. John R. Mott,
   Chair-[man]." Both usages appear in the same primary document. The chapter does not resolve
   this as a contradiction (the two words plausibly describe the same presiding function) but
   notes it here per Rule 9, and does not claim Mott held the separate, later-attested title of
   "Chairman of the Executive Committee" of the SVM on the strength of this convention-officer
   title alone; the dossier's R2/F3 entries instead cite only what the 1892 History states
   directly -- that Mott was appointed the YMCA's representative on the Executive Committee in
   1888-89 and had held that position continuously as of 1892.
