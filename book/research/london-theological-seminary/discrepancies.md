# Discrepancies — london-theological-seminary

1. **"Two-year course" claim not found in any fetched document.** The roster
   (`02_institution_roster.md`) and the task brief both describe LTS as offering "a two-year
   course," and the known-facts sheet lists this under "LTS's founding position... [VERIFY
   against LTS documents]." Lloyd-Jones's own inaugural address, the single primary document
   located and fully read this session, does not state a course length anywhere in its text.
   No secondary source fetched this session states a course length either. Status: UNVERIFIED.
   The chapter will not state a specific course length as fact; it will note the sources are
   silent on this point, per the Editor's instruction in Procedure ED step 4.

2. **First Principal: Hywel Jones (1977) vs a WebSearch-only claim of 1985.** A WebSearch
   summary (not a fetched, opened document) suggested Hywel Jones "became its first principal
   in 1985," implying someone else held the post 1977-1985. Against this, a fetched Evangelical
   Now article on the Seminary's 40th anniversary (2017) explicitly names "The first Principal
   was Hywel Jones" in a list running Hywel Jones, then Philip Eveson, then Robert Strivens
   (who was outgoing at the time of the article), then Bill James from January 2018 -- four
   Principals across 40 years (1977-2017), which is consistent with Hywel Jones serving from
   near the founding, not from 1985. Per Rule 2 (a fact must come from an opened source, not a
   search-engine summary), this dossier follows the fetched Evangelicals Now article: Hywel
   Jones as first Principal. The unconfirmed 1985 claim is recorded here for transparency and
   is NOT used in the chapter.

3. **Course length, subscription formula, and the original 1977 prospectus/constitution text
   remain unlocated.** The roster's "LTS Aims and Prospectus (1977)" and the Scout ticket's
   target ("find the LTS 1977 prospectus/aims and the opening address") were only half
   satisfied this session: the opening address (Lloyd-Jones's inaugural address) was found in
   full, but a separate written prospectus/aims document, and the Seminary's own constitution
   (quoted only at second hand, in one phrase, by a 2017 Evangelical Times article -- see
   dossier F5, quote_id lts-q023), were not independently located as digitized primary texts.
   This affects dossier fields F8 (full doctrinal basis/subscription formula) and C1 (course
   length). Flagged as a follow-up Fetcher target: the LTS/London Seminary office may hold a
   copy of the original 1977 prospectus and constitution.
