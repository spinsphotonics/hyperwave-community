Our Lecturers

London Seminary’s lecturers have decades of experience as church pastors and connect every element of their teaching to the lives of people in churches across all kinds of contexts and cultures, locally, nationally, and globally

London Seminary provides robust theological training for pastors and preachers. We are an evangelical seminary in London, UK.
