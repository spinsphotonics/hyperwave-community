# Discrepancies — moody-bible-institute

1. **Founding date: 1886 vs 1887.** The roster's known-facts sheet gives "1886 (society); 1889
   (institute opened)" in one place (02_institution_roster.md Part IV table row) and "society
   1887; building opened 1889" in the Known-facts sheet (section 3). The two sources fetched
   this session give a compatible but not identical sequence: William R. Moody's biography
   narrates Moody's fund-raising appeals and the chartering of "The Chicago Evangelization
   Society" as occurring before his four-month Chicago campaign that began 31 December 1886
   [moody-bible-institute-q002], which implies the charter itself was granted at some point in
   1886 or very early 1887, but no source read this session states the exact incorporation date
   (commonly given elsewhere as 5 February 1887, per web search snippets — see sources.csv;
   NOT independently confirmed from a fetched primary or quoted-primary text this session).
   Norman H. Camp's 1915 sketch narrates the same fund-raising and chartering sequence without
   giving any date for the incorporation itself, only that Moody's 22 January 1886 "City
   Evangelism" address preceded it [moody-bible-institute-q011, moody-bible-institute-q012].
   Recommendation: state "1886-87" or "chartered as the Chicago Evangelization Society following
   Moody's 1886 fund-raising appeals" rather than asserting 5 February 1887 as a fact, since no
   source read this session gives that specific date. Do not state "5 February 1887" in the
   chapter without a citation to a source actually read.

2. **Emma Dryer's role.** The roster names "Emma Dryer" as a founder alongside Moody and Torrey.
   Neither fetched source (the 1900 biography or the 1915 historical sketch) mentions her by
   name (checked by direct text search of both fetched files: zero hits for "Dryer"). This does
   not mean her role is false — only that it was not confirmed by either source read this
   session. A dedicated Scout pass for material on Emma Dryer (who, per widely repeated
   secondary/tertiary accounts not verified this session, ran Moody's earlier "May Institute"
   Bible-work classes for women in Chicago in the early 1880s) is needed before any sentence
   about her specific contribution can be written into the chapter. Status: UNVERIFIED.
