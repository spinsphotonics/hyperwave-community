# Discrepancies — aim-sim

1. **Publication date of `sim-burden-of-sudan`.** Internet Archive catalogues identifier `burdenofsudansto00suda_0` with `date: 1900`. The pamphlet's own internal content refers to events as late as 1920 (a table of stations opened through 1920; see text/sim-burden-of-sudan.txt) and lists "Bible Training Institute, Glasgow" among British referees. The printing used for this chapter is therefore later than 1900, likely c. 1920-1921; it cannot be a document actually printed in 1900, three years before the Sudan expedition it describes even took place (1893, described as a past event "led by Messrs. Gowans, Kent and Bingham"). Both the catalogue date and the internal evidence are recorded; the catalogue date is not used as the printing date in the chapter.

2. **Spelling of "Gowans" vs "Gowan."** `sim-burden-of-sudan` (Bingham's own narrative) consistently spells the name "Gowans." `sim-principles-practice` (1958), Article 1, spells it "Gowan" ("formed in 1893 by Messrs. Gowan, Kent and Bingham"; quote_id aim-sim-q013). Both spellings are recorded; the roster and secondary sources (Fiedler, Wikipedia, DACB) use "Gowans," which this chapter follows in narrative prose while quoting the 1958 document's own spelling exactly where quoted.

3. **AIM founding date: 1895 vs the roster's earlier lineage-map reference to Scott's illness "1892."** No discrepancy in the sources actually read this session; both `aim-leaflets` and secondary web material agree Scott's first Congo service and 1892 illness preceded the 1895 sailing. Recorded here only to note that F2 in the dossier follows the 1895 sailing date, consistent with the roster's `[HIGH]` marking.

4. **AIM candidate age ceiling ("must not be over thirty").** This rule appears in a leaflet inside the undated bound volume `aim-leaflets`, which mixes material from across several decades (internal dates from 1895 to 1952 appear in different leaflets). The specific leaflet giving this rule is not independently dated within the source; it is presented in the dossier and chapter as an undated statement of AIM practice, not tied to 1895 itself.

5. **No AIM constitution text of 1895 or SIM "1898 reorganization principles" (as named in the roster's Key Primary Documents cell) was located and fetched this session.** What was found and fetched instead is (a) a mid-20th-century AIM leaflet quoting the constitution's doctrinal/faith-basis summary, and (b) the SIM Principles and Practice as revised in 1958, which is explicitly a modernized, corporately merged document (see charter_abridged.md headnote) rather than the 1893/1898 founding text. This gap is recorded, not silently filled.
