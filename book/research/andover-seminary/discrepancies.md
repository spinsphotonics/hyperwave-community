# Discrepancies — andover-seminary

1. **Running heads inconsistent between "ADDITIONAL STATUTES" and "ASSOCIATE STATUTES."**
   Woods 1885's documentary appendix prints a continuous block of statute text (pp. 247-269
   of the printed book) whose running head reads "ADDITIONAL STATUTES" on pp. 247-255 and
   "ASSOCIATE STATUTES" on later pages of the same apparent span, before the whole appendix
   moves on to "LAWS OF THE THEOLOGICAL INSTITUTION" (p. 270 onward). Woods's own prefatory
   "Explanation of Words and Phrases" (quote_id andover-seminary-q016, andover-seminary-q017)
   distinguishes two separate 1808 instruments: the "Additional Statutes" (executed 3 May
   1808 by the original Founders -- Phoebe Phillips, John Phillips, Samuel Abbot -- and
   containing the Associate Creed) and the "Associate Statutes" (executed 21 March 1808 by
   the Associate Founders -- Moses Brown, William Bartlett, John Norris -- governing the
   Associate Professors and funds specifically). The Extractor was not able, within this
   session, to locate a clean internal boundary marker in the OCR text separating the two
   instruments from each other inside the single continuous appendix block. The Associate
   Creed itself (quoted at andover-seminary-q008, andover-seminary-q009) appears within the
   text under the "ADDITIONAL STATUTES" running head, consistent with Woods's own statement
   (item 7) that the Creed belongs to the Additional Statutes of 3 May 1808 -- this dossier
   follows that attribution. A human reviewer with the original 1808 pamphlet or a cleaner
   scan should confirm the exact page boundary between the two instruments.

2. **"Constitution of the Society of the Brethren" (1808), named in the roster as a possible
   fourth key document [VERIFY], was not found.** A string search of both fetched Andover
   texts (woods1885, rowe1933) for "Society of the Brethren" and "BRETHREN" returned zero
   hits in either file. This does not disprove the roster's claim (Anderson's 1861 ABCFM
   Memorial Volume, fetched under the `abcfm` folder, does discuss "The Brethren" as a
   student missionary society transferred from Williams College to Andover in 1808/1810 --
   see abcfm/quotes.jsonl) -- only that its own constitutional text was not located in the
   two Andover institutional histories read this session. Status: NOT FOUND (searched:
   woods1885, rowe1933). See also abcfm/discrepancies.md for the cross-reference.

3. **Exact date of the Seminary's opening/first instruction is not pinned down by a single
   quoted passage this session.** Woods 1885 gives several dates in close succession (the
   Founders' subscription of the original Constitution, 31 August 1807; the Associate
   Statutes, 21 March 1808; the Additional Statutes uniting the two foundations and the
   Creed, 3 May 1808), but the Extractor did not locate and formally quote a single passage
   stating the date instruction actually began or the first class convened. The roster's
   "1808 [HIGH]" founding-date label is consistent with the documented 1808 unification of
   the two foundations, but this dossier does not assert a specific day for "opening" beyond
   the dated instruments themselves. See dossier field F2.
