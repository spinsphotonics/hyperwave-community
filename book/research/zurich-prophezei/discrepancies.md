# Discrepancies — zurich-prophezei

1. **Founding date: 19 June vs 19 July 1525.** Two sources fetched this session agree on
   19 June 1525: the University of Zurich's own 2025 Faculty of Theology feature states
   "Monday, 19 June 1525, 8:00 am" (`uzh-news-prophezey`, quote_id zurich-prophezei-q015),
   and a blog post quoting Bruce Gordon's 2021 scholarly biography *Zwingli: God's Armed
   Prophet* (p.142) states "The sessions began on 19 June 1525 in the Grossmunster"
   (`adfontes-prophezei`, quote_id zurich-prophezei-q019). Against this, the 1858 English
   translation of Christoffel's *Zwingli* states the new service "came into existence on
   the 19th July 1525" (`christoffel1858`, quote_id zurich-prophezei-q001). This book's
   roster (`02_institution_roster.md`) already lists 19 June 1525 as `[HIGH]` confidence.
   Given that two independent, more recent, directly-dated sources (one an institutional
   2025 account, one quoting a named 2021 academic monograph by page number) agree on
   June, and 19 June 1525 was in fact a Monday (consistent with the UZH source's own
   day-of-week claim, checkable independently against a perpetual calendar) while 19 July
   1525 was a Wednesday, this session treats "19 June 1525" as the better-supported date
   and follows the roster. The Christoffel translation's "19th July" is recorded here,
   not silently corrected or dropped, per Rule 9. Do not treat this as fully resolved
   without checking a primary Zurich Council register or Bullinger's *Diarium* directly
   (not achieved this session — see sources.csv, `heinrichbulling00bullgoog` row).

2. **Time of the German sermon: morning ("at nine") vs "9:00 pm."** Christoffel's 1858
   narrative places the sermon shortly after the roughly hour-long morning exposition
   that began at eight o'clock: "In the meantime, the congregation had assembled to hear
   the sermon at nine" (quote_id zurich-prophezei-q005) — read in context as 9:00 am,
   immediately following the 8:00 am start of the Latin/Hebrew/Greek exposition. The 2025
   UZH source states instead: "Historical sources indicate that a service for ordinary
   people was always held at 9:00 pm, after the seminars had finished for the day"
   (quote_id zurich-prophezei-q018). These cannot both be correct as stated (an interval
   of roughly one hour vs. roughly thirteen hours after the 8:00 am start). This may be a
   translation slip in the UZH article's English rendering (am/pm) rather than a genuine
   historical disagreement, but this session has no way to confirm that without the UZH
   article's original-language source or a primary record, so both statements are
   recorded here rather than one being silently preferred. The chapter should state the
   sermon followed the exposition "shortly afterward," per Christoffel and per the blog
   quoting Gordon (which also places Leo Jud's sermon immediately "when [Zwingli] had
   finished" the exposition, with no time given), and should not assert a specific clock
   time without further verification.

3. **Which day(s) were excluded from the daily exercise.** The blog post quoting Gordon
   states sessions "took place every day of the week except Fridays and Sundays" (quote_id
   zurich-prophezei-q020), and the 2025 UZH source agrees: "day by day, apart from Friday,
   which was market day, and Sunday" (quote_id zurich-prophezei-q015). Grob's 1883
   narrative, independently, states "a daily biblical discourse, with the exception of
   Friday, in the chapter-house" (quote_id zurich-prophezei-q013) and does not mention
   Sunday at all — not a contradiction (Grob may simply not have mentioned the obvious
   exclusion of the Lord's Day), but recorded here because Procedure E requires every
   disagreement or partial silence between sources on a specific, checkable fact to be
   logged rather than harmonized by the Extractor. The chapter states "except Fridays and
   Sundays" on the strength of the two sources that state it explicitly.
