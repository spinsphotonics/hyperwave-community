# Discrepancies — london-missionary-society

1. **"Fundamental Principle" dated 1796 (roster) vs. the specific minute-book date, 9 May 1796.**
   No contradiction: Lovett dates the adopting meeting precisely to "the meeting of Directors
   held at the Castle and Falcon on May 9, 1796" [lms-q004 context], consistent with the
   roster's "(1796)" label; this dossier records the fuller date as attested.

2. **Two roster documents not independently located as standalone texts this session:**
   Bogue's Gosport Academy lecture syllabus, and the LMS's "Instructions" to departing
   missionaries. Lovett Vol. I (document_id `lovett1`) was read this session for its 1795-96
   founding chapters only (the Society's origin, the 21-25 September 1795 meetings, the Plan
   of the Society, and the 9 May 1796 fundamental-principle minute); its later chapters
   almost certainly discuss Bogue's Gosport Academy (named as the Society's training
   arrangement for missionaries) and individual sets of Instructions (e.g. to the *Duff*
   party of 1796), but these were not reached and no standalone text was independently
   fetched and quoted. See `sources.csv` rows `lovett1-bogue-syllabus` and
   `lovett1-instructions`. Marked NOT FETCHED rather than guessed at.

3. **OCR misprint of the Roman numeral "II" as "H." in the Plan of the Society.**
   The OCR of the Plan's Article II heading reads "H. T/ie Object" for what the 1795 original
   printed as "II. *The Object*" [lms-q002]. Left uncorrected in the quote record per Rule 9;
   the dossier's own prose refers to it as "Article II" outside quotation marks.

4. **OCR error rate on `lovett1`.** Spot-checked at 0-1 errors per 100 words on the three
   pages sampled (the Plan of the Society and fundamental-principle passages) — well under
   the 5/100 threshold; no re-transcription needed for this document.
