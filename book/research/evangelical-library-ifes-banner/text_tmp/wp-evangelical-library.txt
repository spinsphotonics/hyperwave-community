The **Evangelical Library** in Bounds Green,
[London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London "London"), is a lending library
for Christian ministers, students and other lay-persons. For many years the
library was located on [Chiltern
Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiltern_Street "Chiltern Street") in
central London. It moved to [North
London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_London "North London") in
mid-2009.[1]

The Library was founded through the vision of Geoffrey Williams (1886–1975) to
be a library that offered the very best of
[Protestant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protestant "Protestant")
[evangelical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evangelicalism "Evangelicalism")
resources. In this aim he was much encouraged in 1938 by the then associate
minister of [Westminster
Chapel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westminster_Chapel "Westminster
Chapel"), Dr [Martyn Lloyd-Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martyn_Lloyd-
Jones "Martyn Lloyd-Jones"). Before the move in 1944 to its Chiltern Street
location close to [Baker Street Underground
station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baker_Street_tube_station "Baker Street
tube station"), it was known as the **Beddington Free Grace Library** , based
in the tiny village of [Beddington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beddington
"Beddington"), [Surrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surrey "Surrey").

The library is home to more than 80,000 evangelical books. It also has an
extensive archive of several evangelical periodicals. It hosts an annual
lecture and publishes a regular magazine called _In Writing_ (formerly known
as _The Evangelical Library Bulletin_).

Following a major refurbishment in 2005, the library now has a reference room,
known as the _Robert Sheehan Puritan and Research Centre_ ,[2] named in honour
of the late [Robert J.
Sheehan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_J._Sheehan?action=edit&redlink=1
"Robert J. Sheehan \(page does not exist\)") (1951–97), the former pastor of
[Welwyn Evangelical
Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welwyn_Evangelical_Church?action=edit&redlink=1
"Welwyn Evangelical Church \(page does not exist\)").

It has links with a number of similar libraries worldwide, which it also
supports.
