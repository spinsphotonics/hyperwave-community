The **International Fellowship of Evangelical Students** (**IFES**) is an
[interdenominational](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interdenominationalism
"Interdenominationalism") association of 180
[evangelical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evangelicalism "Evangelicalism")
[Christian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christianity "Christianity") student
movements worldwide, encouraging
[evangelism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evangelism "Evangelism"),
[discipleship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discipleship "Discipleship") and
[mission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_mission "Christian mission")
among students. The headquarters is in [Oxford,
England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford,_England "Oxford, England").

## History

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=International_Fellowship_of_Evangelical_Students&action=edit&section=1
"Edit section: History")]

In [United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom "United
Kingdom"), the [Oxford Inter-Collegiate Christian
Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_Inter-Collegiate_Christian_Union
"Oxford Inter-Collegiate Christian Union"), founded in
[1879](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1879 "1879"), was a founding member of
the [Student Christian Movement of Great
Britain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Student_Christian_Movement_of_Great_Britain
"Student Christian Movement of Great Britain") (SCM) in
[1892](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1892 "1892").[1][2] Then in 1928, CICCU
students left the SCM because of its
[ecumenical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecumenical "Ecumenical") beliefs
and its approval of new methods of [biblical
exegesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biblical_exegesis "Biblical
exegesis"), and founded the [Inter-Varsity Christian
Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universities_and_Colleges_Christian_Fellowship
"Universities and Colleges Christian Fellowship").[3] This movement was
established in Canada in 1928, Australia in 1930, and in the United States in
1941.[4]

In 1947, the International Fellowship of Evangelical Students was founded.[5]
Representatives from [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia
"Australia"), the [United
Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom "United Kingdom"),
[Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada "Canada"),
[China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China "China"),
[Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands "Netherlands"),
[Norway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norway "Norway"),
[France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France "France"), [New
Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand "New Zealand"),
[Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland "Switzerland"), and
the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States "United
States") came together in
[Boston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston,_Massachusetts "Boston,
Massachusetts") (USA) to strengthen evangelism, discipleship and world mission
among students all over the world. [6]

It was present in 180 countries in 2023. [7]

## Programs

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=International_Fellowship_of_Evangelical_Students&action=edit&section=2
"Edit section: Programs")]

Group meetings are held each week to discuss the
[Bible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bible "Bible") in universities.[8] These
groups are [Evangelical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evangelical
"Evangelical"), but not attached to any particular
[confession](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confession "Confession").[9] IFES
faces a variety of challenges due to the different contexts in which it works
with college students. [10]

## Organization

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=International_Fellowship_of_Evangelical_Students&action=edit&section=3
"Edit section: Organization")]

Representatives of all the member movements gather once every four years at
the "World Assembly", where the General Committee meets to conduct official
business of the fellowship.[11] IFES's General Secretaries have included:[12]

  * Stacey Woods (1947–1972)
  * [Chua Wee Hian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chua_Wee_Hian "Chua Wee Hian") (1972–1991)
  * Lindsay Brown (1991–2007)[13]
  * Daniel Bourdanné (2007–2019)
  * Jamil (acting, 2019–2020)
  * Tim Adams (2021–present)[14]

## Publishing houses

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=International_Fellowship_of_Evangelical_Students&action=edit&section=4
"Edit section: Publishing houses")]

Christian literature distribution is also a feature of this ministry.
[InterVarsity Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/InterVarsity_Press
"InterVarsity Press") (USA), [Inter-Varsity
Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inter-Varsity_Press "Inter-Varsity
Press") (UK), _Presses Bibliques Africaines_ (Francophone Africa), Harmat
(Hungary), Andamio (Spain), and _Ediciones Certeza_ (Latin America) are
examples of regional publishing houses.

### Partnerships

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=International_Fellowship_of_Evangelical_Students&action=edit&section=5
"Edit section: Partnerships")]

[Tyndale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyndale_House "Tyndale House") is a
Christian publishing house, publishing Christian fiction, nonfiction,
children’s books amongst others.[15] Tyndale has supported IFES since 1967,
through Tyndale House Foundation and helps establish IFES-affiliated
publishing programs in other countries including Pakistan, Malaysia, Hong
Kong, the Philippines, Brazil, Uganda, India and the United States. These
programs enable students to write and publish their own brochures, books and
curriculums, and to serve their local campuses.[16]
