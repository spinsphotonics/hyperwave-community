# Discrepancies — serampore-college

1. **Exact date of the 1827 Danish Royal Charter.** The roster's known-facts sheet gives "Danish royal
   charter 1827 [HIGH]." A WebSearch snippet of the Senate of Serampore College's own website
   (not independently fetched and read this session — see sources.csv row
   senate-royal-charter-page) states the charter was issued "23 February 1827" and quotes its
   opening words ("We Frederick the Sixth, by the Grace of God King of Denmark..."). Neither
   Marshman 1859 nor Smith 1885, the two sources actually read this session, gives a specific
   calendar date for the charter's issue; Smith 1885 dates Marshman's visit to Copenhagen and the
   royal audience to 1826, and states the charter received royal sanction and was "engrossed on
   vellum" before Marshman's departure. Status: the year 1827 is corroborated by two sources;
   the specific day (23 February) rests only on an unverified search snippet and should not be
   stated in the chapter without a source actually opened and read.

2. **Names on the charter as incorporators.** The same unverified search snippet names "William
   Carey and Joshua Marshman, Doctors of Divinity, and John Clark Marshman, Esq." as the parties
   to whom the charter was granted. Marshman 1859 and Smith 1885, read this session, describe
   Joshua Marshman as the sole negotiator present in Copenhagen and speak of "the missionaries"
   or "the Brotherhood" (implicitly Carey, Marshman, Ward) as beneficiaries, without listing John
   Clark Marshman by name as a charter party in either quoted passage. This is not a contradiction
   so much as an unconfirmed addition; do not name John Clark Marshman as a charter party in the
   chapter without a source read this session that says so.

3. **The 1818 Prospectus and 1827 Charter texts themselves were not located as digitized full texts.**
   Every quotation in this dossier from the Prospectus or the Charter is quoted at second hand,
   inside a 19th-century secondary narrative (Marshman 1859) or biography (Smith 1885), rather
   than from the founding document itself. This differs from the ideal described in
   `03_research_protocol.md` (work from the Fetcher's own transcription of the primary document).
   It follows the same "charter-in-substance" fallback used for `geneva-academy`. A follow-up
   Fetcher ticket should attempt: (a) a JS-capable fetch of
   https://senateofseramporecollege.edu.in/royal-charter/, which the college's own website
   states carries the full charter text; (b) Google Books/HathiTrust for a printed pamphlet
   edition of the Prospectus or the College Statutes; (c) George Smith's or Marshman's endnotes
   for a citation to a full reprint.
