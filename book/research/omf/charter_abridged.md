Producing a fresh abridgement of that same pre-1964 text under the `omf` slug, without any confirmed post-1964 revision to abridge instead, would duplicate the CIM chapter's charter section rather than showing what changed, and was judged not to serve this chapter's stated purpose.

**What is known about the post-1964 document, from a source that could not itself be opened.** A web search of omf.org turned up a page (title: "The Formation, Growth, and Development of the Hong Kong Council of the Overseas Missionary Fellowship") whose indexed snippet states that OMF Council members signed an agreement on the last page of a document titled "Principles and Practice of the OMF." Every attempt to fetch that page, or any other omf.org page, this session was blocked by an interactive bot-check (`sgcaptcha`) that `curl` cannot pass (WebFetch is unavailable per the task's tooling notes). This fact -- that a document called "Principles and Practice of the OMF" existed and was signed by Council members -- is recorded here for a future session's Scout step, not treated as a verified fact of this dossier, since it was never read directly.
