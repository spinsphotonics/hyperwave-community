# Discrepancies — columbia-bible-college

1. **No document from the founding decade (1923-1933) was located.** The earliest digitized primary
   material located and fetched this session is the 1939 yearbook, sixteen years after the 1923
   founding. The 1948 (25th-anniversary) yearbook does supply a first-person institutional account
   of the 1923 opening itself ("This is the place where, after more than four years of prayer and
   preparation, full time courses were begun..."), so the founding narrative is not itself missing,
   but it is a retrospective account published a quarter-century later, not a document contemporary
   with 1923. This is recorded as a gap rather than treated as equivalent to a founding-era source.

2. **"Mother and Daddy McQuilkin," the dedicatees of the 1952 Finial**, are described as having
   guided "the founding group... since its beginning" as "President of the Board of Directors."
   No source read this session gives a full name for this couple beyond "McQuilkin," and it is not
   established this session whether this refers to Robert C. McQuilkin (the college's president,
   1923-1952) and his wife under affectionate campus nicknames, or to a distinct relative who
   separately chaired the Board of Directors. Both readings remain open; the dossier does not
   assert which is correct, and quote cbc-q014 is used only for the general fact of continuous
   board leadership "since its beginning," not to identify the individual.
