## Headnote

No founding-era document survives in this partial abridgement. What follows is copied verbatim,
in its own original order, from the caption facing a photograph of the original one-room
classroom in *The Finial 1948* (Columbia Bible College, 1948) — English, as printed, digitized by
the Internet Archive, document_id `cbc-finial-1948`. `[...]` marks the one cut within the
passage (an intervening sentence about the college's subsequent year-by-year growth, retained in
full in the chapter's Founding section rather than here).

## Text

This is the place where, after more than four years of prayer and preparation, full time courses were begun at Columbia Bible College. Known then as Co- lumbia Bible School, its entire visible possessions were this one room furnished with a single hanging blackboard, an organ with a soap-box stool, and about ten chairs; one teacher, Mr. McQuilkin; and a handful of earnest Christian students who attended every class.

[...]

let us remember that these first faithful few planted, others since have watered; but then, as now, the Lord giveth the increase.
