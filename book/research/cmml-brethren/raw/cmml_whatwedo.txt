## What We Do

##### CMML advances the spread of the Gospel globally by serving US assemblies and their missionaries _.   _

#### 

Prayer 

CMML encourages prayer for more than 700 missionaries in more than 80 countries.

[Learn More](/pray)

Tax-Deductible Donations

CMML processes donations suggested for missionaries around the world.

[Learn More](/waystogive)

Missions Resources

CMML offers conferences, training, and literature to help you participate in the Great Commission.

[Learn More](/resources)

Missionary Care

CMML provides accommodations, clothing, support, and resources for missionaries.

[Learn More](/guesthome)

Partnerships

CMML maintains strategic partnerships to bolster our ability to serve. 

ACM HealthLine

ACM HealthLine is a free, telemedicine consultation service for North American full-time workers and their family members who serve around the world.

[Learn More](http://assemblycare.org/what-we-do)

Stewards Advisory Services

As a registered investment advisor, Stewards Advisory Services can assist missionaries, individuals, churches, ministry groups, and other organizations with a variety of services and provide a Christ-honoring perspective to help them thrive with the resources that God has entrusted to them.

[Learn More](https://stewardsadvisory.com/)

Legacy Ministry Services (formerly Believers Stewardship Services)

Legacy Ministry Services (formerly Believers Stewardship Services) helps Christians accomplish their financial and estate-planning goals in fulfilling biblical stewardship and supports local New Testament-patterned churches and associated ministries with financial and governance-related matters.

[Learn More](https://legacyministryservices.com/contact/)
