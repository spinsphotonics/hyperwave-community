# Discrepancies — cmml-brethren

1. **Headquarters location: Spring Lake vs. Wall Township, New Jersey.** The roster
   (`02_institution_roster.md`, Part V table) gives CMML's place as "US (Spring Lake, NJ)."
   CMML's own website, in its history page, states: "In 1972, CMML purchased the CMML Guest Home
   located in Wall Township, New Jersey... This property remains the headquarters for CMML, Inc.
   to this day" [cmml-brethren-q026]. Spring Lake and Wall Township are adjacent New Jersey
   municipalities (Wall Township borders Spring Lake), so this may be a mailing-address versus
   physical-property difference, or the roster may be using an older or informal place-name for
   the same or a nearby location. Not resolved; both values are recorded here rather than one
   being silently corrected.

2. **Exact year of Wheaton catalog / 1940s statement of faith not obtained.** The roster's key
   primary documents for `wheaton-college` include "statement of faith (1926) [VERIFY]" and a
   "1940s catalog." A WebSearch summary (not a fetched or opened document) gave the Statement of
   Faith's authorship as Charles Blanchard and its date as either 1924 or 1926 in different
   sentences of the same AI-generated search summary. Neither wheaton.edu (Cloudflare-gated) nor
   a2z.my.wheaton.edu (Wheaton's own history-wiki page for the Statement of Faith; TLS connection
   failure through the proxy) could be fetched this session, so the actual 1924/1926 text was not
   read. This affects the `wheaton-college` dossier's F8 field directly; it is logged here as well
   because CMML's own current (2021) Statement of Faith is analogously undated-in-origin from what
   this session could verify, and neither institution's exact historic (1940s/1950s) confession
   was confirmed against a primary document.

3. **George Müller connection to Groves's "Christian Devotedness" — sourcing gap, not a factual
   contradiction.** A WebSearch results summary (returned by the WebSearch tool itself, not the
   text of any page this session fetched and opened) stated that Groves's 1825 booklet "influenced
   George Müller toward his lifelong principle of faith." This claim was not found within the
   Groves text itself (which does not mention Müller) nor within any other document fetched and
   read this session. It is plausible and is widely repeated in general Brethren-movement
   historiography, but per Rule 1 it is not stated as verified fact in the dossier (F9); logged
   here so a future research pass knows to look for a primary or properly-read secondary source
   (e.g., Müller's own *Narrative*, or Dann's biography of Groves, neither of which was located as
   a digitized full text this session — see sources.csv).
