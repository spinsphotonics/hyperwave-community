# Discrepancies — abcfm

1. **Founding date: 29 June 1810 (roster [HIGH]) vs. the Board's 1811 second meeting and June
   1812 incorporation, all attested in the same sources.** Anderson 1861 and firstten1834
   together document a multi-stage founding, not a single date: the Board was "instituted"
   at Bradford on 29 June 1810 (per the roster and consistent with Anderson's own retrospect,
   quote_id abcfm-q011 dossier field F2 discussion); the Board held a second meeting in 1811
   at which it corresponded with the Presbyterian General Assembly; the Assembly's reply is
   dated 2 June 1812; and the Massachusetts Act of Incorporation itself is referenced (not
   independently fetched as a standalone text this session, see sources.csv) as occurring in
   1812. This dossier records the sequence rather than collapsing it to one date; no
   contradiction with the roster's "29 June 1810 [HIGH]" label, which names the founding
   meeting specifically.

2. **The Constitution/Act of Incorporation's full text was not independently located.** The
   roster names "Constitution (1810/1812)" as a key document. This session located and
   quoted its purpose clause only, as quoted within Anderson 1861's narrative ("for
   propagating the gospel in heathen lands by supporting missionaries and diffusing a
   knowledge of the Holy Scriptures"), not a standalone digitized copy of the incorporation
   instrument itself. Per the E-abcfm ticket instruction ("Charter = 1812 Instructions to
   Judson's party. Extract the ABCFM constitution's purpose clause separately."), this
   dossier follows that split: the chapter's charter document is the 1812 Instructions
   (firstten1834, quoted in full in charter_abridged.md), and the Constitution's purpose
   clause is recorded separately in dossier field F6, sourced to the Anderson quotation
   rather than to an independently verified original. See sources.csv row
   `abcfm-1810-1812-constitution-standalone`.

3. **"Constitution of the Society of the Brethren" cross-reference.** See
   `andover-seminary/discrepancies.md` item 2. Anderson 1861 names the Brethren society and
   quotes its stated object (quote_id abcfm-q014) but its own separate constitutional text
   was not located as a standalone document in either the `abcfm` or `andover-seminary`
   research sessions.

4. **OCR name-spelling artifacts.** The OCR of anderson1861 twice renders "Adoniram" as
   "Adqniram" and inserts a stray underscore in "Samuel_ Nott" (quote_id abcfm-q012). These
   are left uncorrected in the quote record per Rule 9/the Fetcher instruction not to
   correct source text; the dossier's own prose uses the standard spelling "Adoniram Judson"
   outside quotation marks.
