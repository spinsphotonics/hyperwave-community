**Scoping note (read before the outline).** This book collects charters of *schools and missionary societies*. The First Book of Discipline is a national church-order document covering nine Heads (Doctrine, Sacraments, Idolatry, Ministers, Ministers'/Poor Provision including Schools, Kirk Patrimony, Ecclesiastical Discipline, Elders and Deacons, Policy of the Kirk), of which only one Head — the Fifth, specifically its subsection "For the Schollis" (education) — addresses this book's subject. The task and the roster's own "Key primary documents" cell for this institution direct attention "especially [to] the head 'Of Schools' / 'For the Universities.'" Consistent with the abridgement rules' priority list (title/preamble; doctrinal basis; admission; course of study; daily-life rules; teacher duties; examinations/degrees; financial principle only if doctrinal), this abridgement therefore keeps the Preface's statement of purpose and the First Head's doctrinal statement only briefly, for context, and then keeps the Schools/Universities material at length, while cutting the other eight Heads (Sacraments, Idolatry, Ministers' election, the general Ministers'/Poor Provision material outside Schools, Kirk Patrimony, Ecclesiastical Discipline, Elders/Deacons, Policy of the Kirk) as outside this chapter's scope. This is a scoping decision, not a claim that those Heads are unimportant to the document as a whole; a chapter on the First Book of Discipline as a *national church constitution* would abridge very differently. Within the kept Schools material, salary schedules and legal-privilege clauses are additionally cut as administrative detail per rule 6.3, except one illustrative number (the bursar quota) that speaks to the plan's universal-access principle.
