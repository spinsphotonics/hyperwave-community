# Discrepancies — church-missionary-society

1. **The 1799 founding pamphlet itself ("Account of the Society for Missions to Africa and
   the East") and a standalone Islington College prospectus were not independently located
   this session.** The roster names both as key documents; the roster also marks the
   Islington items "[VERIFY]." Stock Vol. I (document_id `stock1`) states that John Venn was
   "the author... of the Rules of the new Society, and of its first Account of itself for the
   public" and that the Rules were adopted at the 12 April 1799 meeting ("In the original
   Minutes the Eules follow" [near cms-q005/q006]), but Stock's own narrative does not reprint
   either the Account pamphlet or the Rules verbatim at the point read this session, and no
   separate digitized copy of either was located by web search. In their place, this dossier
   uses the four founding Resolutions of 12 April 1799 (quoted in full by Stock as direct
   reproductions of "the original Minutes") as the chapter's charter text, and John Venn's
   18 March 1799 principles (also quoted/summarized by Stock from the Minutes) for the
   Society's stated emphasis. See `sources.csv` rows `cms-account-1799-standalone` and
   `cms-islington-rules-standalone`. Marked NOT FETCHED rather than guessed at.

2. **OCR misspellings of key terms.** The OCR of `stock1` renders "Church-principle" as
   "Ghurch-iyrinciple" and "principle" (second instance) as "prin- cijjle" [cms-q008], and
   "Rules" as "Eules" throughout (a systematic R->E misreading, e.g. "Eev." for "Rev.,"
   "Eector" for "Rector," in cms-q001). Left uncorrected in the quote records per Rule 9; the
   dossier's own prose uses the standard spellings ("Church-principle," "Rules," "Rev.,"
   "Rector") outside quotation marks.

3. **Founding date: 1799 vs. Islington College's separate 1825 founding date.** No
   contradiction; the roster itself distinguishes "1799; college 1825." This dossier follows
   that distinction throughout (F2 records both dates).

4. **OCR error rate on `stock1`.** Spot-checked at 0-1 errors per 100 words on the three
   pages sampled (the four founding Resolutions and the Islington College opening/examination
   passages) — well under the 5/100 threshold; no re-transcription needed for this document.
