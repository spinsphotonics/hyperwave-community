# Abridged charter — Church Missionary Society (`church-missionary-society`)

**Source document:** the four founding Resolutions adopted at the public meeting of 12 April
1799, Castle and Falcon, Aldersgate Street, London, quoted by Eugene Stock as direct
reproductions "from the original Minutes," in *The History of the Church Missionary Society:
Its Environment, Its Men and Its Work*, Vol. I (London: CMS, 1899), pp. 68-69, document_id
`stock1`.

**Note on scope.** The roster's principal candidate for this chapter's charter, the 1799 pamphlet
*An Account of a Society for Missions to Africa and the East*, was not independently located or
fetched this session (see `discrepancies.md` item 1); nor was the Society's original Rules text,
referred to but not reprinted at this point in Stock's narrative. In their place, and per dossier
field F5, this abridgement uses the four Resolutions of 12 April 1799 -- the Society's actual
constituting act, quoted by Stock from the original Minutes. John Venn's address of 18 March
1799, which the dossier's field X3 also names as a candidate for reprinting, is Stock's own
narrative *summary* of Venn's three principles with short embedded quotations, not a verbatim
reproduction of a primary text comparable to the Resolutions; it is quoted directly, with
citations, in the chapter's Founding and Emphasis sections instead of here.

**Translation:** none needed (English original).

Stock's connecting narrative sentences and marginal running heads). **Abridgement word count:**

## Headnote

The four founding Resolutions of the Church Missionary Society, adopted 12 April 1799 at the
Castle and Falcon, Aldersgate Street, London, English, quoted here from Eugene Stock's 1899
history, which reproduces them "from the original Minutes."

## Text

[Resolution 1, adopted 12 April 1799.]

(1) "That it is a duty highly incumbent upon every Chris- tian to endeavour to propagate the
knowledge of the Gospel among the Heathen."

[...]

[Resolution 2, on the ground left unreached by the existing Church societies.]

(2) " That as it appears from the printed Reports of the Societies for Propagating the Gospel and
for Promoting Christian Know- [...] ledge that those respectable societies confine their labours
to the [...] British Plantations in America and to the West Indies,* there [...] seems to be
still wanting in the Established Church a society for [...] sending missionaries to the Continent
of Africa, or the other parts of the heathen world."

[...]

[Resolution 3, constituting the Society.]

(3) " That the persons present at this meeting do form them- selves into a Society for that
purpose, and that the following rules be adopted."

[In the original Minutes, the Rules referred to here follow; they were not independently located
and fetched this session -- see `discrepancies.md` item 1.]

[...]

[Resolution 4, directing the Society's first practical step.]

(4) " That a Deputation be sent from this Society to the Arch- bishop of Canterbury as
Metropolitan, the Bishop of London as Diocesan, and the Bishop of Durham as Chairman of the
Mission Committee of the Society for Promoting Christian Knowledge, with a copy of the Rules of
the Society and a respectful letter."
