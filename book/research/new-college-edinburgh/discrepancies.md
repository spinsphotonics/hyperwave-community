# Discrepancies — new-college-edinburgh

1. **Three distinct "inaugural"/"introductory" addresses, easily conflated.** The material
   read this session shows at least three separate occasions that a careless reading could
   collapse into one "1843 inaugural address":
   (a) The College's actual opening, November 1843, in the Brick Church, Castle Terrace,
   Edinburgh, at which Chalmers delivered what Brown's Annals calls "the inaugural address"
   (new-college-edinburgh-q005) — this is the event the roster's "Chalmers's inaugural
   address (1843)" almost certainly refers to, but only a short quoted fragment of its
   content was located this session (q007).
   (b) An annual "introductory address" Chalmers gave as Principal opening the new session in
   November 1846, in which he announced a short course of lectures on German philosophy
   (new-college-edinburgh-q016) — an ordinary session-opening lecture, not the founding event.
   (c) A separately published "Inaugural Address... delivered at the opening of the New
   College [building]" by Dr. Cunningham (referenced, not quoted, in Hanna's footnote,
   new-college-edinburgh-q015) — this appears to mark the opening of the permanent college
   building on the Mound, which was not completed until after Chalmers's 1847 death, when
   Cunningham had apparently become Principal. This address's own text was not located this
   session.
   The chapter must keep these three separate and not attribute (b) or (c)'s content to the
   1843 founding event. Status: the November 1843 date and the Brick Church, Castle Terrace
   venue for the founding opening are corroborated across both sources read this session
   [HIGH within this session's sources]; the full text of that specific 1843 address is
   UNVERIFIED / NOT FOUND beyond the short quoted fragment.

2. **Full text of Chalmers's 1843 inaugural address not located.** The roster names this as a
   key primary document. WebSearch (multiple queries) and a curl attempt against one secondary
   PDF (Stewart J. Brown, "The Making of New College 1843-46") did not turn up a digitized
   standalone text of the address, and the PDF fetch itself failed (108-byte error response,
   not a valid PDF; see sources.csv row brown-stewart-making-of-new-college). Only the fragment
   quoted in Brown's Annals (citing Assembly Proceedings, 1843, Glasgow, p. 170) was found and
   verified. Flagged for a follow-up Scout/Fetcher pass against the Free Church of Scotland's
   own Assembly Proceedings for 1843 and New College Library / National Library of Scotland
   holdings.

3. **Free Church "Act anent the College."** The roster names this document with a [VERIFY]
   flag. WebSearch found only unrelated Free Church Acts (an 1846 "Act anent Questions and
   Formula" and an 1892 "Act anent Confession of Faith"), neither confirmed to be an Act
   specifically establishing or governing the College. No primary text of any Act specifically
   titled or plausibly matching "anent the College" was located this session. Status: NOT
   FOUND (searched: WebSearch queries recorded in sources.csv row
   freechurch-act-anent-college). The chapter does not cite or rely on this document.
