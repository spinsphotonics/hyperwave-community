# Discrepancies — trevecca

1. **Fletcher's title at the College.** The roster states, as a [HIGH]-confidence fact, that
   John Fletcher of Madeley served as Trevecca's president. The one volume fetched this session
   documents Fletcher's close personal association with Lady Huntingdon and with Trevecca (he
   met her there in 1767 and conducted her to Madeley) and separately records, in its own table
   of contents, that he was "appointed Vicar of Madely" — a different, ordinary parish
   appointment. No passage located and quoted this session states that Fletcher held a formal
   title such as "president" at the College itself, nor does it describe how often he was
   actually present there once installed at Madeley (a separate parish some distance away). This
   chapter therefore reports Fletcher's association with Trevecca as the sources fetched this
   session support it, and marks his specific title UNVERIFIED rather than asserting "president"
   without a primary citation. A follow-up Fetcher pass against Seymour's Volume II, or the
   Cheshunt Foundation archive named in the roster, would be the place to look for confirmation.

2. **Passages read but not independently entered as quote records.** Three passages were read
   in full this session and are summarized in the dossier (the 1768 St Edmund Hall, Oxford
   expulsions and the reception of at least two of the expelled students at Trevecca; Mr. Edward
   Riddell's request that Lady Huntingdon send students to Hull; and the naming of "the first
   students at Trevecca who went down to Hull") without a dedicated quote_id isolating each one,
   because the surrounding paragraphs were long and the most load-bearing single sentences were
   prioritized for formal quote records instead. This is flagged here, not silently left
   unmarked: no fact in the dossier or chapter rests on these passages beyond what the paragraphs
   quoted above (trevecca-q005 through trevecca-q007) already support, but a follow-up Extractor
   pass should add discrete quote records for the Oxford-expulsion and Hull-request passages
   before this chapter's Admission or Sending sections are expanded further.
