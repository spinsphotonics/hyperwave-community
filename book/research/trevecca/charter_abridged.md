## Editorial notes on the transcription

- OCR artifacts are reproduced exactly as scanned, per Rule 7 (modernize only u/v, i/j, long s;
  never correct or expand words) — for example "bftth" (an OCR misreading of "hath"), "tho" and
  "tlie" (both misreadings of "the"), "Larvcst" (a misreading of "harvest"), and "arc" (a
  misreading of "are"). The printer's running page-header ("308 THE LIFE AND TIMES OF") and a
  page-signature mark ("X. 2") fall inside the kept passage and are left in place, exactly as
  scanned, rather than removed.

  discussed at length in this chapter's Fruit section) rather than the College's own purpose,
  the abridgement here departs from rule 8's "print whole" default for short documents and cuts
  more heavily, per the priority order in rule 6.2, keeping only the passage that states the
