# Discrepancies — wheaton-college

1. **Elisabeth Howard's Prairie Bible Institute span: "1948" vs "1948-49."** The roster's
   known-facts sheet gives "Prairie Bible Institute 1948-49 [VERIFY Prairie dates]." The Elisabeth
   Elliot Foundation's own timeline, the only source read this session that speaks to this
   directly, states only: "1948 — Elisabeth spends the year at Prairie Bible Institute in Alberta,
   Canada" [wheaton-college-q019], without stating whether her attendance extended into 1949. Both
   values are recorded; the chapter should say "1948" (what the fetched source actually states)
   and not silently extend it to "1948-49."

2. **No historical Statement of Faith text obtained; year uncertain even in secondary summaries.**
   The roster names "statement of faith (1926) [VERIFY]" as a key primary document. Neither
   wheaton.edu (Cloudflare JS challenge on every page tried) nor archives.wheaton.edu (Cloudflare
   Turnstile human-verification gate) nor a2z.my.wheaton.edu (Wheaton's own history wiki; TLS
   connection failure through the proxy on every attempt) could be fetched this session. A
   WebSearch results summary (not a document this session opened and read) gave the statement's
   authorship as Charles Blanchard and its date as either 1924 or 1926 in two different sentences
   of the same summary. Do not state a specific year or wording for the Statement of Faith in the
   chapter; state plainly that the sources available this session could not confirm it.

3. **"Foreign Missions Fellowship" constitution not located.** The roster names an "FMF
   constitution [VERIFY]" among Wheaton's key documents, and ticket E-wheaton-college specifically
   calls for extracting its "aims." No document by this name, or describing this specific student
   organization, was located or fetched this session; WebSearch for it returned only unrelated,
   present-day Wheaton pages. The 1904 catalog's "Student Missionary Department" (a formal
   scholarship arrangement with the American Sunday School Union) is a genuine, quoted 1904-era
   missionary-preparation program at Wheaton [wheaton-college-q007], but it is not the Foreign
   Missions Fellowship and long predates the Elliots; it should not be conflated with FMF in the
   chapter. This is flagged as a follow-up Fetcher ticket requiring either direct access to
   archives.wheaton.edu from a browser session (past its Turnstile gate) or a request to Wheaton
   College Archives & Special Collections.
