## OCR-quality note

This 1776 pamphlet's Internet Archive OCR is badly corrupted in numerous places: printers' decorative rules and running folios between paragraphs are rendered as strings of stray characters and digits, and the long s is inconsistently recognized (sometimes as "ſ", sometimes dropped). This session did not re-OCR the source or have access to the original page images to correct it. Per the book's Ten Rules ("never invent"), the text below is copied exactly as OCR'd, errors included; nothing has been silently corrected. Passages of pure scanning noise (no recoverable words) are cut and marked `[...]`, reason code `ocr-noise`, distinct from the `admin`/`repeat`/`names` cut reasons used elsewhere in this abridgement. Readers should treat single garbled words inside otherwise-legible sentences (e.g. "expetted" for "expected," "Pap/ift" for "Baptist," "Miss10nar1es" for "Missionaries") as OCR artifacts of a genuine 1776 print, not as errors introduced by this book.
