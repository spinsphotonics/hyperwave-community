# Discrepancies — westminster-chapel-fellowship

1. **Year Lloyd-Jones joined Westminster Chapel: 1938 vs 1939.** The roster's known-facts
   sheet states "Westminster Chapel 1938-68 (co-pastor with Campbell Morgan until 1943)" [HIGH],
   and Powell's 2007 memoir (eusebeia-powell-2007), narrating events "In September, 1938," and
   stating "Dr. Lloyd-Jones joined Campbell Morgan as Associate Minister in 1938" (quote_id
   wcf-q019), agrees with this. However, MLJ Trust's own "Meet Dr. Martyn Lloyd-Jones" page
   states "In 1939, Lloyd-Jones returned to London as associate pastor of Westminster Chapel"
   (quote_id wcf-q003), and the MLJ Trust FAQ page separately says his Westminster Chapel
   ministry lasted "almost 30 years" (quote_id wcf-q007) without giving a start year directly
   comparable. Both sources agree Morgan retired and Lloyd-Jones became sole minister in 1943.
   Status: the roster's [HIGH] 1938 date is followed in the chapter narrative, with the MLJ
   Trust 1939 date recorded here rather than silently dropped, per Rule 9 (record, do not
   resolve).

2. **Two different "Institute Hall" discussion formats, not reconciled by any single source.**
   Powell's 2007 memoir describes a Sunday-afternoon "Bible Study with discussion in the
   Institute Hall, often led by the Associate Minister," with a monthly voted topic (quote_ids
   wcf-q020, wcf-q021). MLJ Trust's page describing Fred Catherwood's tribute instead describes
   "Friday night discussions at the institute hall" led by Lloyd-Jones himself, using a
   Socratic question method (quote_ids wcf-q011, wcf-q012, wcf-q013). No source fetched this
   session states whether these are the same meeting recalled two different ways, two different
   regular meetings at the same venue, or a conflation somewhere in one of the secondary
   accounts. The roster's ticket brief itself asks for "the Friday discussion meetings" and
   "Friday-night discussion transcripts" as a key primary document target; no transcript of any
   such meeting was located as a digitized text this session. This is left as an open question
   for a human reviewer or a follow-up Fetcher pass, rather than resolved by assumption.

3. **Unverified attendance figure ("as many as 400 pastors").** A WebSearch summary (not a
   document this session fetched and opened) suggested Westminster Fellowship attendance reached
   "as many as 400 pastors" by the 1960s. Per Rule 2, no fact may be stated from a summary alone;
   this figure is NOT used anywhere in the dossier or intended chapter. It is recorded here only
   so a future researcher knows the figure was seen and deliberately excluded for lack of a
   primary or fetched-and-opened secondary citation.

4. **"Westminster Fellowship" name confusion risk (not a discrepancy in the sources, but a
   documented near-miss in this session's own research).** A WebSearch for "exercising ministry
   in a local church" phrase returned, alongside the correct Evangelical Times article about
   Lloyd-Jones's Westminster Fellowship, material about an unrelated, modern "Westminster
   Fellowship" of the Presbyterian Church in Ireland (a ministers' network with its own,
   differently worded aims). That PCI material was positively identified as a different
   organization and excluded entirely from this dossier. Recorded here as a documented
   near-miss so a future researcher checking this dossier's completeness does not re-introduce
   the PCI organization's material by mistake.
