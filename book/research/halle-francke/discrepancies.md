# Discrepancies — `halle-francke`

Format: field, source A (id, page), value A, source B (id, page), value B. Not resolved; recorded for human review per Rule 9.

1. **Founding year of the "institution" as a whole.** The roster row (`02_institution_roster.md`) gives "1698" for the Halle foundation and "1706" for the mission. The primary text read this session (Francke's own *Pietas Hallensis*) instead dates the charity-school's own founding narrative to 1694 (first catechizing of poor children, `pietas-hallensis-1727-selections`, p. 10, quote halle-francke-q002) and 1695 (the alms-box, `pietas-hallensis-1727-selections`, p. 13, quote halle-francke-q004, and the charity-school proper, p. 14, quote halle-francke-q005). 1698 is the year usually given in general scholarship for the opening of the purpose-built orphan-house building, which is a later, distinct milestone from the charity-school's 1694-95 origin narrated in the pages read this session; no passage dated 1698 was located and read this session to confirm that later date directly. Not resolved here.

2. **Exact day of the missionaries' departure and arrival.** The Royal Instruction itself is dated "Copenhagen, 17. Nov. 1705" (`fenger1863-tranquebar`, p. 309-311, quotes halle-francke-q027, halle-francke-q029, halle-francke-q030) -- this date is clear. But the missionaries' own report of their departure, quoted by Fenger, gives the day of departure as an OCR-garbled numeral ("Ontlie 2 9tli of November 1705" in the digitized text; almost certainly "On the 29th of November 1705," but the source used this session cannot be read with certainty on this point) and the day of arrival at Tranquebar similarly as "the dth July 1706" (almost certainly "9th," per the general chronology of the mission, but again not legible with certainty in the source used). See `fenger1863-tranquebar`, quotes halle-francke-q018 and halle-francke-q019. This is an artifact of the source's OCR quality (see `sources.csv` note on `fenger1863-tranquebar`, which is otherwise a well-OCR'd text), not a disagreement between two sources -- but since the pipeline provides no other field for OCR-illegibility, it is recorded here rather than silently guessed. Not resolved: the day-of-month for both departure and arrival is left as "not clearly legible in the source" in the chapter rather than asserted as 29 November / 9 July.

3. **Number of missionaries sent to Tranquebar in the mission's early decades.** A modern secondary summary surfaced by web search (not opened or read as a primary or primary-quoting source this session) states that "between 1706 and 1818, thirty-eight missionaries came to Tranquebar from Halle." This figure is NOT used anywhere in the dossier or chapter, because it was not verified in a source actually opened this session, per Rule 1-2. The only mission personnel numbers attested in the sources actually read this session are: two founding missionaries (1705-06), a third (Grundler) recorded before 1719, and one later arrival (Gericke, 1765) -- see dossier field S3 and R1-R4.

4. **"1698" vs. Francke's own narrative sequence not being cross-checked against the full *Pietas Hallensis*.** Only pp. 9-15, 61-62, 73-75, and 78-79 of *Pietas Hallensis* (of roughly 90-100 printed pages in the 1727 edition) were transcribed and read this session (manually, see `sources.csv` and the header of `text/pietas-hallensis-1727-selections.txt`, because the available OCR of this edition was unusable). It is possible that the 1698 date, or a fuller account of the orphan-house's founding as distinct from the charity-school's, appears on pages not read this session. This is flagged as an incompleteness, not a contradiction, and is why field F1/F2 in the dossier describes the 1694-95 charity-school narrative rather than asserting a single "the Foundation was founded in 1698" sentence.
