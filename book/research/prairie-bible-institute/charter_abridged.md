# Abridged charter — Prairie Bible Institute (`prairie-bible-institute`)

**Coverage note.** No constitution, incorporating statute, or founding-era catalog/prospectus for
Prairie Bible Institute was located and fetched this session (see `discrepancies.md`, item 1, and
`sources.csv`). What follows is therefore not a charter, statute, or prospectus in the sense the
other chapters of this book use the word. It is an abridgement of the fullest primary-adjacent
document this session could obtain: *Hoping For Nothing: The Miracle of the Prairie Bible
Institute* (Prairie Press, Three Hills, Alberta, ca. 1948-1950; document_id
`pbi-hoping-nothing-1950`), a promotional/historical pamphlet published by the Institute itself.
It reprints a 2 September 1945 magazine article about the school by Philip E. Howard Jr., editor
of the *Sunday School Times*, followed by L. E. Maxwell's own "Further Sketch of History," a
"Vital to Prospective Students" section on admission, curriculum and fees, and end matter
(a book advertisement, a subscription notice, a school calendar, and a current-attendance line).
It is used here, in place of a governing document, because it is the one document read this
session that states the school's purpose, doctrinal stance, admission terms, course of study, and
financial principle in the founders' own words. The Abridger applied the same KEEP/CUT priority
order used for a true charter (`01_book_design.md` section 6.2), reading "purpose/preamble" for
6.2(a), "doctrinal basis" for 6.2(b), "admission" for 6.2(c), "course of study" for 6.2(d), and
"financial principle, since explicitly doctrinal here (the no-debt, no-salary 'faith' principle)"
for 6.2(h).
