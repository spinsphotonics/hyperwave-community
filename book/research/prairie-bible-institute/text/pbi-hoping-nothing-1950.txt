On the prairies of western Canada, at 
‘Three Hills, Alberta, is a large and thriving 
Bible Institute of Which many Christians

In 1921 a young farmer, J.F. Kirk, heard 
‘a returned missionary speak in a little 
country schoolhouse near Three Hills. Kirk 
‘was moved to tears, and felt that God was 
calling him to study the Bible and to teach it
