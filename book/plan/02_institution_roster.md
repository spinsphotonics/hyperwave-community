# 02 — Institution Roster

Confidence labels used in this file: `[HIGH]` = well established, still cite a source in the dossier; `[VERIFY]` = plausible but the executor must confirm from a primary or reliable secondary source before use; `[UNVERIFIED]` = included because the commissioning brief named it, origin unknown.

Tiers: **A** = required chapter. **B** = chapter if budget allows; otherwise a paragraph in the part introduction. **C** = appendix note only.

Slugs are fixed. Do not invent new ones. New institutions proposed by an executor go in `discrepancies.md` for the Coordinator.

### Geneva and the "school of death"
- Academy opened 5 June 1559 with a public ceremony in St Pierre; Beza first rector. [HIGH]
- The *Leges Academiae Genevensis* were printed in Latin and French in 1559 by Robert Estienne. [HIGH]
- Structure: *schola privata* (seven classes of grammar and rhetoric) and *schola publica* (lectures in Hebrew, Greek, arts, theology). [HIGH]
- The Genevan Company of Pastors sent pastors into France in the late 1550s and early 1560s; Kingdon's count from the *Registres* is on the order of 88 missionaries between 1555 and 1562. [VERIFY exact figure in Kingdon 1956]
- The nickname "school of death" (French *école de la mort*) is attributed to the Academy because many graduates were martyred in France. **Origin of the phrase is not established.** The Scout ticket for `geneva-academy` includes a dedicated step to find the earliest printed use. Do not attribute the phrase to Calvin, Beza, or any named person without a primary citation. [UNVERIFIED]
- Calvin's *Ecclesiastical Ordinances* (1541) name four offices including "doctors" whose task is teaching; the Academy institutionalized that office. [HIGH]

### Judson
- Adoniram Judson born 9 Aug 1788, Malden, Mass.; Brown University (Rhode Island College) 1807; Andover 1808–1810. [HIGH]
- Society of the Brethren (secret student missionary society) began at Williams College 1808 after the Haystack meeting (Aug 1806), moved to Andover with Samuel Mills. [HIGH]
- ABCFM organized 29 June 1810 at Bradford, Mass., in response to the students' petition. [HIGH]
- Judson ordained 6 Feb 1812 at Salem; sailed 19 Feb 1812 with Ann Hasseltine Judson. [HIGH]
- Judson and Ann became Baptists and were baptized at Calcutta by William Ward, Sept 1812. [HIGH]
- The Baptist General Convention (Triennial Convention) organized May 1814 in Philadelphia to support Judson. [HIGH]

### Hudson Taylor and CIM
- Taylor born 21 May 1832, Barnsley; first sailed for China 1853 under the Chinese Evangelization Society; returned to England 1860; CIM founded 25 June 1865 (Brighton); Lammermuir sailed 26 May 1866. [HIGH]
- *China's Spiritual Need and Claims* first published 1865. [HIGH]
- The written *Principles and Practice* (P&P) was formalized in the 1880s; earlier "arrangements" existed from 1866. Exact first printed date [VERIFY]. Do not state a date without a citation.
